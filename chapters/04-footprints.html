7  E04 Footprints, Fisher information and identifiability

Question. Which source combinations can a sensor network actually distinguish, and does the singular spectrum of the stacked response predict this better than sensor count or coverage? Evidence label: exploratory (one scenario layout, one replicate). Current status: rerun at a_ref = 1 g s⁻¹ (A1, commit 04bbd28).

Headline. At the preregistered σ (0.253 µg m⁻³) the greedy D-optimal (“informative”) 4-sensor design reaches practical rank 4/4 under both winds; the coverage design 1 (westerly) or 2 (veering); the redundant design 2. Moving the source vector along the weakest mode changes the data by less than 1σ in 9 of 12 cases, all for non-informative designs. At the old 100 µg s⁻¹ scale the practical rank was 0/4 for every design (superseded).

Simulated passive tracer only; nothing here is evidence about ambient PM2.5. Every number below is generated by scripts/e04_footprints.py (one registered run) into tables/e04-identifiability.csv, tables/e04-identifiability.md and tables/e04-results.md; the figure is figures/e04-footprints.png. Settings are in tables/e04-meta.json.

7.1 Design

  • Smoke scale (32² grid, 12 h spin-up, 24 h window), perfect model, known source locations (four sources), two training wind regimes (steady westerly, veering). G is built from the truth’s own solver; no estimator sees the truth strengths except the counterexample below, where they define the “true vector”.
  • Three 4-sensor designs, chosen from G and the admissible sites only: informative (greedy Bayesian D-optimal, prior sd equal to the reference rate), redundant (the four admissible sites nearest the first informative site that respect the minimum separation) and coverage (farthest-point spreading). Designs are chosen under the first wind and evaluated under both.
  • Practical rank: the number of singular values s of G/σ with s·A_ref ≥ 1, i.e. the modes in which a reference-sized source change moves the data by at least one noise σ. This is our operationalisation of “rank at the preregistered noise scale”; it is not a preregistered threshold. Floating-point rank (relative tolerance 1e-8) is reported beside it.
  • Noise scales: the preregistered σ, and an extra non-preregistered “information scale” σ (0.1 × rms reference signal over admissible sites). It was added when, at the old 100 µg s⁻¹ scale, no mode was above noise; it is a rescaling, not a realistic instrument.
  • Counterexample: the true strength vector is moved along the weakest right singular vector of G/σ (nonnegativity kept, step at most half of ‖a‖). If the data change is below one σ, the two vectors fit equally well yet differ.
  • Ellipses are local 95% ellipses of the posterior covariance (Fisher plus a prior of sd A_ref), because the unregularised Fisher matrix is singular in several cases.

7.2 Results

At the preregistered noise sigma = 0.253 ug m^-3 the practical rank is [1, 2, 4] (values across the 6 wind/design cases) of K = 4 sources, although the floating-point rank (tol 1e-08) is [3, 4]. At the non-preregistered information scale sigma = 0.0258 ug m^-3 the practical rank is [2, 3, 4]. In 9 of 12 wind/design/noise cases a source vector moved by a median 0.5 of ||a|| along the weakest mode changes the data by less than one noise sigma (rms); the smallest and largest such data changes are 1.15e-10 and 14.1 sigma.

7.3 Audit outcome (gate-04)

Gate-04 partly cleared E04. The Fisher/CRB machinery is correct in the linear-Gaussian interior case and conservative under the NNLS constraint, but badly miscalibrated when the noise model is wrong, and it cannot be evaluated for designs whose Fisher matrix is singular. The hypothesis (spectrum beats count or coverage) remained unresolved: one replicate, at the time at a scale where nothing was above noise. It also noted that the 1e-8 rank rule is applied to singular values in one place and eigenvalues in another. What changed: A1 rescaled the problem, the Bayesian (prior-regularised) D-criterion is used so singular designs stay comparable, and the paired-seed comparison moved to E06/E07.

7.4 Limitations

One scenario layout, one replicate, smoke scale, perfect model, local linear (Fisher) analysis, an ad hoc practical-rank threshold and a rescaled noise level. This does not yet test the hypothesis (spectrum versus sensor count or coverage) across seeds; that comparison remains unresolved here.
