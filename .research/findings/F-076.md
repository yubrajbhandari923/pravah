# F-076 · EXP-036 / DYN-08 v2 (RUN-0101): surrogate-designed layouts beat random layouts at Belauri scale; static design indistinguishable; H8 supported under the D-026 counting rule, with post-hoc amendments (replaces F-074)

## Statement

Corrects F-074 after F-074/R1 (all numbers reproduce exactly). DYN-08 v2 (RUN-0101, Duke, 16 CPUs; 192 test episodes of which 184 valid (the same 8 unobservable-boundary episodes as v1 excluded); layouts hash 2bb1afbaecc2ce1a, hyperparameters 123c6d12f5e12eaf; edge gate 2/12) is a POST-HOC-CORRECTED RERUN (D-029) of v1 (F-069, which stopped at its controls), not an independent replication: only the episodes/noise are new (seeds 44000-44191 / 45000-45047, untouched by v1); the winds, the dyn_opt layouts and the 10 primary random layouts are identical to v1; the two-level bootstrap used as primary analysis was adopted after F-069 had shown on v1 data that it excluded 0 in all four cells. Order verified: design -> worst-layout check (ratios to the pool median 2.72/3.49/2.50/2.28 at k3/0.02, k5/0.02, k3/0.10, k5/0.10, all >= 1.5, before tuning) -> tune on validation -> freeze -> test -> model error. CONTROLS (post-hoc redesigned, all passed): (a') spread beats the worst-surrogate-risk layout in 4/4 cells (-41.5%, -62.7%, -26.7%, -37.3%; a gross-magnitude sensitivity check); (b') two groups of 10 fresh random layouts, two-level bootstrap at 0.0025: criterion not met in any cell (-4.8%, -8.3%, +1.1%, +2.2%; the k=3 signs persist leave-one-wind-out, so sign persistence protects little; the gate is one-sided in the code, B-A reported only). PRIMARY (dyn_opt vs the 10 primary random layouts, two-level bootstrap, 0.0025, sqrt(J_pop) of M2): criterion met in 4/4 cells: k3/0.02 -31.5% (-0.0024 [-0.00387,-0.00129]); k3/0.10 -27.4% (-0.00429 [-0.00642,-0.00243]); k5/0.02 -23.3% (-0.00142 [-0.00379,-0.00001]); k5/0.10 -31.5% (-0.00376 [-0.0065,-0.00185]). FRAGILITY: k5/0.02 is borderline and not robust (upper bound seed-dependent: -1.35e-4 to +1.9e-5 over 40 seeds; layout-level t interval [-0.0035,+0.00067] and wind-cluster CI [-0.0040,+0.00055] include 0; exploratory reanalysis against the 20 never-seen random layouts of the b' groups meets the criterion in 3/4 cells, with k5/0.02 -15.6% [-0.00217,+0.00010] not met); H8a's >=3/4 holds without it. SINGLE-LAYOUT contrasts (about that layout; episode bootstrap): vs spread met 3/4 (-32.0%, -30.0%, -26.5%; k5/0.02 -12.2% n.s.); vs highpop met 2/4 (k3 -29.6%, -24.0%; k5 +4.6%, -3.4% n.s.); vs src_opt 1/4 (k3/0.02 -26.9%); vs static_opt 0/4 (-0.8%, -5.9%, +0.1%, -0.4%): the evidence supports that surrogate-designed layouts beat random layouts, not a benefit specific to dynamic design over a static-model design. CONTRARY to the registered prediction, the margin over highpop is LARGER than in the toy at the same budgets (toy F-050: highpop better or tied at k3 and k5), so that sub-prediction failed and does not count toward H8a. H8b: M2 < M1 in all 8 layout x cell rows (-24% to -52%, criterion met 8/8). H8d (92 episodes per row; ratios of means with 95% episode-bootstrap CIs, unadjusted): M2 error grows monotonically with truth wind rotation in 8/8 rows (e.g. k3/0.02 spread x1.00, 3.62 [3.07,4.31], 7.15 [5.94,8.73], 12.92 [10.59,15.9] at 0/5/10/20 degrees); M2's advantage over M1 does not merely erode but REVERSES (M2/M1 > 1 with the CI excluding 1) in 4/8 rows at 10 degrees and 6/8 at 20 degrees, unlike the toy; dyn_opt's M2 error is below spread's in every perturbed row (episode CI excludes 0 in 19/20, the registered sub-claim cannot fail); mesh250 truth multiplies M2 error by x1.26-2.54 (spread and dyn_opt rows; x1.04-2.60 over all layouts) and speed x1.2 by x1.25-3.25 (x1.11-3.25 over all layouts). VERDICT: H8 is SUPPORTED under the D-026 counting rule (H8a 3/4 on the spread threshold exactly and 4/4 vs random, H8b yes, H8d's registered direction holds; H8c exploratory), with controls and analysis amended post hoc by D-029 after v1 was seen; this is not a clean confirmatory test. EXPLORATORY H8c: CAUSAL < CT by 77-94% with bin 0 filled from bin 1 in both arms and flagged acausal; CT's error (0.12-0.18) is mostly a property of the CT construction (bin 1 has a single observation time for 5 parameters; F-065).

## Limitations

Not independent of v1 (same winds/layouts); mesh: F-075 reports the 500 m / dt 100 mesh INADEQUATE at the 5% shape margin in 2 of 4 EXP-027b cells (rankings preserved, rho 0.94-0.96; a different objective than J_pop), and the mesh250 error inflation (x1.26-2.54) is about as large as the design effects (24-32%); M2 tuning grid 96 vs 12 for M1 and the tuned M2 hyperparameters changed between v1 and v2 (instability of tuning on 48 episodes); model-error CIs are unadjusted and resample episodes not winds; ERA5 uniform wind, 2 months of 2023; WorldPop modelled; synthetic emissions; NOT empirical validation; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
