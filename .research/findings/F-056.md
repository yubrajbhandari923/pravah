# F-056 · DYN-07 v2 model-error sensitivity, corrected interpretation after review F-049/R1 (supersedes F-049; all numbers reproduce, M2 is below M1 in all 40 layout x noise x condition cells but by only 0.1-2% at 9 sensors). Rotation 5/10/20 deg multiplies the 4-sensor M1 error by x1.84/2.59/3.74 and the 9-sensor M1 error by x2.72/4.51/7.33; M2's advantage over M1 at 4 sensors erodes -20% nominal to -3% at 20 deg; M2 beats the static M0 in 91-96% (4 sensors) and 61-99% (9 sensors) of episodes. CONFOUND DISCLOSED: the unmodelled source (6000,4500) m lies 354 m from the 9-sensor layout's centre sensor (cell (12,9), inside the source's 600 m sigma) and far from every 4-sensor sensor, which alone explains the extra-source cost x4.58 vs x1.37 and the 61% minimum; the statement 'extra sensors tighten the fit to a wrong model' is therefore NOT established by the source perturbation. In absolute error the 9-sensor M1 is still BETTER than the 4-sensor M1 at 5 deg (0.00558 vs 0.00688), 10 deg (0.00923 vs 0.00969) and both speed errors, and WORSE only at 20 deg (0.01502 vs 0.01398), diffusion x2 (0.00696 vs 0.00672) and the extra source; the larger ratios partly reflect the lower nominal baseline (0.00205 vs 0.00373). What is supported: wind/diffusion error costs a factor 1.7-9 depending on size and layout, and beyond ~15-20 degrees of wind error the sensor-rich layout no longer has an absolute advantage. Caveats: global rotations/scalings, one geometry/fixture, hyperparameters not re-tuned, preliminary.

## Statement

DYN-07 v2 model-error sensitivity, corrected interpretation after review F-049/R1 (supersedes F-049; all numbers reproduce, M2 is below M1 in all 40 layout x noise x condition cells but by only 0.1-2% at 9 sensors). Rotation 5/10/20 deg multiplies the 4-sensor M1 error by x1.84/2.59/3.74 and the 9-sensor M1 error by x2.72/4.51/7.33; M2's advantage over M1 at 4 sensors erodes -20% nominal to -3% at 20 deg; M2 beats the static M0 in 91-96% (4 sensors) and 61-99% (9 sensors) of episodes. CONFOUND DISCLOSED: the unmodelled source (6000,4500) m lies 354 m from the 9-sensor layout's centre sensor (cell (12,9), inside the source's 600 m sigma) and far from every 4-sensor sensor, which alone explains the extra-source cost x4.58 vs x1.37 and the 61% minimum; the statement 'extra sensors tighten the fit to a wrong model' is therefore NOT established by the source perturbation. In absolute error the 9-sensor M1 is still BETTER than the 4-sensor M1 at 5 deg (0.00558 vs 0.00688), 10 deg (0.00923 vs 0.00969) and both speed errors, and WORSE only at 20 deg (0.01502 vs 0.01398), diffusion x2 (0.00696 vs 0.00672) and the extra source; the larger ratios partly reflect the lower nominal baseline (0.00205 vs 0.00373). What is supported: wind/diffusion error costs a factor 1.7-9 depending on size and layout, and beyond ~15-20 degrees of wind error the sensor-rich layout no longer has an absolute advantage. Caveats: global rotations/scalings, one geometry/fixture, hyperparameters not re-tuned, preliminary.
