# F-068 · EXP-026 (RUN-0098): greedy gaps, near ties and design-sample optimism swamp differences among weighted objectives

## Statement

Greedy design objectives are 28-79% above the best found on average (design_gap.csv; greedy is suboptimal in essentially every cell), yet the test-RMSE difference greedy minus best-found is mixed in sign (150 positive vs 90 negative cells; 26 of 240 resolved): the best-found layout is not a reliable test improvement. The design objective is strongly optimistic: at budgets 5 and 8 the other-half design objective exceeds the own-half by a median of 175-531%. Population and area objectives are near ties in 54.5% of pair rows but only 36 of 2535 near-tied rows are non-identical layouts. Median margin of the exact optimum over the second best is 4.3% (8.6% of cases under 1%). Median per-draw IQR width of the diff at budgets 2-4 is 0.000323 against a median |mean diff| of 0.000563. Reading: at this scale (6 design cases per draw, 54 sites) differences among weighted design objectives are small compared with greedy path dependence, design-sample optimism and fixture-draw variability; this is consistent with Q-013 and F-062 (design objective not estimable with few cases).

## Limitations

Exact search only to budget 4; best-found at 5 and 8 not proved optimal (44% of starts hit the best value exactly once); design_split covers only budgets 5 and 8, draw 0; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
