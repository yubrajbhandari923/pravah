# F-028 · Toy wind-error response: error grows ~linearly with direction error (B: 0.010 -> 0.040 at 30 deg, known-wind 0.010), direction more damaging than speed per unit vector error, wind error dominates sensor count, chi2/dof detects it but identifiability flags do not, ensemble strategy helps only inside/near its prior

## Statement

Synthetic Phase-1 domain, 3 local sources + inflow basis, 32 fresh test cases, 17 truth-wind conditions (direction +-5/10/15/30 deg, speed +-15/30%, joint +-15 deg/+-15%), shared noise, layouts greedy/spread/random at budgets 3/5/8, alpha frozen from validation on strategy B. Total field RMSE (ug/m3, mean over layouts and budgets): known wind A ~0.0104 at every direction error; fixed nominal wind B 0.0104 / 0.0168 / 0.0242 / 0.0303 / 0.0371 at 0 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 30 deg (negative side similar); ensemble C 0.0114 / 0.0178 / 0.0104 / 0.0125 / 0.0194 (at +5 and -5 C is not better than B; at 10 deg the truth is a prior member). Paired bootstrap intervals over test cases: C-B excludes 0 and is negative at |10|,|15|,|30| deg (-0.010 to -0.020), is slightly positive at 0 (+0.001) and -5 deg. Per unit vector error direction is more damaging than speed (B 0.0242 at vec err 0.17 from 10 deg vs 0.0138 at 0.15 from -15% speed); underestimating speed (-30%: 0.0202) hurts more than overestimating (+30%: 0.0132). Sensor count: A improves with budget (0.0144 -> 0.0074 at 0 error) but B at 15 deg stays 0.024-0.029 for all budgets, so wind error outweighs the sensor-count effect. chi2/dof for B: 1.0 at 0, 1.6 at 5 deg (53% rejects), 3.5 at 10 deg, 13.1 at 30 deg (91% rejects); identifiability flag fraction constant at 0.515 for B at every condition. External fraction error B 0.06 -> 0.23 at 30 deg (A 0.05-0.06). 90% interval coverage at held-out sensors: A ~0.70 even with known wind (regularisation bias), B falls to 0.12-0.18 at |15-30| deg, C holds ~0.74 at 10 deg but 0.26-0.35 at 30 deg outside its +-20 deg prior (overconfident).

## Limitations

Not yet independently reviewed; toy Phase-1 domain, fixed source positions, one wind (single segment), truth built by linear superposition of cached responses, alpha selected on B's validation RMSE, C's advantage depends on the true wind being near a member of a 3x3 prior grid (grid spacing 10 deg), chi2 dof approximation under regularisation (5% rejects at zero error), coverage low even for A because of regularisation, bootstrap intervals are over 32 test cases not confidence intervals for new scenarios, mesh/dt fixed at Phase-1 values (not justified by EXP-015 stage 2)
