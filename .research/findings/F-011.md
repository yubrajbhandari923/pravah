# F-011 · Belauri forward simulations: wind direction and numerical diffusion dominate; terrain layers are context only (real geography, synthetic emissions and meteorology)

## Statement

On the real Belauri 500 m grid the external patch reaches the municipality only under the WSW wind (centroid arrival 2627 s after onset; external concentration fraction 0.22) and not under north/east winds (<3e-4); a smaller outer domain matters only when the wind reverses (-10.5% mean); K_eff/K is 2.5-4.9 on the 250/500 m meshes, so numerical spreading exceeds the nominal diffusivity; replacing DEM/hillshade/slope by constants changes no concentration.

## Limitations

Synthetic emissions and winds; flat terrain in the domain, terrain dynamics unresolved; one emission set
