# F-049 · DYN-07 v2 model-error sensitivity (RUN-0088; protocol D-023: converged DYN-02 v3 hyperparameters frozen, never re-tuned per condition; the same 96 synthetic episodes, seeds 8500-8595, in 10 paired conditions; truth perturbed, inverse nominal; 10% noise unless stated; degradation-ratio bug of F-040 fixed). All registered predictions hold. (P1) Error grows monotonically with wind rotation: M1 x1.84 / x2.59 / x3.74 and M2 x1.96 / x2.95 / x4.57 of nominal at 5 / 10 / 20 degrees with 4 sensors; with 9 sensors M1 x2.72 / x4.51 / x7.33 and M2 x2.77 / x4.58 / x7.46. (P2) M2's advantage over the fair M1 at 4 sensors erodes with misspecification: -20% nominal, -15% / -9% / -3% at 5 / 10 / 20 degrees, -7% combined; M2's ratio degradation is slightly larger than M1's (x4.57 vs x3.74 at 20 degrees) but in absolute terms M2 error is lower than M1's in every one of the 20 layout x condition cells; TV does not buy robustness. (P3) M2 beats the static M0 in a majority of episodes in every condition: 91-96% at 4 sensors and 61-99% at 9 sensors (lowest: unmodelled source at 30% of the local emission, 61%). Other effects: speed error x0.8 / x1.2 costs x2.07 / x1.68 (4 sensors, M1) and x2.17 / x2.06 (9 sensors); diffusion x2 costs x1.80 (M1, 4 sensors) / x3.40 (9 sensors); an unmodelled constant source of 10% / 30% of the mean local emission costs x1.04 / x1.37 (4 sensors) and x2.00 / x4.58 (9 sensors). The 9-sensor layout is therefore MORE sensitive to model error than the 4-sensor layout (x7.3 vs x3.7 at 20 degrees; at 20 degrees the 9-sensor M1 error 0.0150 exceeds the 4-sensor 0.0140 and is only 26% below a static model), consistent with earlier work that wind error dominates sensor count: extra sensors tighten the fit to a wrong model. Same values at 2% noise (e.g. 20-degree M2 x4.66 / x7.78). Caveats: global rotations/scalings of every wind segment; one source geometry and population fixture; hyperparameters deliberately not re-tuned (model-error-aware tuning would likely help); a sensitivity study without success criteria; preliminary, not independently reviewed. Supersedes F-040.

## Statement

DYN-07 v2 model-error sensitivity (RUN-0088; protocol D-023: converged DYN-02 v3 hyperparameters frozen, never re-tuned per condition; the same 96 synthetic episodes, seeds 8500-8595, in 10 paired conditions; truth perturbed, inverse nominal; 10% noise unless stated; degradation-ratio bug of F-040 fixed). All registered predictions hold. (P1) Error grows monotonically with wind rotation: M1 x1.84 / x2.59 / x3.74 and M2 x1.96 / x2.95 / x4.57 of nominal at 5 / 10 / 20 degrees with 4 sensors; with 9 sensors M1 x2.72 / x4.51 / x7.33 and M2 x2.77 / x4.58 / x7.46. (P3) M2 beats the static M0 in a majority of episodes in every condition: 91-96% at 4 sensors and 61-99% at 9 sensors (lowest: unmodelled source at 30% of the local emission, 61%). Other effects: speed error x0.8 / x1.2 costs x2.07 / x1.68 (4 sensors, M1) and x2.17 / x2.06 (9 sensors); diffusion x2 costs x1.80 (M1, 4 sensors) / x3.40 (9 sensors); an unmodelled constant source of 10% / 30% of the mean local emission costs x1.04 / x1.37 (4 sensors) and x2.00 / x4.58 (9 sensors). The 9-sensor layout is therefore MORE sensitive to model error than the 4-sensor layout (x7.3 vs x3.7 at 20 degrees; at 20 degrees the 9-sensor M1 error 0.0150 exceeds the 4-sensor 0.0140 and is only 26% below a static model), consistent with earlier work that wind error dominates sensor count: extra sensors tighten the fit to a wrong model. Same values at 2% noise (e.g. 20-degree M2 x4.66 / x7.78). Caveats: global rotations/scalings of every wind segment; one source geometry and population fixture; hyperparameters deliberately not re-tuned (model-error-aware tuning would likely help); a sensitivity study without success criteria; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
