# F-010 · External vs local decomposition from total observations: recoverable when well conditioned, flagged when not; boundary input cannot mimic a localised plume (synthetic)

## Statement

Idealised linear setting, alpha frozen on validation: 8 interior sensors recover local/external amplitudes to 1.1%/0.6% (2% noise) and the concentration external fraction to 0.0016; the confounded pair (column cosine 0.991) and the no-arrival case (external/local ratio 4e-6) are flagged unidentifiable, not recovered; an equal-budget upwind reference site lowered external-term error but raised total/local error versus 8 interior sensors; uniform or sector boundary bases reduce the omitted-E1 field error only from 0.257 to 0.250 of peak; outer-domain size changes the study field by 0.9-3.3% of peak.

## Limitations

Synthetic, matched model; 4+6 cases, 3 noise draws; one geometry (E1 1 km from domain-A boundary); wind mismatch not flagged by identifiability diagnostics; does not identify real emitters
