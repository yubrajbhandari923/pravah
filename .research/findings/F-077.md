# F-077 · EXP-027b post-hoc sensitivity (RUN-0102): the INADEQUATE call is method-dependent; 500 m shape error is about 4-12% at noise 0.05 (exploratory)

## Statement

POST-HOC, EXPLORATORY sensitivity analysis of the registered EXP-027b evaluation (RUN-0102, scripts/exp027b_sensitivity.py, 72 cases from RUN-0100's paired_work; 16 CPUs, ~0.9 core-h; the registered verdict of F-075 is unchanged and not rescued). Cells shown as noise/budget; E = shape error, margin 0.05. (1) Bootstrap noise is NOT the issue: the registered percentile interval at B=400 gives INADEQUATE in 20/20 seeds for 0.05/3 (E_lo95 0.054-0.062) and 0.05/5 (0.052-0.060), and at B=10000 (0.0575 and 0.0540); 0.15/3 and 0.15/5 are INCONCLUSIVE in 0/20 seeds. (2) The INTERVAL TYPE is the issue: basic (reflected) intervals at B=10000 give INCONCLUSIVE for both inadequate cells (lower bounds -0.0125 and -0.0161) and BCa gives INCONCLUSIVE (0.0381 and 0.0408); under basic/BCa the 0.15/3 cell becomes INADEQUATE (0.0505, 0.0574) and 0.15/5 ADEQUATE; the 2 km control stays INADEQUATE in 4/4 cells under percentile and BCa (basic: 3/4, 0.15/5 lower bound 0.0472), i.e. the gate is robust. (3) Not driven by one draw or a few cases: per-draw E (0.05/3 main: 0.260, 0.080, 0.127; 0.05/5: 0.158, 0.057, 0.170), leave-one-draw-out and drop-top-5-influential-cases (0.05/3 E 0.0746 [0.0527,0.1289]; 0.05/5 E 0.1005 [0.0556,0.2051]) keep both cells INADEQUATE under the percentile rule; only draw 2 alone gives INCONCLUSIVE at 0.05/5. (4) Menu without layouts derived only from the 2 km control mesh: 0.05/3 E 0.0807 [0.0476,0.1631] INCONCLUSIVE; 0.05/5 E 0.0697 [0.0501,0.2208] INADEQUATE (lower bound 0.0001 above the margin). (5) Out-of-sample menu (cross-fit: menu from the other two draws, scored on the held-out draw): mean E over held-out draws 0.0997 (0.05/3), 0.1161 (0.05/5), 0.0724 (0.15/3), 0.0444 (0.15/5); per-held-out-draw intervals INADEQUATE for draw 1 only (0.05/3 [0.0593,0.4892]; 0.05/5 [0.0680,0.2946]), otherwise INCONCLUSIVE; so the shape error is not an artefact of in-sample menu selection. (6) Noise-debiased E (split-half cross-fit, 200 splits; point estimate, intervals truncate at 0): 0.0442 (0.05/3), 0.0382 (0.05/5), 0.0405 (0.15/3), 0.0147 (0.15/5), i.e. at or below the 0.05 margin. Reading: at noise 0.05 the 500 m shape error is probably between about 4% (noise-debiased) and 10-12% (out-of-sample, undebiased); whether it exceeds 5% depends on the estimator and interval; the registered INADEQUATE label is reproducible under the registered method but not robust to the choice of interval; evidence of material inadequacy is stronger for the 2 km control than for 500 m. 500 m / dt 100 stays PROVISIONAL (F-075, F-076).

## Limitations

Post hoc; chosen after F-075/R1 criticism; many analyses (multiplicity not controlled); E is a scale-free shape statistic; cross-fit menus are smaller (21-33 layouts); debiased interval lower bounds truncate at 0; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
