# F-045 · DYN-05 / P0 corrected statement (supersedes F-041 after review F-041/R1). Design-stage objective = closed-form ridge risk (verified against Monte Carlo and re-derived by the reviewer), 16 independent ensembles per N, regret measured on an independent 20000-episode reference (D-018). DISCLOSURES: D-018 was written after the RUN-0077 summary was printed and flipped one borderline cell (noise 0.10, k=2: criterion 'p90 regret <= 5%' met from N=48 under RUN-0077 vs from N=24 under RUN-0078; a p90 over 16 replicates is an interpolated order statistic with no stated uncertainty); an earlier real-estimator run (RUN-0079) had a layout-key collision (k=2 layouts overwritten) and was re-run as RUN-0080 on the same seeds 8400-8495. SURROGATE (design-objective) RESULT: p90 relative regret <= 5% is reached from N=24 (noise 0.10, k=2: 4.8%), from N=48 (noise 0.02, k=3; all 16 ensembles identical), and is not reached up to N=192 for noise 0.02, k=2 (10.5%) or noise 0.10, k=3 (23-46%); N<=12 at noise 0.02, k=3 gives regret up to x29 when an ensemble misses a wind (wind sampled at random, unstratified). REAL-ESTIMATOR CHECK (nonnegative ridge, 96 fresh episodes, ratio of mean J_pop relative to the 20000-episode reference layout, mean over the 16 ensembles, 95% episode bootstrap, ensemble layouts treated as fixed): noise 0.10, k=2: +34% [15,58] at N=6, +28% [8,54] at N=24, +16% [2,34] at N=192; noise 0.02, k=2: +29% [10,51] at N=6, +14% [3,26] at N=192; noise 0.02, k=3: +9% [3,17] at N=6, +6% [2,11] at N=24, exactly 0 from N=48; noise 0.10, k=3: -10% [-27,+9] at N=6 and -13% [-22,-3] at N=192 (the ensemble layouts are BETTER than the reference layout). So surrogate sufficiency does not carry over to the real estimator at k=2 (even the cell the surrogate calls sufficient from N=24 costs +28% in the real estimator) and at noise 0.10, k=3 the unconstrained-ridge surrogate cannot rank near-optimal layouts (reviewer: the real estimator rates S*_B 22% better than S*_A while the surrogate has them within 0.5%). Conclusion supported: a 6-episode design ensemble is insufficient in every cell; the number of episodes needed depends on budget, noise and wind coverage and, for the actual constrained estimator, is not established by this proxy. Not claimed: that N=6 in EXP-013/014/020/026 failed in the same way (their wind sampling was not checked). Caveats: one fixture, k=2,3 only, wind known, preliminary, review pending on this corrected statement.

## Statement

DYN-05 / P0 corrected statement (supersedes F-041 after review F-041/R1). Design-stage objective = closed-form ridge risk (verified against Monte Carlo and re-derived by the reviewer), 16 independent ensembles per N, regret measured on an independent 20000-episode reference (D-018). DISCLOSURES: D-018 was written after the RUN-0077 summary was printed and flipped one borderline cell (noise 0.10, k=2: criterion 'p90 regret <= 5%' met from N=48 under RUN-0077 vs from N=24 under RUN-0078; a p90 over 16 replicates is an interpolated order statistic with no stated uncertainty); an earlier real-estimator run (RUN-0079) had a layout-key collision (k=2 layouts overwritten) and was re-run as RUN-0080 on the same seeds 8400-8495. SURROGATE (design-objective) RESULT: p90 relative regret <= 5% is reached from N=24 (noise 0.10, k=2: 4.8%), from N=48 (noise 0.02, k=3; all 16 ensembles identical), and is not reached up to N=192 for noise 0.02, k=2 (10.5%) or noise 0.10, k=3 (23-46%); N<=12 at noise 0.02, k=3 gives regret up to x29 when an ensemble misses a wind (wind sampled at random, unstratified). REAL-ESTIMATOR CHECK (nonnegative ridge, 96 fresh episodes, ratio of mean J_pop relative to the 20000-episode reference layout, mean over the 16 ensembles, 95% episode bootstrap, ensemble layouts treated as fixed): noise 0.10, k=2: +34% [15,58] at N=6, +28% [8,54] at N=24, +16% [2,34] at N=192; noise 0.02, k=2: +29% [10,51] at N=6, +14% [3,26] at N=192; noise 0.02, k=3: +9% [3,17] at N=6, +6% [2,11] at N=24, exactly 0 from N=48; noise 0.10, k=3: -10% [-27,+9] at N=6 and -13% [-22,-3] at N=192 (the ensemble layouts are BETTER than the reference layout). So surrogate sufficiency does not carry over to the real estimator at k=2 (even the cell the surrogate calls sufficient from N=24 costs +28% in the real estimator) and at noise 0.10, k=3 the unconstrained-ridge surrogate cannot rank near-optimal layouts (reviewer: the real estimator rates S*_B 22% better than S*_A while the surrogate has them within 0.5%). Conclusion supported: a 6-episode design ensemble is insufficient in every cell; the number of episodes needed depends on budget, noise and wind coverage and, for the actual constrained estimator, is not established by this proxy. Not claimed: that N=6 in EXP-013/014/020/026 failed in the same way (their wind sampling was not checked). Caveats: one fixture, k=2,3 only, wind known, preliminary, review pending on this corrected statement.
