# F-013 · Geographic sensor design on Belauri: greedy layouts help most at 1-3 sensors; attribution-objective layouts are not worse on total error at small budgets; external reference sites are not a clear win at equal budget (synthetic, matched)

## Statement

Interior-only, 5% noise, alpha=0.01 frozen on validation, 6 test scenarios: total RMSE (ug/m3) at 1/2/3/5/8 sensors greedy_total 0.0223/0.0093/0.00525/0.00353/0.00272, greedy_attribution 0.0105/0.0065/0.00542/0.0037/0.00327, spread 0.0148/0.0117/0.0058/0.00512/0.00323, random-mean 0.025/0.0135/0.0103/0.00666/0.00328; greedy_total beat spread in 3,2,4,5,5 of 6 cases (mean difference +0.0075 at one sensor); greedy_attribution lowered attribution error at 1-2 sensors (0.132 vs 0.285; 0.087 vs 0.107); differences beyond 5 sensors are within noise; greedy_total never chose a reference site, greedy_attribution 1 (budget 5) and 2 (budget 8); spread layouts with reference sites had higher total error than interior-only spread.

## Limitations

6 test scenarios x 3 noise draws; sd is a standard deviation; synthetic matched model on a numerically diffusive mesh (K_eff/K 2-15); provisional simulation design, not field-validated
