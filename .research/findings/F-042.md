# F-042 · DYN-06 threshold study (RUN-0082, 100 noise replicates per cell, 6 winds, d_after_rescale scaled to 2/4/8/16; synthetic, wind known): the registered predictions P1-P4 CANNOT be evaluated because the attribution metric is flawed: the worker's 'no change detected' rule (|group contribution| < one single-observation sigma, applied to a sensor-time MEAN contribution) is uncalibrated, so the registered 'confusion' is dominated by non-detection (detection is 1% at 9 sensors even at d=16, impossible for a sound detector) and mis-states layout comparisons (the reference-site layout appears to detect 2x more only because of how the mean is formed). Verdicts: P1-P4 inconclusive (metric invalid), not contradicted and not supported. Robust, metric-independent parts: (a) the analytic distinguishability d between local-increase and boundary-influx look-alikes is in the hundreds of noise sd at full amplitude for all winds tested (so separation fails only for changes smaller than ~1/50 of a typical event); (b) among replicates where a group WAS named, the wrong-group rate is low at 4 sensors for S1 local-increase vs influx (3%), S2 shutdown vs cessation (0-3%) and S4 opposing changes (0-4%), but high for S5 'influx ends while the plume persists' (16-23% at 4 sensors, 0% at 9 sensors) and for the local-vs-local S6 with the boundary-reference layout (34-35% at d<=8, 14% at d=16); (c) controls: the perfectly-confounded fixture gives d=3.6e-14 and attribution ~chance only once the signal is large (confusion 0.5 at d=16; 1.0 at small d because nothing is detected), and the no-variation control reports 'no change' in 100% of replicates. Needed before any claim: replace the detection rule by a threshold calibrated on the null (no-change) distribution of each operator, then repeat the threshold study with new noise seeds. Not independently reviewed.

## Statement

DYN-06 threshold study (RUN-0082, 100 noise replicates per cell, 6 winds, d_after_rescale scaled to 2/4/8/16; synthetic, wind known): the registered predictions P1-P4 CANNOT be evaluated because the attribution metric is flawed: the worker's 'no change detected' rule (|group contribution| < one single-observation sigma, applied to a sensor-time MEAN contribution) is uncalibrated, so the registered 'confusion' is dominated by non-detection (detection is 1% at 9 sensors even at d=16, impossible for a sound detector) and mis-states layout comparisons (the reference-site layout appears to detect 2x more only because of how the mean is formed). Verdicts: P1-P4 inconclusive (metric invalid), not contradicted and not supported. Robust, metric-independent parts: (a) the analytic distinguishability d between local-increase and boundary-influx look-alikes is in the hundreds of noise sd at full amplitude for all winds tested (so separation fails only for changes smaller than ~1/50 of a typical event); (b) among replicates where a group WAS named, the wrong-group rate is low at 4 sensors for S1 local-increase vs influx (3%), S2 shutdown vs cessation (0-3%) and S4 opposing changes (0-4%), but high for S5 'influx ends while the plume persists' (16-23% at 4 sensors, 0% at 9 sensors) and for the local-vs-local S6 with the boundary-reference layout (34-35% at d<=8, 14% at d=16); (c) controls: the perfectly-confounded fixture gives d=3.6e-14 and attribution ~chance only once the signal is large (confusion 0.5 at d=16; 1.0 at small d because nothing is detected), and the no-variation control reports 'no change' in 100% of replicates. Needed before any claim: replace the detection rule by a threshold calibrated on the null (no-change) distribution of each operator, then repeat the threshold study with new noise seeds. Not independently reviewed.
