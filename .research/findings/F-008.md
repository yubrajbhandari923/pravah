# F-008 · Greedy layouts optimised for the matched model are not robust to model error at small budgets

## Statement

In results/mismatch-final the spread layout has lower mean RMSE than greedy at b=2 for wind error (0.025 vs 0.075) and at b=2-3 for the extra source (0.032 vs 0.090, 0.031 vs 0.046); at b>=5 greedy is better or equal for wind and better at b=5-8 only for the extra source. Matched-model design optimisation does not guarantee robustness.

## Limitations

6 test scenarios x 3 reps; one magnitude per mismatch kind; sd large
