# F-066 · EXP-026 (RUN-0098): population-weighted layouts do not reduce population-weighted RMSE at Belauri scale (H1 not supported, H2 area part supported, H3-H6 unresolved)

## Statement

EXP-026 / RUN-0098 (Duke, 8 CPUs, 12 held-out synthetic scenarios, fixture draws 6501-6506, noise fractions 0.05 and 0.15 both reported (the registration names no primary), 500 m / dt 100 provisional (F-057), amendments D-027 and D-028). Registered criterion used: case-bootstrap CI (10000 resamples) excludes 0 AND same direction in >=75% of the 12 cases; diff = objective layout minus area layout (positive = worse). Uniform-fixture control reproduced the area layout exactly (met); frozen-config guard passed; evaluators match design.score_layout/greedy_trace to <=1e-12. H1 (population weighting lowers pop-weighted test RMSE on concentrated fixtures): NOT SUPPORTED, partly contradicted. Of 200 cells (4 concentrated fixtures x 5 budgets x 2 noise x 5 objectives) none meets the criterion beneficially and 5 meet it harmfully (e.g. mixed_rural_urban, noise 0.15, budget 3: +0.00578, CI [0.00183,0.00996], 75% agree, +50% of the area-layout RMSE 0.01169; separated_settlements noise 0.05 budget 4 mixed_0.25 +0.000901 [0.000105,0.001787], 83%). Pooled population-objective change in pop-RMSE: noise 0.05 +7.2/+17.5/+7.6/-3.0/-2.3% at budgets 2/3/4/5/8; noise 0.15 -3.0/+13.2/+9.6/0.0/+1.1%. The expected largest benefit at small budgets is contradicted. H2 (area RMSE rises): supported in direction, resolved (harmful) in 9/200 cells; area RMSE +14/+35/+21/+21/+34% (noise 0.05); the rise is NOT concentrated in the lowest-population quartile (low-quartile rise exceeds area rise in 13 of 35 non-identical cells; low-quartile part not supported). Pop and area RMSE mostly move together (r=0.79): of 35 non-identical cells 19 both worse, 13 true trade-off, 3 both better. H3 (attribution not degraded beyond between-case variability): not resolved, harm tendency (27 of 40 population-objective cells worse; 20 of 200 resolved harmful, 4 beneficial). H4 (benefit shrinks on mismatched test field): not resolved, premise not met (matched benefit already absent; matched ratio <1 in only 3 of 40 cells). H5 (direction transfers to WorldPop): not resolved (see F-067). H6 (effect exceeds inter-draw spread at budgets 2-4): not supported as a benefit; the 5 resolved cells are harmful and exceed the IQR of per-draw diffs (the IQR operationalisation is the analyst's; the registration does not define it, and it is trivially satisfied when most draws give identical layouts).

## Limitations

Synthetic emissions/winds/noise; WorldPop modelled 2025 estimate; 500 m/dt100 only provisionally admissible (numerical diffusion K_eff/K 2.4-9.4); 12 test scenarios sharing one ensemble; exact search only to budget 4 (best-found at 5 and 8); D-028 deviation: the inherited Phase 2 regression check was skipped (validation-ensemble noise scale 0.012548 vs the design scale 0.015370 of results/geodesign/layouts.csv), decided after seeing the failure but before test data; run's own interval resamples draws and cases (differs from the registered case-only bootstrap; analyst recomputed case-only intervals); registered baselines random n=20 and spread appear only as unlabeled layouts in test_case_means.csv; provenance.json lacks git revision (copied source tree); the run record lists EXP-016; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
