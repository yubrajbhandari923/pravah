# F-069 · EXP-036 / DYN-08 (RUN-0099): STOPPED at registered stop rules 4.5(a) and (b); no H8 verdict; descriptive results only

## Statement

EXP-036 / DYN-08 (RUN-0099, Duke, 16 CPUs, 192 test episodes of which 184 valid; layouts hash a54267ac1fcf370b, hyperparameters 7b179f28f24f32e1, edge gate 1/12 (M2 alpha 0.01 at k5/0.02); protocol D-026): STOPPED at registered stop rules 4.5(a) and 4.5(b); no H8 verdict is issued and nothing is rescued; arms H8a, H8b, H8d are not resolved (stopped), H8c is exploratory. (a) lowpop did not lose to spread in 3 of 4 cells (k3/0.02: -16%, episode CI [-0.0029,-0.0002], wind-cluster CI includes 0; other cells -0.3%, +1.8%, -5%, n.s.). Post-hoc review diagnosis (RUN-0099 review): no pipeline bug; the design surrogate had already ranked lowpop near spread before testing (ranks 7/16 vs 8/16 at k3/0.02) and site-cell population is a weak proxy of layout quality here (9.4% of people live in the 54 candidate cells; layout-level Spearman between site population and error -0.16 to -0.65), so the control was not a 'bad' layout in this setting. (b) The null control (fixed random layouts 0-4 vs 5-9, episode-only bootstrap) met the criterion at k=5 (-20% and -17.5%), driven by two poor layouts (#7, #8); 33-48% of all 126 distinct 5/5 splits of the 10 layouts differ by >=10% at three of four cells, so as constructed the control fails by design and the registered criterion cannot separate individual-layout effects from design effects. DESCRIPTIVE (not confirmatory): dyn_opt had lower M2 sqrt(J_pop) than all 10 random layouts in all 4 cells (-23% to -32%; a two-level layout x episode bootstrap at 0.0025 excludes 0 in all four); vs the single spread layout -23%, -26%, -10% (n.s.), -27%; 9 of 20 primary contrasts meet the effect criterion but vs highpop only k3/0.10, vs src_opt only k3/0.02 and vs static_opt none (dyn_opt numerically worse than static_opt at k3/0.02, +9.3% n.s.); single-layout contrasts describe that layout, not the design rule. M2 < M1 by 17-42% in all spread/dyn_opt rows. EXPLORATORY: CAUSAL < CT by 46-65%, but both leave bin 0 at zero (no observation precedes t=7200 s), inflating both arms (CAUSAL J_pop ~0.062 in every cell) so the size is uninterpretable; F-065 applies (CT is a causal static fit). MODEL ERROR (96-episode subset; ratios of means, no intervals; truth perturbed, inverse nominal): M2 error x1.2-2.5 (mesh250: 250 m/dt 50 truth block-averaged to 500 m), x1.5-4.1 (rot5), x2.2-8.3 (rot10), x3.5-15.6 (rot20), similar across layouts; M2's advantage over M1 vanishes or reverses by 10-20 degrees; absolute M2 errors under rot5 are 7-12x the projection floor 0.0023 of the unperturbed truth; mesh250 measures 500 m numerical-transport error plus dt effect (K_eff/K 2.4-9.4 is the known cause), not mesh adequacy. Invalid episodes (8): boundary-event families whose boundary was unobservable (excluded consistently, non-random).
