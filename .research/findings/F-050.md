# F-050 · DYN-04 confirmatory (EXP-032, RUN-0090; 96 synthetic test episodes, seeds 7800-7895, 6 winds; layouts hash 8bdef74d7c30fe2e designed on the robust 6-wind surrogate BEFORE any episode; hyperparameters (hash 103b54317bac1be5) tuned per budget and noise on the spread layout only and applied unchanged to every layout of that budget; 70 candidate cells; real nonnegative M2 estimator; 0 non-converged fits; paired episode bootstrap at Bonferroni 0.05/4, effect criterion = CI excludes 0 and >=10% relative change and sign persisting leaving each wind out). Dynamic-optimal layouts (surrogate with 30 time-resolved parameters averaged over the winds) beat the registered baselines in most cells: vs RANDOM layouts they win in all 8 budget x noise cells (-32% to -84% sqrt(J_pop), e.g. k=3: 0.0030 vs 0.0128 ug/m3 at 2% noise) and vs SPREAD layouts in 7 of 8 (k=2: -28/-29%, k=3: -67/-72%, k=5: -64/-65%) but LOSE to spread at k=1, 10% noise (+20% [+0.00155,+0.00410]; equal at 2% noise because the two layouts coincide). vs the STATIC-model-optimal layouts: win at k=1 (-38/-29%), k=3 (-40/-50%), tie at k=2 (+2%, CI includes 0) and effectively tie at k=5 (-4/-5%, below the 10% criterion). vs the HIGH-POPULATION heuristic: win at k=1 (-41/-31%) and k=2 (-31/-36%) but NOT at k=3 (+23% not significant at 2% noise; -7% at 10%) and the heuristic is BETTER at k=5 (+13% [+0.00003,+0.00033] at 2% noise, +8% at 10%); the population-weighted objective with three concentrated settlements makes 'put sensors where people live' a strong baseline. vs SOURCE-identification-optimal (A-optimal) layouts: win at k=1 (-33%), k=2 (2% noise: -63%) and k=3 (-60/-67%), tie at k=5, but LOSE at k=2, 10% noise (+35% [+0.00024,+0.00494]), a population-vs-source tradeoff visible in the per-layout tables. Registered H3 (dynamic-optimal outperforms static, spread, random, high-population and source-oriented layouts) is therefore SUPPORTED against random and spread layouts at budgets >=2, PARTLY against static (k=1,3 only) and source-oriented layouts, and NOT SUPPORTED against the high-population heuristic at k=3 and k=5. Surrogate-vs-actual agreement (Spearman over the 15 evaluated layouts per cell) improves with budget: k=1 0.43/0.37, k=2 0.61/0.67, k=3 0.81/0.88, k=5 0.89/0.95 (2%/10% noise): the Gaussian surrogate ranks layouts well at larger budgets and poorly at k=1 (consistent with F-046's finding that it cannot rank near-optimal sets). Search: greedy is far from the surrogate optimum at small budgets (dynamic surrogate risk greedy gap x2.1 at k=3, 2% noise; x4.2 at k=5) and swap multistart nearly closes the gap; there is no optimality certificate at k=5. Caveats: evaluation hyperparameters were tuned on the spread layout (could disfavour other layouts), one population fixture and source geometry, nominal known wind (DYN-07 shows model error dominates sensor count), design at the evaluation noise level, ensemble of 10 random layouts averaged, multiple comparisons (stricter 0.05/40 intervals are in the run summary), preliminary and not independently reviewed.

Dynamic-optimal layouts (surrogate with 30 time-resolved parameters averaged over the winds) beat the registered baselines in most cells: vs RANDOM layouts they win in all 8 budget x noise cells (-32% to -84% sqrt(J_pop), e.g. k=3: 0.0030 vs 0.0128 ug/m3 at 2% noise) and vs SPREAD layouts in 7 of 8 (k=2: -28/-29%, k=3: -67/-72%, k=5: -64/-65%) but LOSE to spread at k=1, 10% noise (+20% [+0.00155,+0.00410]; equal at 2% noise because the two layouts coincide). vs the STATIC-model-optimal layouts: win at k=1 (-38/-29%), k=3 (-40/-50%), tie at k=2 (+2%, CI includes 0) and effectively tie at k=5 (-4/-5%, below the 10% criterion). vs the HIGH-POPULATION heuristic: win at k=1 (-41/-31%) and k=2 (-31/-36%) but NOT at k=3 (+23% not significant at 2% noise; -7% at 10%) and the heuristic is BETTER at k=5 (+13% [+0.00003,+0.00033] at 2% noise, +8% at 10%); the population-weighted objective with three concentrated settlements makes 'put sensors where people live' a strong baseline. vs SOURCE-identification-optimal (A-optimal) layouts: win at k=1 (-33%), k=2 (2% noise: -63%) and k=3 (-60/-67%), tie at k=5, but LOSE at k=2, 10% noise (+35% [+0.00024,+0.00494]), a population-vs-source tradeoff visible in the per-layout tables.
