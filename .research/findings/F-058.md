# F-058 · DYN-05 / P0 corrected final statement (supersedes F-046 after review F-046/R1; all numbers reproduce). DISCLOSURES restated here: (i) D-018 (independent evaluation reference B and 20000-episode references) was written AFTER the RUN-0077 summary was printed and flipped one borderline cell (noise 0.10, k=2: registered criterion met from N=48 under RUN-0077, from N=24 under RUN-0078; a p90 over 16 replicates is an interpolated order statistic); (ii) an earlier real-estimator run RUN-0079 had a layout-key collision and was re-run as RUN-0080 on the same seeds 8400-8495. SURROGATE RESULT (registered criterion: 90th percentile of relative regret <= 5% on independent reference B): met from N=24 (noise 0.10, k=2: 4.8%) and N=48 (noise 0.02, k=3; all 16 ensembles identical); not met up to N=192 for noise 0.02, k=2 (10.5%) and noise 0.10, k=3 (23-46%); N<=12 at noise 0.02, k=3 gives regret up to x29 when an unstratified ensemble misses a wind. REAL-ESTIMATOR CHECK (nonnegative ridge, 96 fresh episodes, ratio of mean sqrt(J_pop) vs the reference layout; this 'CI excludes 0' criterion is NOT the registered one and is only descriptive): episode-bootstrap CIs with the 16 ensemble layouts held fixed: 0.10,k=2: +34% [15,58] at N=6, +28% [8,54] at N=24, +16% [2,34] at N=192; 0.02,k=2: +29% [10,51] at N=6, +14% [3,26] at N=192; 0.02,k=3: +9% [3,17] at N=6, +6% [2,11] at N=24, exactly 0 from N=48; 0.10,k=3: -10% [-27,+9] at N=6, -13% [-22,-3] at N=192. A two-stage bootstrap (resampling ensembles and episodes, the right interval for 'is N sufficient') is wider: 0.02,k=2 [5,78]; 0.10,k=2 [12,65]; 0.02,k=3 [-0.06,+22] (P(<=0)=0.027); so a 6-episode ensemble is clearly worse than the reference layout in 2 cells and borderline in a third. The reference layout is itself a poor reference: in a split-half check (best evaluated layout chosen on even episodes, measured on odd) S*_A is +31% [6,70] worse than the best evaluated layout at noise 0.10, k=3 and +21.5% [-5,57] at noise 0.02, k=3 (where the stable choice from N=48 is S*_A), so regret relative to S*_A cannot certify sufficiency in those cells, and the surrogate cannot rank near-optimal sets (the real estimator rates S*_B 22% better than S*_A at 0.10,k=3 while the surrogate has them within 0.5%). CONCLUSION: design sufficiency is cell-dependent; by the surrogate criterion 6 episodes are insufficient everywhere; by the real estimator they are clearly insufficient in two cells and not clearly different in the others; an unconstrained-ridge surrogate with a surrogate-selected reference cannot certify sufficiency for the constrained estimator. Not claimed: that EXP-013/014/020/026 failed the same way (wind sampling unchecked). Caveats: one fixture, k=2,3 only, wind known, preliminary.
