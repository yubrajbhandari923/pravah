# F-035 · EXP-015 stage 2: H1 untested (baseline not commensurable); 500 m provisionally admissible on narrow grounds

## Statement

EXP-015 stage 2 did NOT test what it was registered to test: the mesh comparison and the resampling baseline are not commensurable, so H1 is untested rather than supported. Every mesh selects layouts from the same seed-101 design draws (zero case-draw variability) while each replicate uses a different draw (full case-draw variability), so a one-sided mesh<=baseline test cannot fail - a deliberately broken 2 km mesh would pass too. Separately the registered two-sided IQR criterion yields no verdict at all, because all six failures are below the baseline floor. 500 m / dt 100 is PROVISIONALLY ADMISSIBLE for EXP-026 on narrow grounds (mesh-induced displacement 2532/2771/607 m at budgets 3/5/8 and noise 0.05 is of the same order or smaller than resampling spread; dt 100 vs 50 is exactly a no-op), but that means no evidence against, not shown adequate.

## Limitations

SYNTHETIC: real Belauri geography, synthetic emissions, winds and noise. Nothing empirically validated. (1) The central H1 test is structurally non-falsifiable (convergence_stage2.py:244,:258 vs :274); the paired fix is to select at 500 m AND 250 m on each of the 8 replicate draws. (2) The registered rule tests IQR_low<=x<=IQR_high while the hypothesis says no larger than; the one-sided reading is a POST-HOC repair found after seeing results and must be registered as a dated amendment before reuse. (3) Held-out test shows NO DETECTABLE DIFFERENCE: the apparent 6-35 percent advantage is one of six cases (leave-one-out -4.0 and -8.7 percent at budgets 3 and 5; Wilcoxon p 0.22-0.56 at n=6). (4) The design objective is NOT IDENTIFIABLE at 6 cases: eps_sd/J_ref 1.01-2.52 at noise 0.05, so tie_fraction does not measure flatness; budget 8 / noise 0.05 excluded from stability claims. (5) Mesh regret is NEGATIVE (-6.8 to -11.5 percent of J_ref), falsifying numerical-methodology.md section 5 non-negativity and bounding greedy sub-optimality at >=7-12 percent at the reference mesh, so regret partly measures greedy path-dependence. (6) Budgets 3/5/8 are nested prefixes of one greedy trace, so effective n = 2 independent comparisons, not 6. (7) Noise 0.15 uninformative for mesh: sampling noise ~1.3 x J_ref swamps the mesh difference. (8) dt claim scoped to 100 vs 50; dt 25 never run; the 125 m arm was dropped although its registered stop condition was not met (undeclared deviation). (9) Cluster numbers carry a ~5e-08 relative architecture floor vs laptop Phase 1-3 numbers (D-005), six orders below anything in play. (10) Test seed 303 is now burned and cannot serve as held-out for EXP-026; seed 304 remains unspent. (11) 1 of 142 interior candidates changes study category between meshes. (12) Not verified: physics, solver internals, re-derivation of the CSVs from the layouts JSONs, figures.
