# F-027 · Toy prescribed winds (corrected after review): outer-domain truncation not separated from boundary artefacts; vortex-type psi 'ridge' has no net deflection by construction; eddy mostly raises local exposure

## Statement

Prescribed winds, not terrain-derived. External exposure of the study box relative to the 2x domain is -1.5% to -21.5% at 1x and up to -8.8% at 1.5x, returned mass -78% / -26%, but the 2x reference is itself unconverged at reversal 19800 s (7.1% of emitted mass leaves east before the reversal; 1.5x vs 2x still differs 26%), the source is 1.5 km from the zero-concentration west boundary so even steady winds lose ~2% of emitted mass there (the 1x -1.5% is a boundary artefact), the Eulerian return definition undercounts when the plume tail is still inside at reversal (t_r 12600) and decay shrinks late returns, so the ~1.5x threshold and the non-monotone return are not established. The Gaussian psi bump is a sub-critical vortex: far from it streamlines return to their upstream y, so a null effect is structural at any amplitude below closed streamlines; the 'ridge' and 'eddy' are the same function. The eddy's +34% total exposure is mostly local (+53%, external +24%). Mass closure 3.9e-11.

## Limitations

Hand-placed idealised winds, one source geometry, dx 750 m with large numerical diffusion, reference domain unconverged, independently reviewed; follow-up needed: larger reference domain, source farther from the boundary, a deflecting-channel psi=U(y-h(x)) instead of a vortex
