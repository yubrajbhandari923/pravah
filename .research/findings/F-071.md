# F-071 · EXP-026 (RUN-0098): WorldPop fixture is a joint null with the synthetic fixtures (replaces F-067)

## Statement

Corrects F-067 after F-067/R1. WorldPop (single fixture and draw, 12 cases): no beneficial effect meets the registered criterion; the one resolved harmful budget 2-4 cell is noise 0.15 budget 3 (+0.00778; analyst case-bootstrap CI [0.00084,0.01622] from scripts/make_exp026_figures.py seed 0, reviewer reproduction [0.00089,0.01613]; 75% = 9/12 agree, exactly at the threshold; area RMSE +0.00914). It is the same (noise, budget) cell as the synthetic harm cell and both are compared against the same area reference layout (17-23-30), which ranks 3rd of 48 on test: a shared-comparator effect, not evidence of transfer. Noise 0.15 budget 2 has CI excluding 0 (-0.00707) but only 67% agree: not resolved. Sign counts for the population objective: 5 negative, 3 positive, 1 zero, 1 negligible (|diff| < 1e-5). H5 is a joint null: neither a transferred benefit nor a resolved reversal. The inter-draw spread cannot be assessed (one draw). Cross-fixture (ratio of mean pop-RMSE to the area layout, cell means over the 4 synthetic fixtures): population-designed synthetic layouts evaluated on WorldPop 1.02-1.31 (35 of 40 fixture-cells above 1, 5 identical layouts); WorldPop-designed layouts on synthetic fixtures 0.65-2.26 (the earlier mean-of-per-case-ratios statistic, 1.12-1.40 and 0.82-1.89, is biased and not used).

## Limitations

One WorldPop field and design sample; modelled population; synthetic atmosphere; provisional mesh; 12 cases (low power, F-070); D-028 deviation; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
