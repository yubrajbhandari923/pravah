# F-024 · Toy prescribed winds: outer domain smaller than ~1.5x upwind extent truncates delayed return of an external plume (up to -21% external exposure, -78% returned mass); ridge-only deflection changed little, eddy changed exposure by ~34%

## Statement

Toy domain, external pulse 1800-5400 s, prescribed winds (not terrain-derived). Relative to the largest outer domain (2x): outer-domain 1x (27 km) underestimates study external exposure by 1.5-21% and returned-mass fraction by up to 78% (reversal at 19800 s), 1.5x by up to 8.8% / 26%; returned mass fraction is non-monotone in reversal time (0.128 at 12600 s, 0.213 at 16200 s, 0.145 at 19800 s at 2x). The ridge-deflection wind gave study exposure within 1% of steady wind at the chosen amplitude; the recirculation eddy raised total exposure by 34%. Mass closure <= 3.9e-11, no negatives.

## Limitations

Hand-placed idealised winds, one source geometry, one grid (dx 750 m) with strong numerical diffusion, returned-mass is an Eulerian net-flux definition (docs/winds.md), per-side flux cross-check agrees only to 7e-3 of emitted mass, ridge amplitude weak by choice, no terrain physics, ERA5 not downloaded
