# F-046 · DYN-05 / P0 final statement (supersedes F-045, which overstated one sentence; every number in F-045 stands). By the registered SURROGATE criterion a 6-episode design ensemble is insufficient in every cell (p90 regret 14% to x29). By the REAL constrained estimator it is insufficient in 3 of the 4 cells (noise 0.02 k=2: +29% [10,51]; noise 0.10 k=2: +34% [15,58]; noise 0.02 k=3: +9% [3,17]) and NOT demonstrably insufficient at noise 0.10, k=3 (-10% [-27,+9], i.e. no detectable loss, and the ensemble layouts are on average better than the surrogate-reference layout from N=96 on: -11% [-18,-3] at N=96, -13% [-22,-3] at N=192). The surrogate and the real estimator therefore disagree on where the design is stable, which is the main outcome: an unconstrained-ridge design objective cannot certify design sufficiency for the nonnegative estimator. All other statements, disclosures and caveats of F-045 apply unchanged. Preliminary.

## Statement

The surrogate and the real estimator therefore disagree on where the design is stable, which is the main outcome: an unconstrained-ridge design objective cannot certify design sufficiency for the nonnegative estimator.
