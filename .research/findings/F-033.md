# F-033 · Toy wind-error controls (EXP-023): F-029's direction-error response survives alpha, off-member prior, model error and base-wind changes; chi2 detection weakens modestly and coverage is much lower under model error

## Statement

Controls on the EXP-019 toy (32 test cases, reduced grids, 10 random layouts). (a) Alpha: with strategy A at its own validation alpha 0.1 its 90% coverage is 0.90 (0.74-0.76 at alpha 10), so the low coverage in F-029 is regularisation shrinkage; B-A gap at 30 deg is 0.0283 (own alphas) vs 0.0247 (alpha 10), 0.0330 at alpha 1, 0.0427 at alpha 0.1; B itself gets worse at smaller alpha (0.0545 at 30 deg, alpha 0.1 vs 0.0400 at 10) so the wind-error penalty is larger with less regularisation; C-B at 30 deg is -0.0183 (alpha 10), -0.0189 (alpha 1), -0.0123 (alpha 0.1). (b) Off-member prior (directions -17.5..22.5 step 10 deg, no test direction a member, nominal wind not a member): C-B is negative with intervals excluding 0 at every non-zero direction: -0.0016/-0.0017 (+-5), -0.0084/-0.0075 (+-10), -0.0120/-0.0143 (+-15), -0.0034 (-30; outside the prior by 12.5 deg) and -0.0215 (+30; 7.5 deg outside); at zero error C is worse than B by +0.0029 (the nearest members are +-2.5 deg). So C's advantage is not an artefact of the truth being a prior member. (c) Truth with K x1.2 and the estimator at nominal K: at zero wind error chi2/dof is 1.09 (vs 1.01) and 20% of fits reject (vs 5.8%); detection of wind error remains strong (B chi2/dof 9.2 and 11.8 at +-30 deg vs 12.7 and 13.8; rejections 90% and 94% vs 93% and 91%) but at +-10 deg B rejects 63%/58% (vs 74%/72%); 90% coverage drops for A from 0.73 to 0.51 and for C from 0.77 to 0.61. (d) Base wind rotated 45 and 90 deg: the same ordering A < C < B holds at +-10 and +-30 deg; B-A at +-30 deg is 0.016-0.024 (vs 0.027-0.029 in F-029), A's own error is larger for some base winds (0.012-0.020), so B/A ratios are 2.0-3.0 (vs 3.5-3.8); chi2/dof for B at +-30 deg is 6.1-12.9.

## Limitations

Not yet independently reviewed. Toy domain, single base geometry per control, reduced direction grids and 10 random layouts per control, reports for three of the controls were rerun after a run-budget block; (b)/(d) baselines are the EXP-019 production runs with different seeds, only (c) has a same-seed baseline; model error is one parameter (K x1.2); bootstrap intervals are over 32 test cases.
