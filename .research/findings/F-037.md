# F-037 · DYN-02 toy (RUN-0061, 48 synthetic test episodes, seeds 7100-7147, wind known and matched, forward model identical to truth model but truth on a finer 12-bin time grid): temporally regularised M2 reduces population-weighted reconstruction RMSE vs independent-time M1 at 4 sensors (mean paired J_pop difference -0.0010 [-0.0022,-0.0002] at 2% noise, -0.0018 [-0.0030,-0.0007] at 10%; ~30-45% of M1's error) and vs static M0 (-0.0073 [-0.0097,-0.0050]); with 9 sensors M1 is already at the 6-bin representation floor (oracle 0.00123) and M2's gain is negligible (-4e-6 at 2%) to small (-1.7e-4 at 10%). Caveats: M2 is NOT uniformly better (4-sensor shutdown family: M2 0.0033 vs M1 0.0020 at 10% noise); local-source RMSE is 4-24% of the emission scale (M2 4.5e3, M0 2.4e4 ug/s) and boundary-amplitude error shows NO resolved M2-M1 difference (CI includes 0); M1 alpha for 4 sensors sat on the grid edge (100), so M1 may be under-tuned; no wind error, no missing sources, one population fixture, 6 episodes per family. Pre-registered criterion for H1 (paired CI excludes 0 at both noise levels) met for J_pop at both layouts; preliminary, unreviewed, synthetic only.

## Statement

DYN-02 toy (RUN-0061, 48 synthetic test episodes, seeds 7100-7147, wind known and matched, forward model identical to truth model but truth on a finer 12-bin time grid): temporally regularised M2 reduces population-weighted reconstruction RMSE vs independent-time M1 at 4 sensors (mean paired J_pop difference -0.0010 [-0.0022,-0.0002] at 2% noise, -0.0018 [-0.0030,-0.0007] at 10%; ~30-45% of M1's error) and vs static M0 (-0.0073 [-0.0097,-0.0050]); with 9 sensors M1 is already at the 6-bin representation floor (oracle 0.00123) and M2's gain is negligible (-4e-6 at 2%) to small (-1.7e-4 at 10%). Caveats: M2 is NOT uniformly better (4-sensor shutdown family: M2 0.0033 vs M1 0.0020 at 10% noise); local-source RMSE is 4-24% of the emission scale (M2 4.5e3, M0 2.4e4 ug/s) and boundary-amplitude error shows NO resolved M2-M1 difference (CI includes 0); M1 alpha for 4 sensors sat on the grid edge (100), so M1 may be under-tuned; no wind error, no missing sources, one population fixture, 6 episodes per family. Pre-registered criterion for H1 (paired CI excludes 0 at both noise levels) met for J_pop at both layouts; preliminary, unreviewed, synthetic only.
