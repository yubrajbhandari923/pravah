# F-067 · EXP-026 (RUN-0098): WorldPop fixture shows the same null as the synthetic fixtures; synthetic population layouts are worse than area layouts on WorldPop

## Statement

On the modelled WorldPop field (single fixture and draw, 12 cases) the population-objective effect on pop-weighted RMSE is unresolved: no benefit meets the registered criterion; the one resolved harmful budget 2-4 cell is noise 0.15 budget 3 (+0.00778, CI [0.00084,0.01622], 75% agree; area RMSE +0.00914 [0.00095,0.01939]); noise 0.15 budget 2 has CI excluding 0 (-0.00707 [-0.01545,-0.00065]) but only 67% of cases agree, so not resolved. Noise 0.05 budget 8 mixed_0.25 is resolved harmful (+0.00106 [0.00041,0.00174], 83%). The WorldPop intervals are the analyst's case-bootstrap recomputation (the run's own interval is NaN with one draw). Sign counts for the population objective on WorldPop: 6 negative, 3 positive, 1 zero (10 cells). The synthetic concentrated fixtures show the same null and the same harm cell (mixed_rural_urban, noise 0.15, budget 3), so H5 is a joint null: neither a transferred benefit nor a resolved reversal is established. The inter-draw spread criterion cannot be assessed for WorldPop (one draw). Cross-fixture: layouts designed on synthetic fixtures are worse than the area layout when evaluated on WorldPop in all 10 cells (ratio to the area layout 1.12-1.40); WorldPop-designed layouts on synthetic fixtures range 0.82-1.89.

## Limitations
