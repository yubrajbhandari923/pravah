# F-072 · EXP-026 (RUN-0098): greedy gaps and design-sample optimism are large; objectives differ rather than tie (replaces F-068)

## Statement

Corrects F-068 after F-068/R1 (numbers reproduce). Greedy design objectives are 28-79% above the best found on average (design_gap.csv). Test-RMSE difference greedy minus best-found is mixed in sign (150 positive vs 90 negative cells; 26 resolved under the run's two-way interval, 32 under the registered case-only interval with 16 greedy-worse). Design-objective optimism, measured on 3-case half-designs (draw 0, budgets 5 and 8 only): other-half objective exceeds own-half by a median of 175-531% (this overstates optimism for 6-case designs). All fixture draws and fixtures share the SAME 6 design cases (seed 101), so draws vary only the population field and design-sample noise is never replicated. Objectives select the identical layout in 54% of pair rows (2499 of 2535 near-tied rows are identical layouts); when layouts differ the design-objective gap exceeds the 1% tie threshold in 98% of rows (near-tied in only 36 of 2151). Exact-search margin of the optimum over the second best: median 4.3%, 8.6% of cases under 1%. Best-found search at budgets 5 and 8: 44% of searches hit the best value from only 1 of 21 starts. For the population objective the median |mean diff| (0.000563) is 1.7x the median IQR of per-draw means (0.000323); for all objectives 0.000181 vs 0.000098. Reading: greedy path dependence and design-sample optimism are large; objective differences are not small relative to fixture-draw spread and are rarely near-tied; the null of F-070 is better explained by the low power of 12 test cases and the single shared area reference layout than by ties.

## Limitations

Exact search only to budget 4; design_split covers draw 0, budgets 5 and 8; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
