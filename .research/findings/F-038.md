# F-038 · DYN-00 (RUN-0059): 20/20 pre-set verification checks pass on a 16x12 toy box: mass budget closes to <=4.4e-15 relative for activation/shutdown, simultaneous change, pulse + wind reversal, rotating wind with changing inflow faces, tangential and zero wind; no negative concentrations; superposition 1.6e-15; closed-box on/off emission matches the analytic mass history with temporal order 0.997 (implicit Euler) and error 1.7e-3 at dt 30 s; dt and dx refinement of a reversing-wind sensor series converge monotonically (dx 16->32 vs 64: 9.6% -> 2.5%); bin-expanded responses reproduce a direct constant-profile run to 1.4e-15; footprint integrals are 1 for point, line and area sources. One check (west_inflow_after_reversal) was amended AFTER the first run because it was ill-posed (the budget 'inflow' term includes the Dirichlet loss a_in*c, so it is negative, not zero, once the face becomes inflow-free); the amended physical claim is 'no net import' (max<=0). Verified numerical behaviour of the implicit-Euler/exponential scheme only; not a nonlinear-scheme (Van Leer) or Belauri-scale check, and spatial order was only checked for monotone convergence.

## Statement

DYN-00 (RUN-0059): 20/20 pre-set verification checks pass on a 16x12 toy box: mass budget closes to <=4.4e-15 relative for activation/shutdown, simultaneous change, pulse + wind reversal, rotating wind with changing inflow faces, tangential and zero wind; no negative concentrations; superposition 1.6e-15; closed-box on/off emission matches the analytic mass history with temporal order 0.997 (implicit Euler) and error 1.7e-3 at dt 30 s; dt and dx refinement of a reversing-wind sensor series converge monotonically (dx 16->32 vs 64: 9.6% -> 2.5%); bin-expanded responses reproduce a direct constant-profile run to 1.4e-15; footprint integrals are 1 for point, line and area sources. One check (west_inflow_after_reversal) was amended AFTER the first run because it was ill-posed (the budget 'inflow' term includes the Dirichlet loss a_in*c, so it is negative, not zero, once the face becomes inflow-free); the amended physical claim is 'no net import' (max<=0). Verified numerical behaviour of the implicit-Euler/exponential scheme only; not a nonlinear-scheme (Van Leer) or Belauri-scale check, and spatial order was only checked for monotone convergence.
