# F-075 · EXP-027b (EXP-037; RUN-0096, RUN-0100): registered outcome INADEQUATE (rule-based, borderline) for the 500 m / dt 100 mesh; gate passed; replaces F-073

## Statement

Corrects F-073 after F-073/R1 (numbers, rule and staging verified). Protocol docs/exp027b_design.md section 3 (margin 0.05 = a convention carried from DYN-05, fixed after pilots, not a calibrated tolerance; seeds 1201-1203; 3 draws x 24 cases = 72 cases simulated once per mesh at 250 m/dt50 reference, 500 m/dt100, 2 km/dt400 control; cells noise {0.05,0.15} x budget {3,5}; menu of up to 36 layouts per cell scored case by case on every mesh; E = median over the menu of the relative change of the scale-free objective; percentile case bootstrap, 400 resamples). Stage 2 (all 72 cases, the registered final evaluation, run because the stage-1 gate passed) : (G) GATE PASSED: the 2 km control has E_lo95 > 0.05 in 4 of 4 cells (E 0.443 [0.268,0.615], 0.379 [0.182,0.472], 0.234 [0.128,0.272], 0.115 [0.075,0.192]). (A) By the registered rule the 500 m mesh is INADEQUATE. It rests on the noise 0.05 / budget 3 cell (E 0.0873 [0.0595,0.1881]); the noise 0.05 / budget 5 lower bound (0.0516; E 0.0919 [0.0516,0.1948]) is within bootstrap Monte Carlo error of the margin (reviewer estimate SE ~0.003 at B=400); the other two cells are INCONCLUSIVE (0.15/3 E 0.0765 [0.0336,0.1025]; 0.15/5 E 0.0284 [0.0262,0.0723]); no cell is ADEQUATE. The percentile interval of this median-absolute statistic is biased upward (point estimates sit near the lower bound; a basic interval would make both inadequate cells INCONCLUSIVE; a toy Monte Carlo gave P(E_lo95>0.05) of 0.01-0.65 at true E = 0.05 depending on case heterogeneity): the evidence is that 500 m shape error is probably around 5-10%, not that it is reliably above 5%. Stage 1 (48 cases) gave INCONCLUSIVE (gate 3 of 4; main E 0.084/0.046/0.050/0.017, E_hi95 0.156/0.109/0.097/0.045); stage 2 was the registered continuation (gate-conditional, stage-1 cases reused), not optional stopping. Descriptive: Spearman rho of 500 m vs 250 m layout scores 0.94-0.96 (point estimates, no intervals; stage-1 rho was 0.80 at 0.05/3); decision regret point estimates 0-0.03 but R_hi95 0.11-0.32, so decision equivalence is not established; median J ratio 0.85-0.93 in stage 2 but 0.96-1.06 in stage 1 (direction not stable). Not tested here: whether the shape error stems from numerical diffusion (K_eff/K 2.4-9.4 measured elsewhere); F-069's mesh250 ratios measure model error, not adequacy, and have no intervals. Rankings are largely preserved, but this experiment neither confirms nor rules out decision-relevant differences. 500 m / dt 100 remains PROVISIONAL.

## Limitations

Menu built and scored on the same cases (in-sample selection, fixed inside the bootstrap) and about a third derived from the 2 km control mesh; E rose from stage 1 to stage 2 in 3 of 4 cells, suggesting a few cases dominate; per-draw E, leave-one-draw-out, other interval types and menu-restricted E are post-hoc sensitivity analyses pending (not yet run); E is a scale-free shape statistic, not a decision-quality measure; no 1 km control; D-016 control tolerance; padded domain; synthetic; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
