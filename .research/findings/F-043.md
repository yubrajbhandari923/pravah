# F-043 · DYN-06 v2 (RUN-0083; null-calibrated detection per D-020, new noise seeds 9100-9299, 100 replicates, 6 winds, scenario pairs scaled to distinguishability d = 2/4/8/16; synthetic, wind known; frozen ridge alpha). Local-vs-boundary attribution is governed by wind-and-layout geometry, not by noise or by the field-level distinguishability d. At 4 sensors and d=16 the local-increase-vs-influx scenario S1 is attributed perfectly (detect 1.00, wrong-group 0.00) in 3 of 6 winds (steady_a, rotation_b, reversal) and essentially not at all in steady_b and rotation_a (detect ~0.1 = the false-alarm level, wrong-group 0.9-1.0), identically at 2% and 10% noise; slow_reversal is intermediate (0.31-0.35 detect). The same layout on S4 (opposing local changes) fails only in rotation_a. The cause is that the group (local vs boundary) contribution is poorly determined in those winds: the null (no-change) fluctuation of the group contribution is as large as the change although the two fields differ by 16 noise sd. With 9 sensors the same scenarios succeed in 5-6 of 6 winds (S1: wrong-group 0.02, detect 0.91 at d=16; slow_reversal remains 0.49 detect). Averaged over winds at d=16, 10% noise: wrong-group rate at 4 / 9 sensors is S1 0.34 / 0.02, S2 0.05 / 0.05, S4 0.17 / 0.10, S5 (influx ends while the plume persists) 0.14 / 0.15, S6 (two similar local sources) 0.04 / 0.00. Registered predictions: P1 (misattribution falls with d) mostly holds (S5 at 4 sensors is flat at 0.11-0.14); P2 (<=10% wrong-group at d=16 when the column-space cosine is < 0.9) is CONTRADICTED for S1 at 4 sensors (cos 0.53, 34%) and for the reference-layout S2 (23%); P3 (>=25% wrong-group at d=2) holds for S1, S2, S4, S6 but not S5 at 4 sensors (18%) or S6 with the reference layout (9%); P4 (a boundary-reference sensor gives no more than 5 points of lower misattribution at equal d) holds: replacing one interior sensor by a boundary-reference sensor changed wrong-group at d=16 by +3 (S1), +18 (S2), +9 (S4), -4 (S5), -4 (S6) points, i.e. no general benefit (it is not a free lunch for attribution). Controls: the perfectly confounded fixture has d=3.6e-14 and attribution at chance only once the signal is large; the no-variation control reports no change in 100% of replicates (legacy rule). Supersedes the conclusions drawn from the flawed detection rule of F-042 (that finding's analytic and misattribution-among-detected statements remain). Caveats: the null threshold is the 95th percentile of 100 null replicates; one geometry/population fixture; frozen alpha from the nominal tune; no model error; winds are the replication unit for the structural claim (6 winds only); preliminary, not independently reviewed.

## Statement

DYN-06 v2 (RUN-0083; null-calibrated detection per D-020, new noise seeds 9100-9299, 100 replicates, 6 winds, scenario pairs scaled to distinguishability d = 2/4/8/16; synthetic, wind known; frozen ridge alpha). Local-vs-boundary attribution is governed by wind-and-layout geometry, not by noise or by the field-level distinguishability d. At 4 sensors and d=16 the local-increase-vs-influx scenario S1 is attributed perfectly (detect 1.00, wrong-group 0.00) in 3 of 6 winds (steady_a, rotation_b, reversal) and essentially not at all in steady_b and rotation_a (detect ~0.1 = the false-alarm level, wrong-group 0.9-1.0), identically at 2% and 10% noise; slow_reversal is intermediate (0.31-0.35 detect). The same layout on S4 (opposing local changes) fails only in rotation_a. The cause is that the group (local vs boundary) contribution is poorly determined in those winds: the null (no-change) fluctuation of the group contribution is as large as the change although the two fields differ by 16 noise sd. With 9 sensors the same scenarios succeed in 5-6 of 6 winds (S1: wrong-group 0.02, detect 0.91 at d=16; slow_reversal remains 0.49 detect). Averaged over winds at d=16, 10% noise: wrong-group rate at 4 / 9 sensors is S1 0.34 / 0.02, S2 0.05 / 0.05, S4 0.17 / 0.10, S5 (influx ends while the plume persists) 0.14 / 0.15, S6 (two similar local sources) 0.04 / 0.00. Registered predictions: P1 (misattribution falls with d) mostly holds (S5 at 4 sensors is flat at 0.11-0.14); P2 (<=10% wrong-group at d=16 when the column-space cosine is < 0.9) is CONTRADICTED for S1 at 4 sensors (cos 0.53, 34%) and for the reference-layout S2 (23%); P3 (>=25% wrong-group at d=2) holds for S1, S2, S4, S6 but not S5 at 4 sensors (18%) or S6 with the reference layout (9%); P4 (a boundary-reference sensor gives no more than 5 points of lower misattribution at equal d) holds: replacing one interior sensor by a boundary-reference sensor changed wrong-group at d=16 by +3 (S1), +18 (S2), +9 (S4), -4 (S5), -4 (S6) points, i.e. no general benefit (it is not a free lunch for attribution). Controls: the perfectly confounded fixture has d=3.6e-14 and attribution at chance only once the signal is large; the no-variation control reports no change in 100% of replicates (legacy rule). Supersedes the conclusions drawn from the flawed detection rule of F-042 (that finding's analytic and misattribution-among-detected statements remain). Caveats: the null threshold is the 95th percentile of 100 null replicates; one geometry/population fixture; frozen alpha from the nominal tune; no model error; winds are the replication unit for the structural claim (6 winds only); preliminary, not independently reviewed.
