# F-048 · EXP-027 / P1 (RUN-0081 on Duke x86_64, 32 workers, 44 min wall; protocol D-012/D-016; padded Belauri domain with comparable 250 m (reference), 500 m (main) and 2 km (falsification control) meshes; synthetic emissions/meteorology on real geography; 8 independent replicate design draws of 6 cases each, seeds 1101-1108, greedy_total, budgets 3 and 5, noise fractions 0.05 and 0.15; tables in results/exp027/). REGISTERED STOP TRIGGERED: the 2 km falsification control PASSES the paired test in all 4 cells, so the test is non-falsifiable at this ensemble size and the experiment is VOID as an adequacy test. Numbers (median mesh-induced mean site displacement, 8 paired values per mesh, vs median of the 28 replicate-pair displacements at the 250 m reference mesh): budget 3 / noise 0.05: 500 m 2108 m, 2 km 2262 m vs pairs 3691 m; budget 5 / 0.05: 2738 m, 2567 m vs 3147 m; budget 3 / 0.15: 1118 m, 2370 m vs 3996 m; budget 5 / 0.15: 1728 m, 3234 m vs 3468 m; |regret| medians are ~0 for both meshes and 1e-4..2e-4 (reference objective units) for replicate pairs; one-sided Mann-Whitney p(mesh > pair) = 0.69-0.9997 (not significant). Interpretation: layouts selected from 6-case ensembles move by 3-4 km between independent draws, which is larger than the displacement caused by degrading the mesh by 4x (500 m) or 8x (2 km), so mesh effects cannot be seen through the design noise; this is the Q-013 / F-046 problem again (the design objective is not estimable with 6 cases), not evidence that 500 m is adequate or that 2 km is. It does show that the earlier statement 'no evidence against 500 m' (F-035/D-006) rests on a test that could not fail. 500 m / dt 100 remains PROVISIONALLY admissible only. Next experiment (EXP-027b, needs approval): the same paired design with ensembles large enough that replicate-pair displacement falls below the control's (the F-046 result suggests >= 48-96 cases per draw; ~4-8x the cost, ~90-190 core-hours at the observed 23 core-hours for this run). Caveats: padded domain differs from the original unpadded one; the 2 km control needed a control-only origin-tag tolerance (D-016, user-approved); one noise scale file and alpha frozen from the unpadded domain; displacement is in metres between assigned sites; not independently reviewed.
