# F-057 · EXP-027 / P1 corrected statement (supersedes F-048; independently reviewed as 'void is the correct registered conclusion'; arithmetic and wording corrected). RUN-0081 (Duke x86_64, 32 workers, 44 min wall, ~23 core-h billed); padded Belauri domain with 250 m (reference), 500 m (main) and 2 km (control) meshes; 8 independent replicate design draws of 6 cases (seeds 1101-1108), greedy total objective, budgets 3 and 5 (nested greedy prefixes sharing cases), noise 0.05/0.15; tables results/exp027. The registered stop triggered: the 2 km control passes in all 4 cells (one-sided Mann-Whitney p(mesh > pair) 0.69-1.000; the 28 replicate pairs come from 8 draws so the p-values are approximate), so the experiment is VOID as an adequacy test. Median mesh-induced displacement (500 m | 2 km | replicate pairs): 2108 | 2262 | 3691 m (budget 3, 0.05); 2738 | 2567 | 3147 m (5, 0.05); 1118 | 2370 | 3996 m (3, 0.15); 1728 | 3234 | 3468 m (5, 0.15). Median |regret| (reference-objective units): control 6.8e-6..4.4e-5, replicate pairs 2.6e-5, 6.0e-5, 6.3e-5, 1.9e-4 (control medians are 10-25% of the pair medians, not zero). Layouts from 6-case ensembles move by 3-4 km between independent draws, more than degrading the mesh spacing 2x (500 m) or 8x (2 km), so mesh effects are invisible through design noise. This does not show 500 m is adequate (it stays provisionally admissible only) and is consistent with, but not a demonstration about, F-035 (which argued analytically that a different, unpaired test could not fail). COST CORRECTION: a version with 48 or 96 cases per draw costs 8x or 16x this run per draw count, i.e. ~190 or ~375 core-hours for 8 draws (about 94 or 188 for 4 draws) at the observed efficiency; the suggestion that 48-96 cases suffice extrapolates the toy DYN-05 result (ridge risk, 70 cells, k=2,3) to Belauri greedy_total and is a guess. Caveats: padded domain differs from the unpadded one; the 2 km control needed a control-only origin-tag relaxation (D-016; labels only, the total objective does not use tags); budgets 3 and 5 are not independent tests.
