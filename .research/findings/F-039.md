# F-039 · DYN-02 v2 confirmatory (RUN-0064; 96 synthetic test episodes, seeds 7200-7295, 6 winds x 8 families x 2 seeds, wind known, fair converged baselines, protocol D-013 frozen before the run). With 4 sensors (L4) temporally regularised M2 lowers population-weighted RMSE vs the FAIR independent-bin baseline M1 (static prior mean) by 24-25%: mean paired difference -0.00081 [-0.00146,-0.00028] at 2% noise and -0.00083 [-0.00133,-0.00038] at 10% (Bonferroni 98.75% CI, M1 mean 0.0033 ug/m3; the registered effect criterion incl. >=10% gain and sign persisting leaving each wind out is met in both cells), and vs the zero-prior M1z by 37-40%. With 9 sensors (L9) M2 gives NO useful gain: +0.00006 (+5%, worse) at 2% noise and -0.00004 (-3%) at 10% (criterion not met); M1 there is within 3-8% of the 6-bin projection floor (mean floor 0.0012), i.e. representation-limited, not estimator-limited. M1/M2 beat the static M0 by 70-86% in all cells. Source errors (identifiable columns, relative to reference amplitude): at L4 M2 lowers local-source RMSE by 44-50% and boundary-amplitude RMSE by 26-32% (CIs exclude 0); at L9 differences are small (+/-16% local, +/-9% boundary, mixed sign). Heterogeneity is large: the L4 gain comes mainly from rotation_a, steady_b and slow_reversal winds and from ramp/compensating/activation families; it is ~0 for steady_a and reversal and M2 is WORSE for the shutdown family (+0.0003 at L4); so TV smoothing helps where the history is smooth or poorly sampled and hurts abrupt shutdown. Caveats: 3 of 384 M2 fits did not converge (excluded; replacing them by M1 changes nothing); validation selections sat on a grid edge in 7 of 16 choices (mostly alpha at the lower bound 0.01 where regularisation is negligible; M2 beta at the lower bound for L9 2%: TV effectively off there), as flagged in the frozen file; many M2 grid points failed to converge on validation (42-84 of 108 per cell), so M2 hyperparameters were chosen among converged candidates only; wind is known and the forward model equals the truth model (no model error: DYN-07); one population fixture; one source geometry; synthetic only; no independent review yet (this supersedes F-037, which was invalidated by review of the earlier pipeline).

## Statement

DYN-02 v2 confirmatory (RUN-0064; 96 synthetic test episodes, seeds 7200-7295, 6 winds x 8 families x 2 seeds, wind known, fair converged baselines, protocol D-013 frozen before the run). With 4 sensors (L4) temporally regularised M2 lowers population-weighted RMSE vs the FAIR independent-bin baseline M1 (static prior mean) by 24-25%: mean paired difference -0.00081 [-0.00146,-0.00028] at 2% noise and -0.00083 [-0.00133,-0.00038] at 10% (Bonferroni 98.75% CI, M1 mean 0.0033 ug/m3; the registered effect criterion incl. >=10% gain and sign persisting leaving each wind out is met in both cells), and vs the zero-prior M1z by 37-40%. With 9 sensors (L9) M2 gives NO useful gain: +0.00006 (+5%, worse) at 2% noise and -0.00004 (-3%) at 10% (criterion not met); M1 there is within 3-8% of the 6-bin projection floor (mean floor 0.0012), i.e. representation-limited, not estimator-limited. M1/M2 beat the static M0 by 70-86% in all cells. Source errors (identifiable columns, relative to reference amplitude): at L4 M2 lowers local-source RMSE by 44-50% and boundary-amplitude RMSE by 26-32% (CIs exclude 0); at L9 differences are small (+/-16% local, +/-9% boundary, mixed sign). Heterogeneity is large: the L4 gain comes mainly from rotation_a, steady_b and slow_reversal winds and from ramp/compensating/activation families; it is ~0 for steady_a and reversal and M2 is WORSE for the shutdown family (+0.0003 at L4); so TV smoothing helps where the history is smooth or poorly sampled and hurts abrupt shutdown. Caveats: 3 of 384 M2 fits did not converge (excluded; replacing them by M1 changes nothing); validation selections sat on a grid edge in 7 of 16 choices (mostly alpha at the lower bound 0.01 where regularisation is negligible; M2 beta at the lower bound for L9 2%: TV effectively off there), as flagged in the frozen file; many M2 grid points failed to converge on validation (42-84 of 108 per cell), so M2 hyperparameters were chosen among converged candidates only; wind is known and the forward model equals the truth model (no model error: DYN-07); one population fixture; one source geometry; synthetic only; no independent review yet (this supersedes F-037, which was invalidated by review of the earlier pipeline).
