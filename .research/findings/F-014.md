# F-014 · Geographic sensor design on Belauri: small-budget layout rankings depend on the noise level; differences beyond 5 sensors are within noise; external reference sites are not a clear win (synthetic, matched)

## Statement

Interior-only, selected alpha=0.01, 6 test scenarios, mean total RMSE (ug/m3) at 1/2/3/5/8 sensors. 5% noise: greedy_total 0.0223/0.0093/0.00525/0.00353/0.00272; greedy_attribution 0.0105/0.0065/0.00542/0.0037/0.00327; spread 0.0148/0.0117/0.0058/0.00512/0.00323; random-mean 0.025/0.0135/0.0103/0.00666/0.00328. 15% noise: greedy_total 0.0366/0.0174/0.0156/0.0108/0.00833; greedy_attribution 0.0625/0.0298/0.0212/0.0086/0.0079; spread 0.0285/0.0214/0.0158/0.0116/0.0082; random 0.0422/0.0227/0.0182/0.0128/0.00826. greedy_attribution is best at 1-2 sensors at 5% noise and worst at 1-3 sensors at 15%; no greedy method beats spread consistently at 1 sensor; random is worst at 5% for 1-5 sensors; reference sites: greedy_total never chose one, greedy_attribution chose 1-2 at budgets 5-8, spread with reference sites had higher total error than interior-only spread.

## Limitations

6 test scenarios x 3 noise draws; sd is a standard deviation; synthetic matched model on a numerically diffusive mesh; not field-validated
