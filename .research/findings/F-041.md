# F-041 · DYN-05 / P0 (design stage RUN-0078 with amended reference D-018; real-estimator check RUN-0080 on 96 fresh test episodes, seeds 8400-8495; synthetic; ridge-proxy empirical-risk design, exhaustive over 70 cells, 16 independent ensembles per N). How many independent design episodes N are needed depends strongly on budget and noise and is NOT reached for every cell by N=192. Registered criterion (90th percentile of relative regret on an independent 20000-episode reference <= 5%): noise 0.10, k=2: met from N=24 (p90 4.8%, 1.9% at N=48); noise 0.02, k=3: met from N=48 (all 16 ensembles recover the same layout at N=48/96/192) but N<=12 is catastrophic (p90 regret x29: ensembles that miss a wind choose layouts blind to it) and N=24 has p90 40%; NOT met at any N up to 192 for noise 0.02, k=2 (p90 10.5% at N=192, 2 distinct layouts chosen) nor noise 0.10, k=3 (p90 23-46%, 3 distinct layouts at N=192). So 'sufficient N' is not a single number: N=6 (the size used in EXP-013/014/020/026) is insufficient everywhere (regret p90 14%-29x), with a wind-coverage failure mode at small N. Surrogate-vs-actual disagreement is large: evaluated with the real nonnegative ridge estimator, the layouts chosen by N=192 ensembles at k=2 are 11-16% WORSE (mean over ensembles; ~25-50% of ensembles >10% worse) than the 20000-episode reference layout, while at noise 0.10, k=3 the ensemble layouts are 10-16% BETTER than the surrogate-reference layout, i.e. the unconstrained-ridge design objective is not a reliable proxy for the constrained estimator's risk. The first design run (RUN-0077) used a selection-biased reference (two independent 4000-episode references disagreed by 15% and picked different layouts); that diagnostic is itself the Q-013 effect and stays registered. Caveats: no confidence intervals yet on the real-estimator comparison (96 paired episodes; ensemble layouts that coincide are not independent); one population fixture and source geometry; budgets k=2,3 only; wind assumed known; preliminary, not independently reviewed. Link: Q-013 / RQ-D5 (Q-020).

## Statement

DYN-05 / P0 (design stage RUN-0078 with amended reference D-018; real-estimator check RUN-0080 on 96 fresh test episodes, seeds 8400-8495; synthetic; ridge-proxy empirical-risk design, exhaustive over 70 cells, 16 independent ensembles per N). How many independent design episodes N are needed depends strongly on budget and noise and is NOT reached for every cell by N=192. Registered criterion (90th percentile of relative regret on an independent 20000-episode reference <= 5%): noise 0.10, k=2: met from N=24 (p90 4.8%, 1.9% at N=48); noise 0.02, k=3: met from N=48 (all 16 ensembles recover the same layout at N=48/96/192) but N<=12 is catastrophic (p90 regret x29: ensembles that miss a wind choose layouts blind to it) and N=24 has p90 40%; NOT met at any N up to 192 for noise 0.02, k=2 (p90 10.5% at N=192, 2 distinct layouts chosen) nor noise 0.10, k=3 (p90 23-46%, 3 distinct layouts at N=192). So 'sufficient N' is not a single number: N=6 (the size used in EXP-013/014/020/026) is insufficient everywhere (regret p90 14%-29x), with a wind-coverage failure mode at small N. Surrogate-vs-actual disagreement is large: evaluated with the real nonnegative ridge estimator, the layouts chosen by N=192 ensembles at k=2 are 11-16% WORSE (mean over ensembles; ~25-50% of ensembles >10% worse) than the 20000-episode reference layout, while at noise 0.10, k=3 the ensemble layouts are 10-16% BETTER than the surrogate-reference layout, i.e. the unconstrained-ridge design objective is not a reliable proxy for the constrained estimator's risk. The first design run (RUN-0077) used a selection-biased reference (two independent 4000-episode references disagreed by 15% and picked different layouts); that diagnostic is itself the Q-013 effect and stays registered. Caveats: no confidence intervals yet on the real-estimator comparison (96 paired episodes; ensemble layouts that coincide are not independent); one population fixture and source geometry; budgets k=2,3 only; wind assumed known; preliminary, not independently reviewed. Link: Q-013 / RQ-D5 (Q-020).
