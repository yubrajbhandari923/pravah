# F-070 · EXP-026 (RUN-0098): no robust reduction of population-weighted RMSE from population-weighted layouts; result depends on optimiser and is low-powered (replaces F-066)

## Statement

Corrects F-066 after F-066/R1 (all counts reproduce). EXP-026 / RUN-0098: 12 held-out synthetic scenarios, fixture draws 6501-6506, noise 0.05 and 0.15 both reported, 500 m/dt100 provisional, D-027/D-028. Registered criterion: case-bootstrap CI excludes 0 AND >=9/12 cases agree; diff = objective layout minus area layout (positive = worse); pooled % = sum of mean differences / sum of area-layout RMSE over the 4 concentrated fixtures. H1 (population weighting lowers pop-weighted test RMSE on concentrated fixtures): NOT RESOLVED and OPTIMISER-DEPENDENT. The primary comparison uses exact/best-found layouts: 0 of 200 cells beneficial, 5 harmful, but those 5 are only 2 distinct layout comparisons (three identical difference vectors at mixed_rural_urban noise 0.15 budget 3: +0.00578, CI [0.00183,0.00996], 75% agree, +49% of 0.01169; separated_settlements noise 0.05 budget 4 mixed_0.25 +0.000901 [0.000104,0.001787] 83% and mixed_0.5 exactly 9/12 with the run's two-way interval including 0) at cells where the single shared area reference layout ranks 2nd-3rd of ~50 test-evaluated layouts; they are not evidence of a systematic penalty. The registered EXP-016 H1 wording names GREEDY layouts; under greedy-vs-greedy the same criterion gives 24 beneficial and 5 harmful cells of 200 (126 negative, 48 positive, 26 zero; pooled population-objective change 0.05: -18.1/+2.2/-6.0/-3.7/-10.5%, 0.15: +9.5/-1.5/-13.8/-11.2/-13.6% at budgets 2/3/4/5/8). Best-found-based pooled changes: 0.05 +7.2/+17.5/+7.6/-3.0/-2.3%; 0.15 -3.0/+13.2/+9.6/0.0/+1.1%, driven by 1-2 fixtures (e.g. +13.2% at 0.15 budget 3 is one fixture, median -0.8%); the expected largest benefit at small budgets is not observed. POWER: with 12 cases the design detects only ~33% (48-55% at budgets 2-3) of area-layout RMSE; benefits of >=20% are not excluded in 15 of 35 non-identical population-objective cells: this is absence of evidence, not evidence of no benefit. H2: area RMSE rises in direction (+14/+35/+21/+21/+34% at 0.05), resolved harmful in 9/200 cells; the rise is not concentrated in the lowest-population quartile (13 of 35 cells); pop and area RMSE move together weakly (Pearson r=0.79 on absolute differences, outlier-driven; Spearman 0.17): 19 both worse, 13 trade-off, 3 both better. H3: attribution error worse in 27 of 40 population-objective cells, resolved harmful in 20 of 200, beneficial in 4: not resolved, harm tendency. H4: not resolved; premise untestable (no resolved matched benefit; matched paired difference negative in 16 of 40 cells, none resolved). H5: not resolved (F-071). H6: effect exceeds the IQR of per-draw means in the 5 harmful cells (all six per-draw means positive in each) but the draw spread excludes design-sample noise (all draws share the same 6 design cases), so H6 is not resolved. Uniform-fixture control reproduced the area layout, but uniform and area weights are equal up to rounding, so it is weak by construction. Evaluators verified against design.score_layout/greedy_trace within the run's 1e-8 tolerance.

## Limitations

Synthetic; modelled WorldPop; 500 m provisional; 12 test cases; one area reference layout shared by all cells; D-028 deviation (Phase 2 regression check skipped, a pure stop gate that cannot change outputs; an equivalent check at the Phase 2 sigma 0.015370 was possible and not run); the run's own two-way interval differs from the registered case-only interval; registered random/spread baselines appear only as unlabeled layouts; provenance.json lacks git revision; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
