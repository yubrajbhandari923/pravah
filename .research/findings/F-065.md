# F-065 · DYN-03 v2 (RUN-0094): causal filter vs causal static fit; interpretation corrected after review F-064/R1

## Statement

Corrects F-064 after F-064/R1 (all numbers, seeds 8600-8695 and causality independently verified; see F-064 for the full table of contrasts: CAUSAL vs CT -57%/-54% (4 sensors, 2%/10%), -79%/-77% (9 sensors); RETRO vs CAUSAL -56/-59% and -33/-37%; M1 vs CAUSAL -48/-49% and -33/-36%; all 12 primary contrasts meet the effect criterion at family alpha 0.0125 and also at 0.05/12; change detection CT misses 61% (4 sensors) / 52-53% (9), CAUSAL 9.7% / 3.2%, RETRO and M1 1.6-8%). WHAT 'CT' IS: in v2, CT is not a current-time-only estimator: its prior is the causal running static (M0) fit to all data up to bin k, and at the frozen alpha (1e9 in L4; 3e4-1e6 in L9) CT equals that running static fit (L4: max|CT-prior|/REF 4e-6..4e-4). CAUSAL-CT therefore compares a time-varying model with a time-invariant model on the same causal data; it does not measure the value of history per se. WHERE THE GAIN COMES FROM: at 4 sensors the CAUSAL-CT gain on constant-emission episodes is ~0 (-0.00002), and CAUSAL is worse than CT on activation episodes (+0.00124); the gain comes from time-varying emission families, i.e. from tracking emission changes. The plume-carry-over mechanism (D-024's rationale for retiring the control) was not tested and is not supported by this run. WIND-VARIATION PREDICTION (registered relative form, D-024): holds in direction in all 8 steady-vs-variable comparisons (CAUSAL-CT, RETRO-CAUSAL; e.g. L4 2% CAUSAL-CT -49% steady vs -62% variable; RETRO-CAUSAL -53% vs -59%) but every episode-bootstrap interval for rel(variable)-rel(steady) includes 0 (e.g. L4 2% CAUSAL-CT -0.125 [-0.368,+0.110]), heterogeneity between steady_a (-71%) and steady_b (-28%) exceeds the steady/variable gap, and the prediction was formulated after it was seen on RUN-0092: undetermined, not confirmatory. NEGATIVE CONTROL: the D-014 steady-wind control (retired by D-024 after it fired on RUN-0092, before this run) would fire again: all 8 steady-group CAUSAL-CT/RETRO-CAUSAL intervals exclude 0; under D-014 this run would be invalid. GENERALISATION ACROSS WINDS (D-024 item 6; reviewer computation, t-interval over six wind means, alpha 0.0125, 5 dof): the M1-CAUSAL gain at 4 sensors does not generalise (2%: [-0.00626,+0.00049]; 10%: [-0.00702,+0.00062]); all other cells exclude 0 (L4 RETRO-CAUSAL only narrowly). EDGE SELECTIONS: 13 flags; CT alpha 1e9 in L4 (validation flat, CT equals the static fit); CAUSAL/RETRO alpha 0.01 in all 4 cells (flat plateau 0.01-1); CAUSAL/RETRO beta 10 in L9|10% (interior); M0 alpha 0.01 in 3 of 4 cells and 10 in L9|2% (M0 validation scores not saved). Seeds 8600-8695 were verified by regenerating truths (floor J_pop matches ~1e-15); the stale '7600-7695' strings in dynamic_history.py were corrected in code (the stored RUN-0094 summary.json is left unchanged and still prints the stale note).

## Limitations

v2 tune was not a registered run (no RUN record between D-024 and 05049a5; reviewer recomputed 4 validation scores and they match); bootstrap unit is the episode and ignores wind blocking; CT detection delay is conditional on detection (not comparable to CAUSAL); RETRO/M1 'misses' not separated into crossed-before-c vs never-crossed; CT/CAUSAL causality tests strengthened (every-bin test added) but the run_episode prior wiring is only covered indirectly; matched model, known wind, one fixture; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
