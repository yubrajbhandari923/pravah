# F-073 · EXP-027b (EXP-037; RUN-0096 stage 1, RUN-0100 stage 2): registered outcome INADEQUATE for the 500 m / dt 100 mesh (2 of 4 cells), falsification gate passed

## Statement

EXP-037 / EXP-027b, protocol docs/exp027b_design.md section 3 (margin 0.05 approved 2026-10-15; fresh seeds 1201-1203 / noise 4400+; 3 draws x 24 cases = 72 cases, each simulated once per mesh at 250 m/dt50 (reference), 500 m/dt100, 2 km/dt400 (control); cells noise {0.05,0.15} x budget {3,5}; greedy_total; menu of up to 36 candidate layouts per cell scored case by case on every mesh; shape error E = median over the menu of the relative change of the scale-free objective; 95% intervals by case bootstrap, 400 resamples). STAGE 2 (all 72 cases, the registered final evaluation): (G) FALSIFIABILITY GATE PASSED: the 2 km control has E_lo95 > 0.05 in 4 of 4 cells (E 0.443 [0.268,0.615], 0.379 [0.182,0.472], 0.234 [0.128,0.272], 0.115 [0.075,0.192]); the control's rho with the reference is -0.15 to 0.48. (A) 500 m: INADEQUATE by the registered rule (E_lo95 > 0.05 in at least one cell): noise 0.05 budget 3 E = 0.0873 [0.0595, 0.1881]; noise 0.05 budget 5 E = 0.0919 [0.0516, 0.1948] (lower bound exceeds the margin by only 0.0016); the other two cells are INCONCLUSIVE: noise 0.15 budget 3 E = 0.0765 [0.0336, 0.1025], noise 0.15 budget 5 E = 0.0284 [0.0262, 0.0723]. No cell is ADEQUATE (E_hi95 <= 0.05 in 0 of 4). Descriptive (not decisive): Spearman rho of 500 m vs 250 m layout scores 0.94-0.96 in all four cells, i.e. 500 m preserves the ranking of candidate layouts well even though its shape error exceeds 5%; decision regret R_hi95 0.12-0.32 (R point estimate 0 to 0.03); the 500 m median J ratio vs 250 m is 0.85-0.93 (500 m reports 7-15% lower objective). STAGE 1 (48 cases, draws 1-2) gave: gate passed in 3 of 4 cells (the 0.15/5 control E_lo95 = 0.0423 < 0.05); main E 0.084/0.047/0.050/0.017 with E_hi95 0.156/0.109/0.097/0.045, i.e. INCONCLUSIVE with one cell ADEQUATE; stage 2 was run because the gate passed, as registered, and the verdict changes from INCONCLUSIVE to INADEQUATE with more cases. INTERPRETATION: with the registered 5% shape-error margin the 500 m / dt 100 mesh is not adequate in this synthetic Belauri domain at the low-noise cells; this is consistent with the known numerical diffusion (K_eff/K 2.4-9.4) and with the mesh250 model-error ratios of F-069; the layout ranking is nevertheless strongly preserved (rho >= 0.94), so the result is about objective shape, not about which layouts are best. 500 m / dt 100 remains PROVISIONAL at best and every Belauri finding must carry that qualifier. Replaces the EXP-027 void result as the mesh evidence (F-057).

## Limitations

Synthetic; 72 cases; margin 0.05 was fixed after pilots (disclosed) and the lower bound in one INADEQUATE cell is only 0.0016 above it; menu is greedy-derived layouts only; no 1 km control; the 2 km control uses a control-only origin tolerance (D-016); padded domain; E is an unrelated scale-free shape statistic, not a decision-quality measure; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
