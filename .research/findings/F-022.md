# F-022 · Belauri field-level convergence: 500 m exponential has 5.0% space-time L2 error vs 125 m, mesh error dominates time-step error, external-patch fingerprint least resolved

## Statement

At dt 25 s the 500 m exponential field has 5.0% relative space-time L2 error vs the 125 m solution (250 m: 1.5%), observed spatial order ~1.4-1.7; at 500 m the time-step self-error (dt 100 vs 12.5) is 0.6%, far below mesh error; K_eff/K is 6.3 / 3.5 / 2.3 at 500 / 250 / 125 m; external-patch fingerprint is the worst resolved (L2 9.8% at 500 m, 2.9% at 250 m); VanLeer at 500 m is about as accurate as exponential at 250 m but nonlinear (synthetic emissions and wind, one case)

## Limitations

One draw (d0), one wind segment; references are themselves discretised (two 125 m references differ by 0.8%, so errors below ~1% are unresolved); vanleer orders and Richardson estimate are not asymptotic and not interpreted; arrival time is local-source driven and uninformative about external transport; in-study fraction of each source depends on mesh (road 0.949-0.986, builtup 0.977-0.995) so the local/external label shifts a few percent with mesh; no sensor-design conclusion yet
