# F-062 · DYN-05 sufficiency (RUN-0078/0080), conclusions corrected after review F-058/R1

## Statement

Corrects F-058 after F-058/R1 (surrogate p90s, RUN-0077/78 flip, x29 cases, bootstrap CIs reproduce; no leakage). Disclosures as in F-058: D-018 written after RUN-0077 summary and flipped 0.10/k=2; RUN-0079 key collision re-run as RUN-0080 (seeds 8400-8495). SURROGATE: registered p90 relative regret <=5% on reference B met from N=24 (0.10, k=2: 4.8%) and N=48 (0.02, k=3); not met to N=192 for 0.02/k=2 (10.5%) and 0.10/k=3 (p90 23-47% for N>=24, not monotone: 23% at N=96, 46% at N=192; 66-131% for N<=12); N<=12 at 0.02/k=3 gives relative regret up to 29 (an objective ~30x optimum) when an unstratified ensemble misses steady_b. H4 (more design episodes stabilise J_pop): supported for the surrogate in 2 of 4 cells, not in 0.02/k=2 or 0.10/k=3. REAL ESTIMATOR (descriptive, not the registered criterion): ensemble layouts at k=2 are worse than S*_A at every N to 192 (N=6 +29%/+34%, N=192 +14%/+16%); the cell the surrogate certifies (0.10, k=2 from N=24) costs +28% [8,54]; N=6 vs N=192 two-stage CIs include 0 in all cells (0.02/k=2 +13% [-7,52]; 0.10/k=2 +15% [-1,37]; 0.02/k=3 +9% [-0.2,22]; 0.10/k=3 +3% [-10,17]), so the real estimator does NOT show that more design episodes help; the persistent k=2 gap reflects surrogate-vs-estimator mismatch among layouts the surrogate treats as near-equivalent. Reference quality: post-hoc split-half on test episodes: S*_A is +31% [5,71] worse than the best evaluated layout at 0.10/k=3 (S*_B 22% better than S*_A, -22% [-34,-9]); at 0.02/k=3 unresolved (+21.5% [-5,+59] with the cell's own 9 layouts, +3.5% [-20,+38] with all 22 k=3 layouts). So regret vs S*_A cannot certify sufficiency at 0.10/k=3; an unconstrained-ridge surrogate cannot certify sufficiency for the constrained estimator. Not claimed: EXP-013/014/020/026 failed the same way.

## Limitations

One fixture, k=2,3 only, wind known, post-hoc split-half, closed-form risk not independently re-checked against Monte Carlo, EXP-033 card says R=8 while D-017 registered R=16; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
