# F-052 · DYN-03 confirmatory (EXP-031, RUN-0092; 96 synthetic test episodes, seeds 7600-7695, 6 winds; frozen hash be1de01dd5250121 tuned on validation seeds on the cluster RUN-0084 with the converged solver; 0 non-converged fits; wind known and exact; estimators: CT = current-bin data and current-bin columns only (earlier emissions ignored), CAUSAL = filter using data up to the end of bin b keeping bin b, RETRO = full-window smoother M2, M1 = full-history independent bins; prior = static M0 fit). Using history helps a lot: the causal filter lowers sqrt(J_pop) relative to the current-time-only estimator by 56% / 52% (4 sensors, 2% / 10% noise; -0.00695 [-0.00910,-0.00482] and -0.00652 [-0.00865,-0.00449] ug/m3) and 80% / 77% (9 sensors); retrospective smoothing (which also uses FUTURE data) improves on the causal filter by a further 55% / 58% (4 sensors) and 32% / 36% (9 sensors) (-0.00305 [-0.00446,-0.00194], -0.00346 [-0.00483,-0.00237], -0.00061 [-0.00087,-0.00041], -0.00079 [-0.00104,-0.00059]); the full-history independent-bin M1 beats the causal filter by 43% / 45% (4 sensors) and 31% / 35% (9 sensors). All 12 primary contrasts meet the registered criterion (CI excludes 0 at Bonferroni 0.05/4, >=10% relative gain, sign persists leaving each wind out). Change detection (episodes with a step in an identifiable parameter, n=58; detected when the estimate crosses halfway between the pre- and post-levels): CT misses 41-55% of changes; the causal filter misses 2-5% and, at 4 sensors, detects with a mean delay of 0.48 bins (about 29 min on 1 h bins) vs 0.32 for the smoother (the smoother's advantage is partly hindsight). IMPORTANT, a registered prediction is CONTRADICTED: the registered negative control (steady winds, where history should help least; 'history helps most where the wind varies') fails: the gain of history over CT is as large or larger in absolute terms on the two steady winds as on the four variable winds (4 sensors: CAUSAL-CT -0.0076 steady vs -0.0066 variable at 2% noise; RETRO-CAUSAL -0.0044 vs -0.0024), and all 8 steady-group intervals exclude zero. By the registered rule this stops the interpretation that wind variation is the reason history helps: the gain arises from plume carry-over (earlier emissions are still present in later observations), not from wind-direction variation, and the negative control as designed could not isolate wind-driven de-confounding (that hypothesis is NOT tested here and is NOT supported). Further caveats: CT is a deliberately naive baseline (its tuned alpha sits at 1e5-1e6, i.e. it collapses to the static prior), so the CT contrast mostly measures the cost of ignoring carry-over; 11 of 16 frozen hyperparameters sit on a grid bound (alpha at the 0.01 lower bound, CT at the upper range); the causal filter is evaluated on bin-b estimates only (no smoothing of earlier bins); one geometry/population fixture; matched model; preliminary, not independently reviewed.
