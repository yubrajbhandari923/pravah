## 2. Methods read for the "what is established" statements
SRC-003 Tibshirani, Saunders, Rosset, Zhu, Knight 2005 (marked examined; Stanford SOL copy, Secs 1-3, 6). Fused lasso: min ||y - X beta||^2 s.t. sum|beta_j| <= s1 and sum|beta_j - beta_{j-1}| <= s2, i.e. L1 penalty on coefficients and on successive differences, for ordered features; solved as a QP; solutions have at most N blocks of equal non-zero coefficients (Theorem 2, under non-redundancy). With X = I it is the fused lasso signal approximator = 1D total-variation denoising. Nonnegativity is NOT part of the method (the beta+/beta- split is a computational device). Established: TV penalty on an ordered coefficient profile with a general design matrix X (so a PDE response operator is a legitimate X). Adding beta >= 0 is a standard convex constraint; not a new method.
SRC-004 Adams & MacKay 2007 (marked examined; arXiv 0710.3742 Secs 1-2.4). BOCPD: observations in a segment are iid from P(x | eta); eta independent across segments; exact recursion on the run-length posterior with a hazard function H(tau); conjugate-exponential predictive; cost linear in data seen so far (truncatable); causal/online. It detects changes in the generative parameters of the observation sequence; it does not invert a transport operator or attribute a change to local vs boundary sources. Relevance to PRAVAH: a possible alternative for flagging change times, not for attribution.
SRC-002 Rauch, Tung, Striebel 1965: original paper paywalled (AIAA J 3(8):1445-1450). Read: the published abstract and secondary derivations (Sarkka lecture handout 7; Willsky 1976 fixed-interval smoothing text; an arXiv RTS paper). NOT marked examined (primary method text not read). Established statement from those sources: for a linear-Gaussian state-space model the RTS forward Kalman pass plus backward recursion gives the fixed-interval smoothed mean and covariance, which equal the MAP / linear least-squares estimate of the whole state trajectory given all data in the interval (Willsky: the smoothed estimate is a linear functional of the observations and is also the MAP estimate). Relation to our estimator: an unconstrained, quadratic-penalty (ridge / random-walk-prior) batch inversion is the batch form of the same estimator; RTS is the O(T) way to compute it. Our TV + nonnegative estimator is an L1, inequality-constrained problem; it is not a Gaussian state-space smoother, so RTS covariances do not apply to it, and RTS cannot represent piecewise-constant jumps without extra machinery. Caveat: the "equals batch MAP" statement is textbook and was taken from the secondary sources, not the 1965 paper.

## 4. Candidate contributions

C1 TV nonnegative batch inversion of local + boundary histories
Closest prior: SRC-003 (TV on ordered coefficients with a general design matrix, established); SRC-014 (nonnegative source-by-temporal-basis coefficients for wind-conditioned lagged transport, ridge/weak prior; a low-dimensional background nuisance space; synthetic + New Delhi PM2.5); SRC-010 (4D-Var estimating time-varying emission-rate correction factors jointly with initial state, so time-varying emission inversion is old); SRC-011 (Kalman-smoother observability of joint states and emissions); SRC-002 (batch smoothing = Gaussian MAP).
Established: TV/fused-lasso penalty; nonnegativity-constrained source inversion; time-varying emission estimation in variational/smoother frameworks; Gaussian batch smoothing.
Different: TV + nonnegativity applied to emission AND boundary-inflow histories jointly through one transport operator for PM2.5 in a valley; internal result F-037 was superseded (F-039), M2 not uniformly better, and F-049 shows TV does not buy robustness to model error.
Verdict: ESTABLISHED for each ingredient; PLAUSIBLY NEW AS A COMBINATION only (weak; a methods paper would not rest on it). Not a stand-alone contribution.

C2 Population-weighted vs source-identification dynamic placement conflict
Closest prior: SRC-013 (variational network design using the sensitivity matrix between emissions and observations, explicitly weighted by population density and emission fluxes, singular-value metrics; static/averaged emissions, zero boundary conditions); SRC-007/008/009 (goal-oriented OED: any linear QoI incl. exposure); SRC-018 and Haskell-Craig 2025 (placement for equity/coverage, empirical); Araki 2015 and Ajnoti 2024 (CTM-based network optimisation; title only); Keats 2010 (information-driven receptor placement for source determination).
Established: population/emission weighting in design objectives; goal-oriented vs parameter-oriented designs differ in principle.
Different: dynamic (time-varying wind and emissions) setting and a quantified trade-off between the two objectives for one valley.
Verdict: method ESTABLISHED (SRC-013 and the goal-oriented OED papers); the quantified conflict is PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT only if the conflict exceeds between-scenario spread (T-073 experiment; not yet shown here). Say "applied to Kathmandu", not "new method".

C3 Local-vs-transboundary separation limited by wind-and-layout geometry (F-044)
Closest prior: SRC-014 (identifiability governed by the projected lagged response matrix; rank, singular values, pairwise coherence; wind diversity improves conditioning; background projected out; sparse sensors; New Delhi); SRC-015 (boundary-condition bias as an irreducible uncertainty; method "limited ... unless additional observations can be used to constrain the boundary inflow"); SRC-011 (observability of joint states and emissions with Dirichlet lateral boundaries); SRC-010 (upwind emission optimisation is essential); SRC-016 (transboundary influence on Kathmandu shown by back-trajectory statistics, not by inversion); Nickless 2019 (boundary concentrations as inversion inputs from a background station); Lauvaux 2012 network design.
Established: that background/inflow and local sources are confounded when sensors and wind geometry are poor; that identifiability can be reported as a matrix property; that wind diversity helps.
Different: an explicit boundary-inflow HISTORY treated as an unknown alongside local emission histories, with misattribution rates versus a distinguishability measure d, number of sensors and wind regime (F-044: attribution reliable from d about 8 with 9 sensors, still 17-26% misattributed at d=16 with 4 sensors for S1/S4; boundary-reference sensor does not help by more than 5 points: P4 holds). SRC-014 treats background as a low-dimensional nuisance, not a transboundary source history.
Verdict: PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT (quantified misattribution curves). The qualitative statement (wind diversity and sensor geometry limit separability) is ESTABLISHED by SRC-014/015. SRC-014 is a recent preprint: must be cited and compared against before claiming anything.

C4 Design-ensemble sufficiency incl. proxy-vs-real-estimator disagreement (F-046)
Closest prior: SRC-017 (SAA for stochastic optimisation: sample-size convergence, confidence intervals; generic theory); SRC-006 (rSAA with convergence bound, internal T-073); Alexanderian 2021 and Huan et al. 2024 (OED reviews: SAA/expected utility estimation); Catanach & Das 2023 and SRC-012 (OED under model misspecification/model uncertainty; abstracts only).
Established: SAA error decreases with sample size; designs from small ensembles are noisy; surrogate objectives can be misspecified.
Different: an empirical regret-vs-N study for this estimator, and the finding that an unconstrained-ridge surrogate criterion and the real nonnegative/TV estimator disagree on where the design is stable (surrogate: 6 episodes insufficient everywhere; real estimator: insufficient in 3 of 4 cells).
Verdict: ESTABLISHED in principle (SAA sufficiency); PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT for the surrogate-vs-real disagreement. I did not find the specific claim, but I did not read the OED reviews in full.

C5 Void / under-powered mesh-stability tests (F-048) and F-049 (model error dominates sensor count)
Closest prior (mesh): no direct hit in the search. SRC-001 Table 1 (T-073) is MSE vs basis size, not layout. SRC-010 states the need for finer model resolution for NOx use (54 km grid), a resolution caveat on inversion skill, not on design.
Closest prior (model error): SRC-012 (weak-constraint 4D-Var design under model error, Gaussian linear; abstract only); Catanach & Das 2023; SRC-014 (structural operator mismatch drives coefficient error to 0.86 in its test; 100% rejection of adequacy test; 32-sensor Delhi network). Established qualitatively: design/inversion quality degrades under model misspecification.
Different: (a) F-048 is a negative methodological finding: a 2 km falsification control passes the test, so the mesh-stability test is non-falsifiable at 6 cases per ensemble and layouts move 3-4 km between independent draws. This is not a mesh-adequacy result. (b) F-049: error grows monotonically with wind rotation; 9-sensor layout is MORE sensitive to model error (x7.3 vs x3.7 at 20 degrees) and 4-sensor M2 advantage over M1 vanishes (-20% to -3%).
Verdict: mesh sensitivity of design decisions: UNCERTAIN (no prior found; search short; internal evidence is void). F-049: PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT (a quantified wind-error-versus-sensor-count curve in this setting) but the principle that model error limits what sensors can buy is ESTABLISHED in spirit. Both are synthetic and preliminary.

## 6. Genuinely novel vs not (conservative)
NOT novel (established): TV/fused-lasso penalty; nonnegative source inversion; batch smoothing equivalence to MAP; time-varying emission-rate estimation; goal-oriented/population-weighted network design (SRC-013, SRC-007/008/009); identifiability-via-response-matrix with wind diversity (SRC-014); boundary-bias as a limit on regional inversions (SRC-015); generic SAA convergence (SRC-017); the general idea that model error limits what extra sensors buy.
Not established as novel but plausibly new, pending an exhaustive search and review: (1) joint TV-nonnegative inversion of local and boundary-inflow histories as a combination (C1, weak); (2) quantified local-vs-transboundary misattribution curves versus distinguishability, sensor count and wind (C3/F-044, strongest candidate); (3) the surrogate-versus-real-estimator disagreement on design-ensemble sufficiency (C4/F-046); (4) the quantified wind-error-versus-sensor-count result (F-049, preliminary); (5) the quantified population-versus-source-ID conflict (C2, if shown). The F-048 void result is a methodological warning about test power, not a scientific finding.
Nothing here is CONTRADICTED BY PRIOR ART. Summary of verdict labels: C1 PLAUSIBLY NEW AS A COMBINATION (weak); C2 method ESTABLISHED, conflict PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT; C3 PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT; C4 ESTABLISHED in principle / PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT for proxy-vs-real; C5 mesh UNCERTAIN, F-049 PLAUSIBLY NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT.
