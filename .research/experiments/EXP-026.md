# EXP-026 · EXP-016v population-aware sensor placement at Belauri scale, with the WorldPop fixture and replicated fixture draws (pre-registered variant)

## Hypothesis

Inherits EXP-016 H1-H4 unchanged and adds: H5 (real-population) the direction and sign of the population-vs-area trade-off measured on the synthetic concentrated fixtures also holds on the WorldPop Belauri field, whose spatial structure was not designed by us; H5-null is a valid and interesting result because WorldPop is a modelled 2025 constrained estimate, not a census observation. H6 (replicate stability) the objective effect at budgets 2-4 exceeds the spread across 6 independent fixture draws; EXP-020 and F-030 found greedy path-dependence and design-sample noise of the same size as the objective differences at small budgets on the toy domain, so without replicates a Belauri-scale effect at budget 2-3 would not be distinguishable from draw noise.

## Motivation

Closes a registration gap. T-037 delivered the WorldPop Belauri extract and said it should feed an EXP-016 variant, but no variant was ever registered, so configs/population_belauri.json implements three additions that are absent from EXP-016's registered parameters. Registering them BEFORE the production run, with no test results yet seen, so the pre-registration genuinely precedes exposure. EXP-016's own parameter set stays untouched as the historical record.

## Method

Gate: use the mesh/dt configuration judged adequate by EXP-015 (default 500 m, dt 100 if EXP-015 shows decisions stable relative to resampling, else the finer one). Region Belauri; population fixtures from src/pravah/population.py (people per cell inside the study mask, fixed totals, seeds declared), 5 fixtures. Objectives (all MSE-based so a convex mix is itself one weighted MSE, units (ug/m3)^2 with no ad-hoc scale): area w=A_i*study_i/sum; population w=N_i/sum N; mixed w=(1-beta)w_area+beta w_pop with beta in {0.25,0.5,0.75}; capped population w=min(N_i, c*mean_N_in_study)/sum with c=3 (equity proxy, exposes a fraction of cells; not a measure of personal exposure). Baselines: random (n=20) and spread, same budgets 2,3,5,8 (candidate set and noise reps identical, Phase 2 ensembles seeds 101/202 for design/validation; alpha frozen at the Phase 2 value 0.01 for all objectives, with a validation-selected-alpha sensitivity rerun clearly labelled secondary). Test: 12 fresh scenarios (seed 305, noise seed 3500) disjoint from design/validation, never used for any choice. Evaluate every layout with every metric: pop-weighted RMSE, area RMSE, RMSE in the lowest-population quartile of study cells (the neglected-region error), worst-cell RMSE, held-out-sensor RMSE (block and random), local/external attribution error. Cross-fixture matrix: layouts designed on fixture X evaluated on fixture Y (overfitting check). Pareto: beta sweep 0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1 plotting test pop-RMSE vs area RMSE.

## Expected outcome

Layouts move toward settlements; pop-RMSE falls most for concentrated fixtures at small budgets; area and low-population RMSE rise; differences shrink as budget grows.

## Success criteria

Inherits EXP-016's criteria unchanged: paired per-case differences (objective minus area baseline) with a percentile bootstrap interval over the 12 held-out test cases; an effect is resolved only if the interval excludes 0 for pop-weighted RMSE AND the same direction holds in >=75% of cases; cost reported as the area and lowest-population-quartile RMSE increase plus the attribution change. ADDED for this variant: (a) the uniform-fixture control must reproduce the area layout exactly, or stop and report a bug; (b) at budgets 2-4 an objective effect is reported as resolved only if it is larger than the inter-draw spread across the 6 fixture draws, reported as median and IQR; (c) the WorldPop fixture is reported SEPARATELY from the synthetic fixtures and never pooled with them, because its population total (184030) and spatial structure differ from the synthetic fixtures (25000); (d) cross-fixture generalisation (layouts designed on X evaluated on Y) must be reported for worldpop_real in both directions. beta, cap_c, alpha, budgets, fixtures, seeds and the bootstrap settings are frozen as registered here and must not be reselected after test results are opened.

## Stop conditions

All EXP-016 stop conditions apply. Additionally: stop if the uniform-fixture layouts differ from the area layouts; stop if population weights are zero across a whole candidate neighbourhood; stop if the WorldPop array totals differ between the 500 m and 250 m levels (they must both be 184030). Do not reselect beta, cap_c, alpha, fixtures, draws or budgets after seeing test results.
