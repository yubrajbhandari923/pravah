# EXP-018 · EXP toy prescribed spatiotemporal winds: ridge deflection and delayed reversal (pre-registered)

## Hypothesis

A prescribed upstream flow reversal (idealised ridge, labelled prescribed, NOT terrain-derived) returns an external plume to the study region after a delay that grows with reversal time and outer-domain size; a too-small outer domain truncates the return and underestimates the external contribution; source-contribution estimates depend on the assumed forcing model.

## Motivation

Q-C: does upstream flow structure matter for delayed arrival and attribution, with the cheapest credible model (existing piecewise-in-time stream-function winds, no new solver).
