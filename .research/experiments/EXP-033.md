Registered criterion (90th percentile of relative regret on an independent 20000-episode reference <= 5%): noise 0.10, k=2: met from N=24 (p90 4.8%, 1.9% at N=48); noise 0.02, k=3: met from N=48 (all 16 ensembles recover the same layout at N=48/96/192) but N<=12 is catastrophic (p90 regret x29: ensembles that miss a wind choose layouts blind to it) and N=24 has p90 40%; NOT met at any N up to 192 for noise 0.02, k=2 (p90 10.5% at N=192, 2 distinct layouts chosen) nor noise 0.10, k=3 (p90 23-46%, 3 distinct layouts at N=192).
