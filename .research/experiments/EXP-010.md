## Synthesis S1 — 2026-10-10 (agent: claude-code)

- **What happened:** RUN-0017 (26.5 min): all four sub-studies on domains A (20 km) and B (30 km). Scenario fields: study-region origin fractions local/external = 0.57/0.43 (explicit E1), 0.78/0.22 (uniform boundary input), 0.52/0.48 (mixed); no_arrival external-to-local ratio 4.1e-6 (verified by the forward model). Recovery from TOTAL observations, alpha selected on 4 validation cases (alpha=1 at both noise levels) then frozen, 6 test cases x 3 noise reps: with 2% noise the 8-interior layout recovers local/external emission amplitudes to 1.1%/0.6% (mean relative error) and the external concentration fraction to 0.0016; the 6-interior layout to 15%/0.8% and 0.015; 6 interior + 2 upwind reference sites 14%/0.4% and 0.015. At 10% noise: 8-interior 5.3%/2.8%, 6-interior 19%/3.9%, 6+2 upwind 24%/2.1%. Equal budget (8 sensors): interior8 beat interior+upwind on total RMSE (lower in 14 of 18 pairs at 2% noise), local RMSE (17 of 18) but interior+upwind was lower on external RMSE (10 of 18) and external amplitude error. Strong prior (alpha=1e5) destroys recovery (amplitude errors 0.7-0.99); alpha=0 is close to the selected alpha at 2% noise. Confounded case: column cosine 0.991 between L1 and the collinear external source, flags rank_deficient and confounded:L1,E1c, effective rank 2, condition 14.9; total-field error stays small relative to decomposition error. Boundary input vs a localised plume: omitting E1 gives 0.257 of the peak as RMSE, adding a uniform or four-sector boundary basis only 0.250 — whole-side boundary input cannot represent a localised plume. Domain A vs B: study-field difference 0.9% (external-only) and 3.3% (mixed) of peak.
- **What worked:** Identifiability flags fire for the confounded and no-arrival cases without a prior hiding them; noise is set relative to a documented signal scale; alpha frozen on validation before the test ensemble; raw rows saved
- **What did not work:** An upwind reference site at fixed budget is not a free win: it improves the boundary/external terms but costs interior coverage, so total and local error are larger than with eight interior sensors in this setup. Wind mismatch is not detected by any flag (see special/wind_mismatch.csv)
- **Interpretation:** In this idealised linear setting the local/external split is recoverable when the sensitivity matrix is well conditioned and fails (flagged) when plumes arrive late or are collinear; whole-boundary inflow bases are a poor stand-in for a distant localised source. None of this identifies real emitters.
- **Limitations:** Synthetic matched model (same solver/grid for truth and inference except the labelled special cases); 4 validation + 6 test cases and 3 noise draws; one demonstration geometry with the external source 1 km from the domain-A boundary; sd is a standard deviation; the confounded default needed a 5 m/s wind, 3.8 km upwind geometry and a dedicated layout
- **Recommended next:** Belauri geography forward runs and geographic design
