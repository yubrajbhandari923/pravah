# EXP-037 · EXP-027b mesh adequacy on common cases (same-ensemble shape error, 2 km falsification gate)

## Hypothesis

H1b: on identical case ensembles, the 500 m / dt 100 mesh changes the shape of the design objective over candidate layouts by no more than 5% (E_hi95 <= 0.05 in all 4 cells). H0b: it changes it by more than 5% in at least one cell (INADEQUATE) or the interval cannot exclude it (INCONCLUSIVE). Validity gate: the 2 km control must show E_lo95 > 0.05 in >=3 of 4 cells, else VOID.

## Motivation


## Method

docs/exp027b_design.md (commit f961713; seeds amended to 1201-1203). Meshes 250 m/dt50 (reference), 500 m/dt100, 2 km/dt400 (control); K=3 draws x N=24 cases, each simulated once per mesh; stage 1 = draws 1-2; stage 2 only if gate passes. Evaluation: scripts/exp027b.py evaluate.

## Expected outcome


## Success criteria

(G) gate: 2 km E_lo95>0.05 in >=3/4 cells; (A) ADEQUATE iff E_hi95<=0.05 in all 4 cells; INADEQUATE iff E_lo95>0.05 in any cell; else INCONCLUSIVE. Regret, Spearman, J ratio, displacement descriptive only.

## Stop conditions

Gate fails on stage-1 cases -> VOID, stage 2 not run, 500 m stays provisional. No criterion, margin, menu rule or cell changes after stage-1 evaluation.
