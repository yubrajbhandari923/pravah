# EXP-019 · EXP toy wind-error response and three inference strategies (pre-registered)

## Hypothesis

H1: source/reconstruction error rises monotonically with |wind direction error| and speed error; H2: per unit vector error direction error is more damaging than speed error; H3: Strategy C (discrete wind-ensemble marginalisation, prior range declared a priori, no access to the true wind) beats B (fixed wrong wind) inside the prior range and loses its advantage or becomes overconfident outside it; H4: chi-square per dof flags the wrong wind when noise is known, rank/identifiability flags do not; H5: external attribution degrades more than local under wind error.

## Motivation

F-021: hidden wind error dominates; identifiability flags cannot show it. Quantify the error-response curve and test whether an uncertain-wind estimator helps out of sample.

## Method

Synthetic Domain, 3 sources, fixed layouts (greedy under nominal wind, random, spread) at budgets 3,5,8. Truth wind direction error in {0,+-5,+-10,+-15,+-30} deg and relative speed error {-30,-15,0,+15,+30}% (grid fixed after a labelled pilot), paired cases and shared noise per truth. A known wind; B nominal wind; C ensemble of M winds (direction +-{0,10,20} deg x speed x{0.8,1,1.2}), per-member regularised NNLS, weights proportional to exp(-chi2/2) with uniform prior, prediction = weighted mean, interval from weighted spread. Metrics: total RMSE, population-weighted RMSE, held-out-sensor RMSE, amplitude error, local/external attribution, bias, chi2/dof, 90% coverage, runtime. Prior range and regularisation fixed on validation scenarios only; test truths never seen.
