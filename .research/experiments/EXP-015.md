# EXP-015 · EXP convergence of sensor-design decisions with mesh, time step and scheme (pre-registered)

## Hypothesis

H1: mesh-induced displacement and regret of greedy_total layouts (500 m, dt 100 -> 250 m, dt 50) are no larger than the displacement/regret caused by resampling the design scenarios on a fixed mesh (so layouts moved by sampling, not by numerics). H0: mesh-induced changes exceed the resampling baseline (decisions are numerically unstable at 500 m). H2: a flux-limited scheme (if T-026 verifies it) reduces the 500 m vs reference gap in field error and K_eff/K but not necessarily in decision regret. Either outcome is a valid finding.

## Motivation

EXP-014 found Jaccard 0-0.2 between 500 m and 250 m selections, but used 3 design cases at 250 m vs 6 at 500 m and had no stability baseline; F-016/F-018 show K_eff/K 2.4-9.4. Need to separate numerical instability from near-ties and sampling.

## Method

Stage 1 field verification (cheap): fingerprints/basis columns and K_eff at 500/250/125 m (dt fixed small) and dt 100/50/25 at 500 m; observed orders; mass closure; min concentration. Stage 2 decisions: same configs/design(seed101,6)/validation(202,6)/test(303,6 + 6 fresh 304 held for final) ensembles and noise seeds on every mesh; candidate sites fixed in UTM metres (lookup of cell per mesh, never cell indices); alpha FROZEN at Phase 2 results/geodesign/alpha_selected.json (0.01) for all meshes; budgets 3,5,8; greedy_total selection per config. Reference model = 250 m, dt 50 (125 m used only for stage-1 fields and 1 design case-set if budget allows). Metrics per config vs reference: exact overlap (Jaccard), mean/max nearest-site displacement via optimal assignment (metres), objective value on the reference design cases, regret = J_ref(layout_c) - J_ref(layout_ref), and test RMSE of layout_c evaluated on reference-mesh truths. Resampling baseline: R=8 replicate design ensembles (6 cases each, new case seeds 1101-1108, noise seeds disjoint) on the 500 m mesh, giving the distribution of the same displacement/regret between replicate layouts, and an epsilon-optimal set size (single-site swaps within one replicate-SD of the objective). Scheme axis (exponential vs vanleer) only if T-026 passes verification.

## Expected outcome

Field error halves-ish per refinement at order ~1.4; layouts at small budgets are near-ties so displacement can be large with small regret; regret decreasing with refinement.

## Success criteria

Report (no universal threshold): per budget, mesh-induced displacement and regret vs the resampling distribution (median, IQR over 8 replicates); stability claimed only if mesh-induced values lie within the replicate interquartile range AND decrease monotonically along 500->250; practical tolerance tau reported at 500 m and 1 km as fraction of sites matched within tau; objective-tie fraction. Tests ensembles and final figures frozen before opening test results.

## Stop conditions

Abort a config if one solve exceeds 3x its measured benchmark (T-025); drop 125 m design stage if the 250 m stage exceeds 4 h; drop scheme axis if T-026 verification fails. Do not change alpha, budgets, tau or reference after seeing test results.

## Known limitations

Scheme finding from T-026: vanleer is nonlinear (limiter), so cached linear source-response bases are invalid for it. Therefore vanleer is NOT a design-selection arm. It is used (a) in stage 1 field verification, and (b) as a less-diffusive TRUTH generator via direct forward solves for the 6 test cases (inverse-crime check: truth vanleer 500 m / 250 m, estimator exponential linear), cost = direct solves only. Vanleer sub-steps to Courant<=0.5, so at equal nominal dt its effective dt is smaller (scheme and time step are confounded; report effective dt). A design arm with vanleer would need direct-solve responses per layout (not planned).

## Notes

Cost (T-025 benchmark, upper bounds): 6-case ensemble at 250 m dt 50 ~30-60 min; replicates at 500 m ~3 min each; total ~4-5 h in resumable batches. Planned figures: convergence curves, displacement/regret vs resampling baseline, layout maps per mesh.
