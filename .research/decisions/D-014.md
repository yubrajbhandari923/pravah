# D-014 · DYN-04 / DYN-03 protocols registered before any of their test stages. DYN-04 (EXP-032): 70 candidate cells (ix 2..20 ste

## Decision

DYN-04 / DYN-03 protocols registered before any of their test stages. DYN-04 (EXP-032): 70 candidate cells (ix 2..20 step 2, iy 2..14 step 2); budgets 1,2,3,5; families random (10 draws, mean), spread (farthest point), highpop (separation >=3 cells), static_opt, dyn_opt, src_opt; optimised families are designed on the Gaussian linear-Bayes surrogate (prior sd = reference amplitude, i.e. prior_var 1 in x=theta/ref units) averaged over the 6 winds, separately for noise 0.02 and 0.10, exhaustive for k<=3, multi-start swap for k=5 (no optimality certificate at k=5). Layouts are written to layouts.json with a hash BEFORE any episode is simulated; the surrogate uses no episodes so there is no design/test leakage. Evaluation uses the real constrained estimator M2 with the static M0 prior; alpha,beta are tuned on validation seeds 6000-6047 per (budget,noise) on the SPREAD layout only and applied unchanged to every layout of that budget (frozen hash). Test seeds 7800-7895 (96 episodes). Primary: paired episode-bootstrap CI (Bonferroni 0.05/4 per budget-noise cell family; stricter 0.05/40 also reported) of sqrt(J_pop) for dyn_opt minus each baseline; a win needs CI excluding 0, >=10% relative gain and sign persistence leaving each wind out. Secondary: surrogate-vs-actual Spearman per budget; search-optimality gap; local/boundary source errors; the population-vs-source tradeoff. DYN-03 (EXP-031): estimators CT (current-bin data only), CAUSAL (data up to the end of bin b, keep bin b), RETRO (full-window M2), M1; hyperparameters tuned on validation; test seeds 7600-7695; primary contrasts CAUSAL-CT, RETRO-CAUSAL, M1-CAUSAL on sqrt(J_pop) with the same criterion; steady winds are the registered negative control (prediction: smaller history gain than on variable winds); detection delay/miss secondary. All truths are synthetic, wind known, no model error.

## Reason

Pre-registration before the design and test stages.
