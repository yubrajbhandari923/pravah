# D-006 · EXP-015's acceptance criterion is amended POST HOC (dated 2026-10-12, after results were seen) from the two-sided 'withi

## Decision

EXP-015's acceptance criterion is amended POST HOC (dated 2026-10-12, after results were seen) from the two-sided 'within the replicate-pair IQR' to the one-sided 'no larger than the replicate-pair distribution' that its own hypothesis states; and EXP-015 stage 2 is recorded as NOT having tested H1, because its baseline is not commensurable with its mesh comparison. 500 m / dt 100 is provisionally admissible for EXP-026 on the narrow grounds in F-035 - no evidence against, not shown adequate.

## Reason

WHY THE AMENDMENT. EXP-015's hypothesis says mesh-induced displacement and regret are 'no larger than' the resampling baseline, but its success sentence says they must 'lie within the replicate-pair interquartile range'. Those are different tests. All six measured cells failed the two-sided rule by being BELOW the baseline floor - i.e. for being more stable than sampling noise - so the pre-registered rule returns no usable verdict on H1 in either direction. The amendment is therefore necessary, but it was found AFTER seeing results and is recorded as post-hoc, not as a reinterpretation of what was always meant. It must not be presented as a pre-registered pass. WHY H1 IS UNTESTED ANYWAY. Independent review (F-035/R1, verdict needs_work) established that the comparison is non-falsifiable: convergence_stage2.py:244 and :258 build the design cases for every mesh from the same seed-101 draws, so the mesh comparison has zero case-draw variability, while :274 builds each replicate from a different draw, so the baseline has full case-draw variability. The baseline contains a variance component the mesh comparison does not, and a one-sided mesh<=baseline test cannot fail: a deliberately broken 2 km mesh would also pass. Confirmed in the data - all 48 vs_main regret rows are positive (median 0.95-2.51 x J_ref), an in-sample versus out-of-sample artefact, against mesh-induced |regret| of 0.068-0.115 x J_ref. WHAT WAS RETRACTED. The author's initial claims that the 500 m layout is 6-35 percent better on held-out test (a single-case artefact; leave-one-out -4.0 and -8.7 percent; Wilcoxon p 0.22-0.56 at n=6) and that the objective is very flat (mis-diagnosed: eps_sd/J_ref is 1.01-2.52, so the tie threshold exceeds the objective) are withdrawn. A new finding not previously flagged: mesh regret is NEGATIVE at -6.8 to -11.5 percent of J_ref, which falsifies docs/numerical-methodology.md section 5's non-negativity expectation and bounds greedy sub-optimality at >=7-12 percent at the reference mesh. WHAT IS REQUIRED NEXT. A pre-registered EXP-015 variant with a paired baseline: select layouts at 500 m AND 250 m on each of the 8 replicate draws, making the distributions commensurable (about 8 extra 250 m ensembles, roughly 31 min at 8 workers on this cluster). NOT DONE: no tolerance or criterion was loosened to make anything pass, and the finer mesh was not preferred merely because it looked better.
