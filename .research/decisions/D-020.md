# D-020 · DYN-06 v2 (supersedes the metric in D-019; predictions P1-P4 and all scales unchanged), registered before the run. Defec

## Decision

DYN-06 v2 (supersedes the metric in D-019; predictions P1-P4 and all scales unchanged), registered before the run. Defect found in RUN-0082 (F-042): the 'no change' rule used one single-observation sigma as a threshold on a group-mean contribution. Replacement: per operator and scenario, a detection threshold per group = 95th percentile of |group change-contribution| over 100 NULL replicates (the pre-change state x_pre continued, same estimator, noise seeds 9100-9199); the signal replicates use seeds 9200-9299. A replicate is labelled 'none' if no group exceeds its null threshold, else by the group with the largest |contribution|/threshold; 'ambiguous' if the top two ratios are within 10%. Reported separately: detection rate (1 - none) and misattribution among detected (named group != true group). 'Confusion' for P1-P3 is defined as misattribution among detected replicates (undetected replicates are reported, not counted as confusion); P4 compares misattribution AND detection of L4_ref vs L4 at equal d. Estimator, hyperparameters (frozen M1z alpha per layout/noise), scenarios, d targets {2,4,8,16}, 6 winds, layouts L4/L9/L4_ref and the two controls are as in D-019.

## Reason

Fix the invalid detection rule before re-running.
