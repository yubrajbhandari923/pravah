# D-013 · DYN-02 v2 protocol (supersedes D-011), registered before any v2 test run, in response to review F-037/R1. Solver: adapti

## Decision

DYN-02 v2 protocol (supersedes D-011), registered before any v2 test run, in response to review F-037/R1. Solver: adaptive-rho ADMM with explicit primal/dual residuals (raises NotConverged) for M2, exact bounded least squares for beta=0; non-converged candidates score +inf. Prior scale: fixed physical reference amplitudes (1e5 ug/s, 0.1 ug/m3), independent of layout. Estimators: M0 static; M1z independent bins with zero prior mean; M1 independent bins with the static M0 estimate as prior mean (the FAIR baseline); M2 = M1 + temporal TV with the same prior. Hyperparameter grids: alpha half-decade 1e-2..1e9 (M0, M1z, M1), decade 1e-2..1e9 x beta decade 1e-1..1e7 (M2); chosen on validation seeds 6000-6047 (48 episodes: 6 winds x 8 families); any selection on a grid edge is flagged in frozen_hparams.json and reported with the result. Truth: 6 winds, boundary events only on bases that are live in that wind/bin, 12 fine bins with edges jittered by +-35% of a bin, truth dt 60 s vs inverse 120 s, noise sd = fraction x S0 where S0 is the reference-history mean concentration of the wind (independent of the truth), independent noise draws per noise level. Metrics: sqrt(J_pop) primary; source errors only over identifiable (parameter,bin) columns; floor = population-weighted NNLS projection on the 6-bin basis (replaces the 'oracle floor'). Test: seeds 7200-7295 (96 episodes), frozen hash. H1 is supported for a (layout,noise) cell only if the paired episode-bootstrap CI of M2-M1 on sqrt(J_pop) at Bonferroni level 0.05/4 excludes 0 AND the mean relative improvement is >= 10% of M1's mean AND the sign persists leaving each wind out. Seeds 7000-7047 (RUN-0060) and 7100-7147 (RUN-0061) are consumed. Model error (wrong wind, K, missing source) is out of scope here and belongs to DYN-07.
