## Decision

DYN-06 v4 (supersedes D-022's scoring), registered before the run, after review F-044/R1. Changes: (1) misattribution is scored only in a (wind, cell) where detection is clearly above the false-alarm level (one-sided binomial test of the detected count against 0.10, p < 0.01); other cells are 'undetected' and reported as such; (2) the summary statistic is POOLED misattribution (sum of wrong labels / sum of detected replicates over the scored winds) alongside the number of winds scored and the mean detection, never an unweighted mean of per-wind rates; (3) cells whose look-alike has no observable change signature (|A cb|/sigma < 1) or an unphysical rescale (c* > 1e3) are flagged detectability_only and reported separately; scenarios whose amplitude matching failed (match_ok False) are invalid, not scored; (4) P4 is evaluated ONLY for S7 (the S1 pair, L4_ref vs L4_interior) as registered in D-019; P1-P3 are adjudicated on the gated pooled metric and on detection (P3 at d=2 is expected to be uninformative because detection is near the false-alarm level there; it is reported as 'not evaluable' where detection is not above null); (5) new noise seeds: null 9500-9599, signal 9600-9699; all other design (d targets, 6 winds, layouts L4/L9/L4_ref, noise 0.02/0.10, frozen ridge alpha, controls) as in D-022.
