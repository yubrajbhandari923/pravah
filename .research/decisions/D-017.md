# D-017 · DYN-05 / P0 (EXP-033) protocol, registered before running. Design objective: empirical-risk design with the closed-form

## Decision

DYN-05 / P0 (EXP-033) protocol, registered before running. Design objective: empirical-risk design with the closed-form noise expectation of the ridge estimator (prior x~N(0,I) in x=theta/REF units), which depends on the design episodes only through per-wind second-moment matrices; verified against Monte Carlo in tests/test_dynamic_sufficiency.py. Ensembles of N in {6,12,24,48,96,192} independent episodes (wind uniform over 6, family uniform over 8, generator of dynamic_study v2), R=16 independent ensembles per N from streams SeedSequence([5,N,r,e]) (not nested); reference objective from 4000 episodes SeedSequence([6,e]); budgets k=2 and 3 (exhaustive over the 70 DYN-04 candidate cells), noise 0.02 and 0.10. Metrics per (noise,k,N): relative selection regret J_ref(S_N)/J_ref(S*)-1 (mean, median, 90th percentile over the 16 ensembles), fraction of ensembles recovering the reference optimum exactly, mean Jaccard overlap and mean nearest-site displacement (cells) between replicates, number of distinct layouts, and the size of the near-optimal set (layouts within 5% of the reference optimum). Registered sufficiency criterion: N is sufficient for (noise,k) when the 90th percentile of relative regret is <= 5%. Exact location agreement is NOT required when alternative layouts are within the near-optimal set. Real-estimator check: every distinct chosen layout is evaluated with the actual nonnegative ridge estimator (M1z, alpha 0.5) on 96 fresh test episodes seeds 8400-8495 (test stage runs only after design.json is hash-frozen; no test episode influences any design). Q-013 link: the prior EXP-015 flaw (replicate spread of the objective comparable to the objective itself with 6 cases) is addressed by an explicit N sweep with a common reference and independent replicates. Caveat registered in advance: the design objective uses the unconstrained ridge proxy, and its agreement with the real estimator is itself a reported outcome.

## Reason

Pre-registration before the design stage.
