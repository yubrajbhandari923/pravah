## Findings

- **F-028** Toy wind-error response: error grows ~linearly with direction error (B: 0.010 -> 0.040 at 30 deg, known-wind 0.010), direction more damaging than speed per unit vector error, wind error dominates sensor count, chi2/dof detects it but identifiability flags do not, ensemble strategy helps only inside/near its prior (superseded)
- **F-029** Toy wind-error response (corrected after review): direction error raises nominal-wind error ~3-4x at 30 deg and exceeds the sensor-count effect for >=10 deg; chi2/dof detects it; ensemble helps only when the truth is near a prior member (preliminary)
- **F-033** Toy wind-error controls (EXP-023): F-029's direction-error response survives alpha, off-member prior, model error and base-wind changes; chi2 detection weakens modestly and coverage is much lower under model error (superseded)
- **F-034** Toy wind-error controls (reviewed): wind-error penalty and C's gain survive alpha and off-member priors, but C's gain shrinks with distance to the nearest prior member; chi2 detection power is NOT an inverse-crime artefact (specificity and coverage are); base-wind rotation controls are confounded (preliminary)
