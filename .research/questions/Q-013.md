# Q-013 · Q-F: Is the sensor-design objective identifiable at all with 6 design cases? EXP-015 stage 2 measured eps_sd/J_ref = 1.01-2.52 at noise 0.05, i.e. the replicate-to-replicate spread of the design objective is comparable to or larger than the objective itself. If the objective cannot be estimated from 6 cases, then which layout greedy selects at small budgets is arbitrary, and every small-budget conclusion in EXP-013, EXP-014, EXP-020, EXP-026 and EXP-015 is affected. How many design cases are needed, and does the answer change the published small-budget rankings?
