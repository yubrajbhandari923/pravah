* **Coarse-resolution caveat (binding):** ERA5 is ~28 km; Belauri is ~16 km across. A single uniform wind per time is all it can supply. It cannot represent
  valley/slope flow, nocturnal drainage flow, land-use roughness or sub-hourly turbulence; the 10 m wind is not a boundary-layer-mean transport wind;
  `docs/winds.md` already records that the stream-function conversion would silently drop the divergent part (zero for a uniform field). Belauri is a flat Terai site
  (relief 36.7 m, mean slope 0.21 deg), which makes a uniform wind less wrong than it would be in a valley, but this is a judgement and not a check.
  "Where scientifically appropriate": appropriate for the **large-scale direction/speed variability and persistence** (rotations, reversals, calms) that the toy
  studies imposed by hand; NOT appropriate as local wind truth. State in every finding: ERA5 supplies the episode wind *histories*; the transport field is spatially uniform.
* Conversion: `era5.to_segments(domain, times_s, u_t, v_t)` makes hold-last uniform segments (sample k holds [t_k, t_{k+1})). Hourly ERA5 over the 8 h run means 8
  segments; each segment builds its own FiPy objects (`docs/winds.md`) and the benchmarks used 1-2 segments: **per-solve cost with 8 segments is not measured.**
* Existing prescribed-wind studies (`src/pravah/winds.py`, `configs/prescribed_winds*.json`, `geo.scenarios.uniform_wind/eddy_wind/build_wind`) use meteorological
  bearings + speeds drawn per case (`configs/geodesign.json` `ensemble`: speed 0.8-2.5 m/s, 8 sectors +- 15 deg, a second segment every third case switching at 3600 s). No existing
  Belauri study uses ERA5 histories; ERA5 so far fed only `results/phase3-era5` descriptions and the `era5` ingestion tests.
* Courant: `docs/belauri-forward.md` uses u dt/dx <= 0.3 (dt 100 s at 500 m allows 1.5 m/s). ERA5 p95 = 2.76 m/s gives Courant 0.55 at 500 m/dt 100 (backward Euler is stable, accuracy
  lower; the 0.8-2.5 m/s geodesign winds already exceeded 0.3). Either cap speeds at the geodesign range (document the clipping), or use dt 50 (1.8x cost). Decide in the pilot.

## 2. Adaptations needed

### 2.1 What can be reused unchanged

* `pravah.dynamic`: `expand(scenario, n_bins)`, `truth_scenario(scenario, history, edges)`, `tv_nnls`, `fit_history`, `sensitivity`, `NotConverged`. `expand` only rewrites each source's/basis' `profile`
  (indicator per bin) via `dataclasses.replace`, so it works for `RasterSource` (it has `name` and `profile`) as well as `GaussianSource` and for `InflowBasis`. `truth_scenario` replaces profiles with piecewise
  profiles and runs with q = ones. Both are domain-agnostic (to be confirmed by a unit test on a small Belauri-like `RasterSource` scenario: a missing test, section 5).
* `pravah.dynamic_design`: `DesignProblem` (exhaustive/greedy/swap on a Gaussian linear-Bayes surrogate), `population_weights`. Candidate-set size is the only scaling issue:
  exhaustive search is C(n, k) (toy: 70 cells, k <= 3 = 54 740 subsets). Belauri n = 54 gives C(54,3) = 24 804 and C(54,5) = 3 162 510 (swap search above k = 3 as in DYN-04).
* `inverse.ResponseCache` / `compute_responses` (n_params + 1 solves per scenario, cached by hash of scenario+solver+version).

### 2.2 Mapping Belauri sources to temporal-bin parameters

The toy has 5 parameters (3 Gaussian local sources + 2 inflow bases), 6 inverse bins over 6 h. Belauri maps naturally to
**P = 5 parameters x B = 6 bins = 30 expanded parameters**, mirroring DYN-04 so the toy layout code and priors carry over:

1. `road` (RasterSource, ug/s), 2. `builtup` (RasterSource, ug/s), 3. `external` (Gaussian patch outside the polygon, ug/s; the "transboundary-like" emitter), 4. `bg_wsw` and 5. `bg_ene`
inflow bases (ug/m3) with the sides split by the wind-relevant halves (as in the toy `B_wsw`: W, S; `B_ene`: E, N). The geodesign config has ONE four-sided `bg`; the split is needed so that a single wind direction
does not make the basis dead; as in the toy, `live_mask` (sensitivity per (parameter, bin)) must decide which (basis, bin) pairs are identifiable before they are scored or used in a truth (rule from F-037 review).
The `facility` group is omitted (as in geodesign's `groups`). Alternatively P = 4 with one `bg` (matches geodesign exactly); the cost difference is 25 vs 31 solves per wind.

Unit and scale rules (AGENTS.md): emission amplitudes (ug/s) and inflow amplitudes (ug/m3) never compared by raw value; a fixed `REF` vector per kind (toy: 1e5, 1e5, 1e5, 0.1, 0.1) replaces any layout-dependent norm.
Belauri REF should be taken from `configs/geodesign.json` base amplitudes (road 1.5e6, builtup 1.0e6, external 5.0e5 ug/s; background range 0-0.05 ug/m3) and frozen before the design stage. The `external_profile` onset ramp (0 -> 1 over 7200 s) and
the `bg` ramp are replaced by `expand`'s indicator profiles (so the 2-hour spin-up ramp disappears: the first bin has a true switch-on transient; the first observations are excluded by `t_exclude` as in geodesign; document this change from the geodesign scenarios).

### 2.5 Noise scale

Toy: noise sigma = fraction (0.02, 0.10) x S0, where S0 is the mean concentration of the reference history (all parameters at REF) at that wind, independent of the truth and layout (`reference_scale`). Same rule at Belauri, computed per wind from its response set. Geodesign used fractions 0.05/0.15 of a validation-ensemble signal scale; do not reuse those values for different units. Keep both toy levels (0.02, 0.10) so the comparison is like for like and optionally add 0.05 for continuity with EXP-026 (not as a design choice after seeing test results).

### 2.6 Mesh and domain choice

500 m / dt 100 (provisional). 250 m as a **declared secondary** at pilot and for the model-error "truth at 250 m, inverse at 500 m" condition (section 3.5). Use the padded grid because it is the one with consistent 250/500/2000 m levels (EXP-027), at the price of regenerating the population arrays. Do not claim mesh adequacy from DYN-08 (F-048 void).

### 2.7 Choosing winds and synthetic episode families

* Episode = 8 h window (T = 28800 s as geodesign; the toy used 6 h) of the ERA5 hourly series at the centroid (bilinear `sample_at`), **start times on a fixed non-overlapping 8 h grid**: 186 candidate windows (93 per month). Windows are not independent (autocorrelation), so they are blocks, not the statistical unit.
* **Documented selection rule (to be frozen before any layout is designed):** compute per window mean speed, vector-mean direction, and direction change (angle between the first-third and last-third vector means). Strata: month (Jan/Jul) x {steady: change < 45 deg, turning: >= 45 deg} x speed {low < median, high >= median} = 8 strata. Split the record by calendar day, **not** by window, to prevent leakage: days 1-10 design, 11-20 validation, 21-31 test (both months). Within each split draw windows by a seeded rule, equal per stratum (design: 12 winds; validation: 12; test: 12; fewer if a stratum is empty, and report which). Speed clip or dt 50 per the Courant remark in 1.4. Report the actual direction/speed/strata table with the run.
* **Why many winds**: F-046 found a 6-episode design ensemble insufficient (p90 regret up to x29; by the real estimator insufficient in 3 of 4 cells) and F-048 showed 6-case design draws differ by 3-4 km. Design winds therefore number 12 (twice DYN-04) and the design objective averages the surrogate risk over all of them (`SumProblem`), plus the family prior is built into the surrogate (no per-episode sample). The sufficiency question is re-asked for Belauri at the end (a DYN-05-style check with N >= 96 real-estimator episodes) rather than assumed.
* **Families (8, from the toy `FAMILIES`):** constant, activation, shutdown, ramp, opposing, pulse, influx_end, compensating; histories are drawn on the 12 fine bins with jittered edges (truth never nested in the inverse bins). Belauri analogues: `activation/shutdown/ramp/opposing` act on road/builtup/external; the `pulse/influx_end/compensating` families need a live boundary window, computed from the wind's `live_mask`. If the ERA5 wind never makes `bg_*` live in a stratum, the episode is flagged invalid (as `make_history` does) and resampled by rule.

### 2.8 Statistical unit and seeds (lessons of F-046, F-048, F-049)

* Unit = independent synthetic episode (wind window x family x seed); wind is a blocking factor; every claim also leave-one-wind-out; paired episode bootstrap at the Bonferroni level of DYN-04 (0.05 / (|K| x |noise| x |baselines|)); registered effect criterion = CI excludes 0 AND >= 10 % relative change AND sign persists leaving each wind out (as F-050). With 12 test winds, also report a wind-cluster bootstrap (as F-054, whose interval "just reached zero" with 6 winds).
* **Seed conflicts in the current EXP-036 registration** (it says "disjoint from legacy 101, 202, 303, 304, 1101-1108", but that list is incomplete): the proposed ranges overlap seeds already spent in the toy program and EXP-026: toy tuning 6000-6047; toy tests 7200-7295 (consumed), 7300-7395 (F-054), 7600-7695 (F-053), 7800-7895 (F-050); DYN-07 8500-8595; EXP-026 fixture draws 6501-6506 and pilot 6511-6516, 6601-6603. The seeds feed different RNG streams in a different domain, so this is not a data leak, but "disjoint" is stated as a design rule and test seeds are supposed to be untouched. **Recommended amendment before any run:** design 5000-5999 (unused), validation 6100-6499, test 7900-7999, replicate draws 8000-8499 (verify with `research search` for each before freezing). Test seeds are opened only once, after hyperparameters (alpha, beta, noise-scale assumption) are frozen with a recorded hash (as D-021/D-023).
* Hyperparameters are tuned on the validation split **on the spread layout only** per budget and noise, then applied unchanged to every layout of that budget (the F-050 protocol), so no layout is advantaged by tuning.

## 3. Experimental design proposal

### 3.1 Hypotheses (H8 refined; registered before running)

* **H8 (EXP-036):** the toy-domain effects persist *qualitatively* on Belauri geography with WorldPop weights and synthetic emissions/episodes. Null: they do not.
* Operationally H8 is a conjunction over the four toy findings below. Each gets a pre-stated prediction, a criterion and a verdict category (**transfers / does not transfer / not resolved**). H8 is "supported" only if at least 3 of 4 transfer and none is reversed in sign; it is "not supported" if >= 2 do not transfer or any key one reverses with the registered criterion met; else "unresolved". These counts are a proposal for the human to approve at registration.

### 3.2 Registered comparisons (as DYN-04)

Layout families per budget k in {1, 2, 3, 5} (toy `KS`; Belauri 54 sites; add k = 8 only as a secondary, since exhaustive search above 3 is a swap search): `dyn_opt` vs each of `random` (10 draws), `spread`, `highpop`, `static_opt`, `src_opt`. ### 3.9 Go / no-go criteria

Pilot gate (must all hold before the full stage): (1) response-set linearity check passes (direct truth vs linear sum, relative difference < 1e-6 as in `Setup.build`); (2) `live_mask`/sensitivity: every (parameter, bin) used in a truth has measurable sensitivity at >= 1 candidate site; (3) per-solve cost with ERA5 8-segment winds <= 3x the benchmark (else cut W or the bin count); (4) every estimator fit converges (0 `NotConverged`); (5) J_pop floor (projection floor) is below the best layout's error by a margin (otherwise the objective cannot resolve layouts: the Q-013 failure mode); (6) the spread layout's tuned hyperparameters are not on a grid bound in more than 25 % of cells (F-054 had 9 of 16 flagged; extend the grid first); (7) the ERA5 strata table is non-degenerate (>= 3 valid winds per split).
Full-stage verdict: H8 categories as in 3.1. **Stop** (and report, do not rescue) if the uniform/zero-effect controls misbehave (e.g. a dyn_opt vs dyn_opt-with-shuffled-sites control shows a "significant" gain), if any run produces a non-converged fit counted in results, or if the 2 km-type falsification logic (a deliberately bad layout, e.g. all sensors in the least populated corner, must lose to spread) fails.

## 4. What is and is not validation

**DYN-08 is NOT empirical validation.** It is a synthetic transfer test of a methodology under the model's own physics: the "truth" is the same advection-diffusion-removal model (with perturbations in the model-error stage), emissions are invented, sensors read model fields plus Gaussian noise, and the estimator is told the noise level. Real geography and a modelled population estimate only decide where the sources and weights lie. WorldPop is a modelled 2025 constrained estimate, not a census; ERA5 is a reanalysis, uniform here, not a measurement at Belauri; "exposure" is residential head count weighting, not personal exposure; numerical diffusion dominates the nominal K (K_eff/K = 2.4-9.4); there is no terrain-coupled flow, no chemistry, no vertical structure; OSM may omit sources; the national border is not verified at this resolution. A result such as "dynamic design beats spread" means: in this synthetic world, with this estimator, it did; it says nothing yet about whether real sensors at those sites would estimate real emissions better.

Required for actual validation (all outside this program, none to be done with data in it):
1. **Measurements**: collocated, quality-assured time series of PM2.5 (or the species modelled) from reference-grade or calibrated low-cost monitors in and around Belauri, plus meteorology (local wind, mixing height/stability), covering the seasons modelled; deployment, calibration and uncertainty of the sensors themselves.
2. **Emission information independent of the fit**: an inventory (traffic counts, household fuel, brick kilns, agricultural burning) or tracer data, to test whether inferred histories are plausible; identification of true transboundary events (e.g. border-region episodes) from independent evidence.
3. **Model evaluation against data**: forward model skill (bias, RMSE, correlation by hour and season) before inversion; local meteorology with terrain/roughness effects; vertical structure; sensitivity to K, lambda, H.
4. **Blind, prospective inversion**: choose the layout from the model, deploy, withhold part of the network (by site and by time), compare reconstructions with held-out readings; compare against a non-model baseline (spread network, kriging); pre-register.
5. **Population exposure**: time-activity/indoor-outdoor data or an exposure model; evaluate sensitivity to the population product (WorldPop vs GHS-POP vs census) and to the 2025 epoch.
6. **Independent review** and, ideally, replication at another site (`docs/transfer.md`).
Before any of that, intermediate non-empirical steps would improve credibility (and are in scope for later phases): resolve the mesh/numerical-diffusion question (EXP-027b needs about 90-190 core-h and approval), Q-013, and review of F-050/F-053/F-054/F-049.

## 6. Go / no-go summary and top risks

**Recommendation: CONDITIONAL GO for a pilot (S0 + EXP-026 + DYN-08 pilot, about 5-8 core-h, local); NO-GO for the full stage until (i) the registration amendment (seeds, criteria, strata rule), (ii) a human decision on how to treat the void mesh question, and (iii) the pilot gates pass.** Data are sufficient (geography, WorldPop, ERA5 present and verified locally); compute is not a constraint (about 70-90 core-h with contingency, under 512). The registered gate "EXP-036 gated on EXP-027 (resolution decision)" cannot be met because EXP-027 is void: either the human relaxes it to "500 m provisional, 250 m cross-check" or waits for EXP-027b (90-190 core-h, approval needed).

Top 5 risks:
1. **Mesh/numerical-diffusion**: 500 m is only provisionally admissible and numerical diffusion dominates (K_eff/K = 2.4-9.4); layouts may reflect discretisation, and a Belauri "transfer" could be a numerical artefact. Mitigation: 250 m secondary, truth-at-250 m/inverse-at-500 m model-error condition, claims worded as synthetic-model results.
2. **Design/identifiability instability (F-046, Q-013, F-048)**: small design ensembles give unstable layouts (3-4 km differences) and the surrogate and the real estimator disagree about stability; the flatter WorldPop field (88 cells = 50 % of people) shrinks the population effect, so contrasts may be unresolved rather than negative. Mitigation: 12 design winds, 192 test episodes, sufficiency check, report "unresolved" as an outcome.
3. **ERA5 realism and cost unknowns**: uniform 28 km winds from a 2-month record cannot represent local flow; the sample (Jan and Jul 2023) is a narrow climatology; hourly wind segments multiply FiPy set-up cost (not measured); Courant above 0.3 for p95 speeds. Mitigation: use ERA5 only for histories, document, pilot-measure cost, speed clip or dt 50.
4. **Low identifiability of Belauri parameters**: road and builtup rasters overlap spatially and the bg bases may be dead for many winds; the transfer of M2/M1 and history findings (F-054, F-053) may fail for geometric rather than methodological reasons. Mitigation: `live_mask`/sensitivity check per (parameter, bin) before scoring; report which pairs were identifiable.
5. **Registration/bookkeeping errors**: EXP-036 seed ranges overlap spent seeds; EXP-026 carries a wrong study total and overlapping seeds; tuning on the wrong layout/noise or reuse of test seeds would void the claim; population and ERA5 files are untracked and not on the cluster (provenance and reproducibility risk). Mitigation: amend before running, hash frozen files, track or stage the small extracts, independent review of the final findings (none of F-050/F-053/F-054/F-049 is reviewed yet; they are preliminary).

Not checked: cluster-core speed, multi-segment solve cost, behaviour of `dynamic_study._fit`/`summarise` with non-toy globals, the exact content of seeds 7000-7199 in any other record.
