ERA5 handling (as documented in readiness 1.4/2.7): windows are non-overlapping 8 h blocks at 00/08/16 UTC at the Belauri centroid (bilinear), **186 candidate windows**; split by calendar day (days 1-10 design, 11-20 validation,
21-31 test, both months); stratum = month x {steady: direction change < 45 deg, turning} x {mean speed below / at-or-above the all-window median 1.439 m/s}; seeded round-robin over strata. Speeds are **clipped to 2.5 m/s**
(the geodesign range; Courant 0.5 at 500 m / dt 100; direction kept; clipped hours reported per split: design 3, validation 0, test 9 of 144 / 48 / 96 hours). Available strata per split:
design has 7 of 8 (no January turning/high window), validation 8, test 8. ERA5 supplies a spatially UNIFORM wind history per episode, nothing local.

Deviations from the toy pipeline (each is a decision for the human, section 8):
1. **Noise scale S0** = mean over observation times and the 54 candidate cells of the reference history (all parameters at REF, constant). The toy used the domain mean divided by the number of bins; at Belauri the 34 km padded domain is mostly zero, so that rule would make the noise tiny relative to sensor readings. Noise fractions 0.02 / 0.10 of S0 are therefore not numerically comparable with the toy's.
2. **REF** = (road 1.5e6, builtup 1.0e6, external 5e5 ug/s; each inflow basis 0.05 ug/m3) from `configs/geodesign.json`; histories are drawn in units of REF as in the toy.
3. **Observations**: every 1800 s with t > 7200 s (12 per sensor), as geodesign; the first bin's switch-on transient is excluded; the 2 h onset ramps of geodesign are replaced by `expand`'s bin indicators.
4. **Live mask** is evaluated at the candidate sites: a (parameter, bin) pair is live if its REF-amplitude response reaches 0.05 S0 (half the 10 % noise sd) at >= 1 candidate. Boundary events in a truth only occur in live windows; invalid episodes are flagged and excluded, as in the toy.
5. **highpop** layout: top population cells with pairwise separation >= 2250 m (1.5 x lattice spacing); **spread**: farthest-point from the candidate centroid; **lowpop** (control): the least populated sites with the same separation.
6. **CT / CAUSAL** history estimators are ported from `dynamic_history` but use **borrowed hyperparameters** (CT: M1's alpha; CAUSAL: M2's alpha, beta), evaluated on spread and dyn_opt only: exploratory.
7. Hyperparameter grids are reduced to alpha 1e-2..1e9 (decade steps) and beta {0, 1e-1..1e5}; tuned on the validation spread layout per (k, noise) and applied unchanged to all layouts (F-050 protocol).
8. Population: padded-grid WorldPop, study mask applied (counts x study fraction, fractional people kept): **study total 54 357.3 people (500 m padded)**; grid total 184 029.57.

## 4. Pre-registration text (proposed; to be copied into EXP-036 by the human)

Freeze this block, `configs/dyn08.json` and the code commit hash BEFORE any layout is designed. Statements of fact refer to the files above.

### 4.1 Question and hypotheses

**Q-024 / RQ-D9.** Do the conclusions of the toy dynamic population-weighted program (F-050, F-054, F-053, F-049) transfer qualitatively to Belauri geography with WorldPop weights, ERA5-derived wind histories and synthetic emissions, on a 500 m / dt 100 s mesh that is only provisionally admissible?

**H8 (conjunction of four arms; each gets a verdict transfers / does not transfer / not resolved).** Null: they do not transfer. Predictions (from readiness 3.3, restated for the reduced design; primary cells are k in {3, 5} x noise in {0.02, 0.10} = 4 cells, metric sqrt(J_pop) of the nonnegative M2 estimator):

* **H8a (design; toy F-050).** `dyn_opt` beats `random` in >= 3 of 4 primary cells and `spread` in >= 3 of 4 (the toy: 8/8 and 7/8), with the registered effect criterion (4.3). The margin against `highpop` is expected to be **smaller than in the toy** because WorldPop is flatter (88 study cells hold half the people at 500 m; top 10 % of populated cells hold 33.7 %), and the gap to `static_opt` is expected to be small at k = 3. Transfer = sign and criterion in the same direction in the majority of cells; a smaller magnitude still counts as transfer.
* **H8b (estimator; toy F-054).** M2 <= M1 (same sign) at both noise levels for `spread` and `dyn_opt`, interval possibly including zero at 12 winds (F-054's interval just reached 0 with 6), effect probably weaker because road and builtup rasters overlap much more than the three toy point sources. Transfer = same sign in both noise cells for >= 3 of 4 (k, noise) cells and the criterion met in >= 1 cell.
* **H8c (history; toy F-053; EXPLORATORY, borrowed hyperparameters).** CAUSAL < CT by >= 10 % in all cells on `spread` and `dyn_opt`. Note F-053 contradicted the registered "history helps most where the wind varies" prediction: no such claim is made here; the gain is expected to come from plume carry-over (transport time over 16 km is ~3 h at 1.5 m/s).
* **H8d (model error; toy F-049).** M2 error grows monotonically with wind rotation (rot5 < rot10 < rot20) in >= 3 of the 4 (layout-family in {spread, dyn_opt}) x noise cells at each k; M2's advantage over M1 erodes with rotation (within the interval by 10-20 deg); `dyn_opt` stays no worse than `spread` under perturbation, **or** the opposite is reported as an informative negative (designs tuned to one wind history are brittle). `mesh250` is reported as a numerical-error proxy only.

**H8 verdict rule (proposal, human to approve).** Supported if >= 3 of the 4 arms transfer and none reverses in sign with the criterion met; not supported if >= 2 do not transfer or a key arm reverses with the criterion met; otherwise unresolved. With H8c exploratory the human may prefer to count only H8a, H8b, H8d (then: >= 2 of 3 and none reversed).
Exploratory only: population weighting moves `dyn_opt` sites toward settlements (compare with an area-weighted dynamic design: not implemented in this reduced plan); reconcile `static_opt` with the EXP-026 `worldpop_real` layouts at the same k.

### 4.3 Comparisons, estimator, effect criterion

* Layouts per (k, noise): `dyn_opt` (surrogate, mean risk over the 12 design winds; exhaustive at k = 3 over C(54,3) = 24 804 subsets, multi-start swap at k = 5 with 6 extra starts) vs `random` (10 replicates, mean per episode), `spread`, `highpop`, `static_opt` (bins collapsed), `src_opt` (identifiable columns, A-optimal), plus the `lowpop` control.
* **Primary comparisons:** `dyn_opt` minus each of the 5 baselines on sqrt(J_pop) of M2, in the 4 primary cells = 20 contrasts, Bonferroni level 0.05 / 20 = 0.0025. Estimator = the real nonnegative M2 (BVLS/ADMM); `NotConverged` is an error counted and reported, never a result and never replaced.
* **Effect criterion (registered):** paired episode bootstrap CI (10 000 resamples) excludes 0, **and** |relative change| >= 10 % in the stated direction, **and** the sign persists leaving each test wind out. Also reported: wind-cluster bootstrap over the 12 test winds, fraction of episodes in which `dyn_opt` is better. Statistical unit = the independent synthetic episode (wind x family x seed); wind is a blocking factor.
* Secondary: M2 - M1 and CAUSAL - CT on `spread` and `dyn_opt` (level 0.05 / 4); model-error conditions as ratios against the matched rows; source-error metrics (identifiable columns only).
* Hyperparameters are tuned on the validation episodes (48) with the spread layout only, per (k, noise), then frozen with a hash; nothing is selected using test quantities.

### 4.4 Controls and gates

Pilot / go gates that must hold on the first cluster `time` + first stage output (from readiness 3.9): (1) response-set linearity < 1e-6 (**9.5e-16 measured**); (2) every (parameter, bin) used in a truth has measurable sensitivity at >= 1 candidate (live mask; 83 % of pairs live for the first design wind); (3) per-solve cost with 8 ERA5 segments <= 3x the benchmark (**1.1x measured**: 5.47 s vs the 4.84 s benchmark); (4) every fit converges in the test stage; (5) the projection floor is below the best layout's error (3-episode pilot: floor 3.0e-4 / 3.7e-3 / 4.0e-4 vs best layout 1.1e-3 / 1.1e-2 / 2.0e-3 ug/m3; untuned, 2 winds: **plausible, not established**); (6) not more than 25 % of the selected hyperparameters on a grid edge (coded gate in `--stage all`); (7) the strata table is non-degenerate (>= 3 valid winds per split: 12 / 6 / 12).

### 4.5 Stop rules (report, do not rescue)

Stop and report if: (a) the `lowpop` control does not lose to `spread` in every primary cell (`controls_all_passed` false); (b) the null control (random replicates 0-4 vs 5-9) meets the effect criterion in any cell; (c) any non-converged fit appears in a result; (d) more than 25 % of tuned hyperparameters sit on a grid edge (`--stage all` exits before opening the test seeds; extend the grid and re-register); (e) fewer than 3 valid episodes per cell; (f) a seed conflict; (g) the registered 500 m / dt 100 mesh is changed after seeing any test number. The original EXP-036 stop "gated on EXP-027 resolution decision" cannot be met (F-048 void): see decision D2.

### 4.6 What these results will not say

Not mesh adequacy (F-048 void; numerical diffusion K_eff/K = 2.4-9.4 persists); not empirical validity; not a statement about local wind (ERA5 is ~28 km, uniform here, 2 months of one year); not about real emissions, exposure (residential head count only), or any other site; the `mesh250` condition is a proxy, not a convergence test; population is a modelled 2025 estimate.
A result of the form "dynamic design beats spread" means: in this synthetic world, with this estimator and these frozen choices, it did.

## 6. What is and is not validation

DYN-08 is **not** empirical validation (see readiness section 4 for the full list). Truth = the same advection-diffusion-removal model (perturbed in the model-error stage); emissions invented; sensors read model fields plus Gaussian noise with the noise level told to the estimator.
Real geography, a modelled population and ERA5 histories only decide where sources and weights lie and how the wind turns. Real validation needs measurements, independent emission information, forward-model skill against data, blind prospective inversion, exposure data, independent review and replication (readiness section 4, items 1-6; none is in this plan).
