## Findings by experiment

### E08 stage-2 variants (map_full, excl1200)

**Design vs code.** `--estimator map_full` calls `estimate_series(..., window=None)`: lower index 0, upper `issue + 1`
(`cross_objective.py:174`), i.e. every reading from hour 0 up to and including the issue hour. Schedules only mask hours.
`--exclude-radius` drops candidates within 1200 m of a true source before every design (`make_designs`).

**Is k\* = 2 for every strategy a bug?** No. Evidence:
1. Independent k\* from the 64² rows (`check_tables.out`) reproduces the journal table exactly (reference 2/4/8 etc.;
   map_full 2/2/2 for all four strategies; excl1200 4/8/8, 4/8/16, 12/8/>32, 16/24/>32).
2. The estimator reads no future data: a direct per-issue-time MAP solve from readings at hours ≤ t equals
   `estimate_series` and poisoning every later reading changes nothing (`e08_rerun32.out`).
3. Mechanism: the sweep calendar is 1440 h; training is days 1–30 (720 h); the first test issue time is after day 42.
   map_full therefore fits K constant strengths to > 1000 h × k hourly readings under changing winds, versus 24 h for the
   reference. With the perfect transport model and time-invariant strengths, two sensors suffice to identify up to 8
   strengths. Even random k = 2 networks reach median nRMSE 0.05–0.09 (max over scenarios 0.14–0.21).
4. The 32² pilot (upwind, linear to 4e-5) shows the same floor (k\* 2/2/2 for dopt), so it is not a VanLeer
   artefact. There the first issue time already has 175 hourly readings per sensor; the map_full estimate of the
   re-run scenario is within 1–3 % of the true strengths from the first issue time on.

**Conditions.**
- The floor is a property of the benchmark's assumptions (constant strengths for 60 days, perfect transport). E10
  (time-varying sources), E12 (wrong wind) and E13 (drift, background) all remove most of the memory gain. Say this
  wherever "sufficiency becomes trivial" appears.
- At 64² the map_full errors are tiny (dopt k = 8 median RMSE 0.0034 vs background-only 1.35 µg/m³), while the VanLeer
  superposition error is up to 0.06–0.18 µg/m³ (max abs, per scenario). The rows do not record the true-strength
  (superposition-floor) RMSE. In the one 64² E17 scenario I re-solved that floor is ≈ 10× below the best estimate's
  error, so numerics are probably not driving the k ≥ 8 H5 reversal (0.003 vs 0.004), but this is not shown for E08.
  The k = 2–4 reversal (33/40 at k = 2, error 10× larger) is not in doubt.
  **Fix:** add a `true_strength` row (forecast RMSE with a_true) per scenario.
- excl1200 H5 at k = 32 is NaN because both designs are undefined in K = 8 seed 8 (and dopt alone in 5 more K = 8
  scenarios: "cannot place 32 sites with min_sep 1250 m"); `log(inf) − log(inf)` is NaN and `np.median` propagates it.
  Not a primary test; the journal's "not investigated" can now say why.

### E12 wind mismatch

**Isolation and conventions.** `perturb_winds` copies the true winds; dir rotates the FROM direction by +θ at constant
speed; lag uses the wind of h − L (the model is late); verified independently (`e12_e14_rerun32.out`). Designs come from
oracle training responses (plus `dopt_m` from the model wind); estimators read hours ≤ issue time. No leakage found.

**Claims vs tables** (k = 8, dopt/source_near, `check_tables.out`):
- H1 (vs persistence): dir30, lag2, lag6, ar30 0/6; dir10 3/3; spd 6/6 — correct.
- **H2 "the sensor fit barely moves (ratio 1.02–1.3) while source RMSE rises 5–16×" is wrong as a general statement.**
  Fit ratios: spd0.8 1.01–1.06, spd0.6 1.06–1.30, dir10 1.13–1.15, ar15 1.25–1.33; but dir30 1.70–2.00, ar30 1.60–1.74,
  lag2 2.25–2.60, lag6 3.61–4.34. Source-RMSE ratios span 1.6–16 (map24 1.6–5.4; map_full 2.2–16). Compensation with a
  near-perfect fit is real for speed and small direction errors only. (E14 independently found the whitened fit warns
  for the large errors.)
- H3: oracle memory gain M = 0.60–0.77 (random 0.53); under every wrong wind M ≤ 0.06 except spd0.8 dopt 0.21 — correct.
- **Persistence baseline.** `persistence = tf[issue + h] − tf[issue]` over every grid cell: it is the *true field's*
  persistence, which no sensor network can realise. "Worse than persistence" means worse than an oracle that knows the
  current field everywhere. It is post hoc (declared in §12.2). State this wherever it is used (E12–E14, E17).
- Journal hygiene: `state/experiments/e12.md` ends with a stray line "oracle fields)."

### E13 background, source-basis and sensor-error mismatch

**Silent failure — confirmed and structural.** In `model_mismatch.world`, `bgoff1` adds +1 to truth and readings,
`common1` adds +1 to readings only. The readings agree to 3.6e-15 µg/m³ (rounding; `e13_rerun32.out`), so every truth-free quantity
(fit 1.000, lag-1 −0.01, strength coverage 0.94–0.99) is identical between the two worlds. With the `_bg` column the
estimator returns bĝ = 1.004 (median) and the forecast adds it to every cell: forecast RMSE 1.000 vs persistence 0.206
→ skill −3.9. This is a genuine non-identifiability, not a bug. Note it is the forecast *field* that fails; the
strengths are fine.

**Post-hoc field-level rule (lead decision: fit ratio < 1.1, |lag-1| < 0.1, forecast worse than persistence).** Applied
at cell-median level it flags 65 cells; in **46** of them the oracle itself is worse than persistence (random networks,
k = 2, map24): the rule flags low-skill configurations, not mismatch. Requiring also D_forecast (vs oracle) > 1.5 leaves
29 cells (common1 12, missing1 8, wide800 6, drift1 2, drop0.5 1). **Fix:** add the oracle-relative condition before the
64² run; keep it labelled post hoc.

**Other claims.**
- "Background offset fixable": bgoff1 map_full_bg skill 0.94 = oracle 0.94, coverage 0.96 — correct.
- **"Source-basis errors … the residual warnings are weak (fit ratio 1.17–1.34, lag-1 0.17–0.35)" is wrong for shifts.**
  shift400: fit 1.30–2.17, lag-1 0.30–0.69; shift800: fit 1.62–3.48, lag-1 0.44–0.82 (source_near networks warn
  strongly). Weak warnings hold for wide800 (1.21–1.27, 0.22–0.31) and missing1 (1.12–1.17, 0.14–0.18).
- "No estimator beats persistence for shift/wide/missing at k = 8": true at the cluster median; missing1 has 1–2/6
  clusters with positive skill.
- AR(1): coverage 0.45–0.62, lag-1 0.67–0.79 — correct.

### E14 reliability diagnostics

**Truth-free property.** `block_diagnostics(A, z, t_b, window, Fr_next)` has no truth argument; it uses z at hours ≤ t_b
and the model responses at t_b + 1; σ is the declared protocol σ. Labels/calibration alone read truth. Thresholds:
Youden on blocks 0–1 of the *test segment* (the first 48 h), evaluation on blocks 2–4. This is held out in time but not
the calendar's validation split; the design note's "validation blocks" should say so.

**Within-config AUC (post hoc).** Pooled vs within-config, L1: sensor_skill 0.743 → 0.713, spread 0.706 → 0.674; NIS,
fit, lag-1 rise by 0.01–0.02. The post-hoc scope does not inflate the headline. But STATUS's "AUC ≤ 0.71 overall" holds
only within-config; pooled the best is 0.74. The cluster bootstrap with 6 clusters is optimistic; the cluster ranges
(0.61–0.85 for sensor_skill) are the honest spread.

**Omitted source — claim is wrong for informed networks.** By design (`check_tables.out`):

| omit1 | L1 rate | NIS | lag-1 | fit | AUC (omit1 + oracle) nis / lag-1 / fit / sensor_skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dopt | 0.60 | 4.29 | 0.41 | 1.93 | 0.85 / 0.86 / 0.84 / 0.87 |
| source_near | 0.57 | 3.73 | 0.42 | 1.92 | 0.88 / 0.86 / 0.86 / 0.89 |
| random | 0.68 | 1.18 | 0.04 | 1.07 | 0.62 / 0.63 / 0.62 / 0.70 |

The 30 unflagged misses in `e14-local-misses.csv` are all random networks. Also note the designs were built from all K
sources (`p0` from the oracle responses), so the informed networks sit near the source the estimator omits — a deployer
who did not know it would not. E13's `missing1` (designer also unaware) shows the weak case: fit 1.12–1.17. **Defensible:**
"an omitted source is flagged clearly (AUC 0.84–0.89) when the network samples its plume; networks that do not sample it
(random here; E13's missing source) fail silently."

**AR(1).** lag-1 is 0.63–0.65 under AR(1) noise vs −0.03 to −0.04 (oracle); NIS 0.89–0.95. So lag-1 detects the
misspecification, but as a *failure* warning its AUC is 0.58 because informed networks rarely fail under AR(1) (L1 rate
0.08–0.09). Say "lag-1 detects AR(1) noise" rather than "catches failures".

**Speed × 0.8.** Residual diagnostics AUC 0.44–0.57 (loso 0.44, drift 0.35); spread 0.84 (it tracks low-signal blocks).

### E15 robust placement

- **Test directions.** noSN6: FROM directions {45, 90, 135, 225, 270, 315}; train3: {90, 270, 315}; no block hour from 0°
  or 180°, including the veering blocks (`e15_e16_check32.out`). The diurnal-rotation test regime sweeps every
  direction and W (270°) is also a training direction, so "no *test-only* direction" is the precise wording.
- **Training-only information.** Designs use `design_input` (training hours), the declared blocks (solved from declared
  winds, not the calendar) and `p.F` (training Fisher information for `full`). E12 perturbed responses enter only the
  evaluation. No leakage.
- **CVaR** matches the Rockafellar–Uryasev minimisation to rounding over 300 random cases; the robust loss
  tr(QΣ(m2 + M)Σ)/L0 matches Monte Carlo of the MAP error.
- **Statistics.** The contrast table has 2,730 rows, 379 at 6/0 or 0/6 (two-sided p = 0.031). Among 648 tail
  contrasts of robust designs, **9** are 6/0 in favour and 17 are 0/6 against; under the null about 648/64 ≈ 10 per
  direction are expected at 6/0. The headline "only clear gain vs dopt at k = 2 under map24 (+0.09, 6/6)" is therefore
  not distinguishable from multiplicity. The journal already says "uncorrected"; it should say "consistent with chance".
- Claims "robust ≈ source_near under map24", "no gain under map_full", "criteria indistinguishable" match the tables.

### E17 regional case

**ERA5 handling (verified).**
- Time: `valid_time` is UTC and stays UTC; the model calendar is UTC hours. The diurnal description holds in local
  time: cool-season 09–12 UTC (14:45–17:45 NPT) vector-mean FROM 237° (SW), 18–21 UTC (night) 352° (N).
- u/v → model: (ux, uy) = (u10, v10), x east, y north; the solver's convention (ux = −s sin θ_from) matches. July box
  mean u10 = −1.05 m/s (westward flow = easterly wind), vector-mean FROM 99°. The journal's "67–112° for ≈ 67 % of hours" is
  the three 22.5° rose sectors centred on 67.5–112.5°, i.e. 56–124°: 0.66 (0.45 with strict 67–112° bounds).
- Box mean: my own NetCDF read (latitude is descending in the raw files), own bilinear weights on the equirectangular
  cell centres → max |diff| 1.7e-14 m/s vs `region.model_winds` over 3,624 hours.
- Courant (64², dt 240 s, dx 1,562.5 m): max per-axis 0.45 (box mean), 0.50 (point). The solver raises above 1, so
  the completed run is itself proof.

**Wrong-wind degradation (map_grow 15–27×): plausible and not an artefact, but mis-framed.**
- 32² pilot, coolA, k = 8 (`e17_check32.out`): forecast RMSE with the **true strengths** under the point wind is 0.0178
  vs map_grow dopt 0.0180; under dir30 0.0220 vs 0.0213. The wrong-wind error is the transport floor: no strength
  estimate, placement or memory can remove it. That is why strategies converge and the memory gain vanishes.
- 64² (coolA K2 s0 re-solved, `e17_floor64_K2_s0.csv`): true-strength forecast RMSE 0.0472 (point) and 0.0624 (dir30)
  vs map_grow 0.0465–0.0469 and 0.0599–0.0602 at k = 4–16, every strategy. Estimation even lands slightly *below* the
  true-strength floor (strengths distort to compensate, E12 H2). Under the oracle wind the true-strength RMSE is 0.0002,
  ≈ 10× below map_grow's 0.0017, so the oracle map_grow error is estimation, not VanLeer numerics.
- The ratio D = 15–27 divides this floor by the oracle map_grow error (≈ 0.002, itself near the numerical floor). A
  stable framing: wrong-wind forecasts are 1.8–3.7× persistence and 0.43–0.76× background-only (`check_tables.out`).
- The point wind's "16.5° and ×1.13" understate the difference: the median direction error is 8°, but 41 % of test
  hours have box speed < 1 m/s (mean error 29°), and the vector RMS difference is 0.45 m/s against a mean speed of
  1.12 m/s (40 %).

**Persistence** is computed exactly as in E12 (`tf[issue] − tf[issue + 1]` over every cell; same issue times). It is the
true-field persistence. The chapter's "repeating the last reading is hard to beat" is wrong: no reading is repeated.

**Claims vs 64² tables.**
- Placement vs random (20/20 at k = 8; 0.35–0.50 map24, 0.53–0.64 map_grow) — correct.
- "map24 forecasts only match persistence" — **overstated**: at k = 8, dopt/source_near are 1.21–1.44× persistence and
  beat it in 3–5 of 20 clusters.
- "every wrong-wind forecast is 2–2.5× worse than persistence" — range over k and strategies is 1.8–3.7×.
- Monsoon "source_near wins at k = 8 (16/20, p = 0.012)": nominal; among 18 dopt-vs-source_near tests (case × estimator
  × k) Holm needs p < 0.0028. Report as uncorrected.
- **Unit of analysis.** The 20 clusters of a case share one ERA5 wind history (and K = 2/4 of one seed may share
  source positions). The p-values are conditional on that wind realisation; the generalisation over wind histories rests
  on 3 cases (2 independent periods + 1 regime switch).
