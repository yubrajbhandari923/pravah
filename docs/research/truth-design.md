# PRAVAH truth, sensor, noise and seed design (E02 specification)

**Status:** DRAFT for human approval; implements `preregistered-protocol.md` (itself DRAFT) and never overrides it. It specifies; it reports no scientific result. Machine-readable form: `configs/benchmark-v1.yaml`, generated by run RUN-0007 of EXP-003 (command: a deterministic generator script run through `research run exec`; see Provenance). Scope: synthetic 2-D passive tracer, not PM2.5 and not real exposure.

Items marked **[decision]** are choices I made where the protocol was silent. They are judgement calls made without outcome data and need human confirmation or amendment before the E02 freeze.

## 1. Truth generator

- Forward model, units, boundary conditions: protocol §1–§3 and `pde-and-discretization.md` (`study` BCs). Truth is produced by the verified solver on the 64×64 grid, dt = 300 s, after a 48 h spin-up from c = c_bg.
- **Source basis.** phi_k is an isotropic Gaussian, sigma = 400 m, truncated at 3 sigma. Normalisation is *discrete*: sum(phi_i·dx·dy) = 1 after truncation and clipping at the walls, so every a_k is a total rate in ug s^-1 even for a source near a wall (no mass lost by clipping). Emission is q = a_k g_k(t) phi_k [ug m^-2 s^-1].
- **Mixing-height convention.** One fixed effective depth H = 500 m. The depth-averaged source is S = q/H [ug m^-3 s^-1]. Strengths a_k are independent of H, so an H change is a model-mismatch question, not a basis change.
- **Strengths** are strictly positive: a_k = a_ref · LogUniform(0.5, 5), a_ref = 100 ug s^-1.
- **Locations** come from the 12×12 source lattice (centres at ((a+0.5)L/12, (b+0.5)L/12)), greedy draw over a seeded permutation with minimum centre separation 1.5 km. K_s in {2, 4, 8}. **[decision]** Each (s, K_s) uses its own stream `SeedSequence([truth_seed, K_s])`, so K = 2 sources are *not* nested in K = 4 scenarios.
- **Background and inflow baseline.** c_bg = 5 ug m^-3, uniform, constant; the initial condition and every inflow face use it (Dirichlet). Unknown-background variant: b ~ N(5, 1²), estimated as an extra parameter.
- **Temporal factor.** Base g = 1; diurnal variant g = 1 + 0.5 sin(2π(t − t_k)/24 h), t_k ~ U(0, 24 h), secondary only.
- **Synthetic population weight** (secondary metric only): three-Gaussian mixture, seed 6000, parameters recorded in the YAML; normalised to sum 1 over cells.

### Perfect vs imperfect model truth

- **PERFECT** (primary): truth uses exactly the nominal model the estimator receives. All headline primary-endpoint numbers come from here.
- **IMPERFECT** (labelled separately, E12/E13 only, never the primary endpoint). Six single-factor variants, one at a time: wind direction +15°; wind speed ×1.2; truth lambda = 1/8 h; truth K = 100 m² s^-1; truth on a 128×128 grid (estimator keeps 64×64); truth source sigma = 300 m (estimator assumes 400 m). **[decision]** The magnitudes are judgement calls, not tuned. The estimator always receives the nominal values. Results from imperfect truth must carry the label in every table.

## 2. Wind protocols

Convention: direction is the meteorological "from" angle clockwise from north; ux = −s sin θ, uy = −s cos θ. Wind is spatially uniform (divergence-free), hourly piecewise-constant, speed clipped to [0.2, 5] m s^-1. **[decision]** Regimes run in 48 h blocks (needed so the "24 h stagnation then front" regime fits), assigned `regime_list[block mod 3]`.

| Regime | Segment | Definition (within a block) |
|---|---|---|
| steady_westerly | train/val | 3 m s^-1 from 270° |
| veering | train/val | 3 m s^-1; 270° → 315° linearly over hours 0–12, then 315° |
| weak_variable | train/val | speed U(0.5, 1.5) per 6 h; direction 270° with a 180° flip every 6 h |
| steady_southerly | **test** | 4 m s^-1 from 180° |
| stagnation_then_front | **test** | 0.2–0.5 m s^-1 from 270° for 24 h, then 3 m s^-1 from 0° (90° shift) |
| diurnal_rotation | **test** | 2 m s^-1; direction phi + 360° t/24 h, phi ~ U(0°, 360°) |

Per block: speed × U(0.8, 1.2), direction + N(0, 10°). **[decision]** The speed after the front, the diurnal speed and the perturbation sizes are mine. Block boundaries create wind jumps at 48 h; the solver's face-reclassification on wind reversal was *not* tested by T-008 (see gate-01), so weak_variable is exercised by this benchmark before its numerical behaviour is verified. That is a risk, not a pass.

## 3. Splits and leakage controls

- Calendar (days after spin-up): train 1–30, validation 31–42, test 43–60 (protocol §7).
- Winds: train and validation use the three training regimes with **different realization seeds**; test uses only the three held-out regimes. Wind seed = 5000 + 100·segment + s (segment 0/1/2).
- Test truth, test winds and test observations are never used for placement, tuning or model selection; `check_no_leak` in `src/pravah/experiment_config.py` is the guard, and it raises on violation.
- Sensor networks are placed from train-period information only.
- **Limit, stated plainly:** source scenarios are independent across replicates s but are *shared across the three segments within one s* (sources persist for 60 days and a_k is the quantity being estimated). Train and test therefore do not differ in sources; they differ in calendar and, critically, in wind regime. The benchmark tests generalisation across winds, not across source scenarios. If the researcher wants tuning on separate scenario seeds from evaluation, that is a protocol amendment (open question O-1 below).

## 4. Candidate sensor lattice, exclusion mask and budgets

- 16×16 sites, one per 4×4 block of the 64×64 grid at cell index 2 + 4i, so spacing 625 m, 256 sites in total.
- **Static exclusion** (fixed before any scenario): the outer ring (i or j in {0, 15}), leaving 196 sites. Reason: sites next to the Dirichlet inflow face mostly read the prescribed background.
- **Scenario exclusion** **[decision]**: sites within 400 m (1 sigma) of a true source centre are excluded. This uses source locations, which the protocol declares known to the estimator and designer, and never the strengths or any concentration. Worst case over all 60 (s, K) scenarios in RUN-0007: 187 admissible sites.
- Minimum inter-sensor separation 1.25 km; budgets k in {2, 4, 8, 16, 32}. RUN-0007 found zero random networks short of k across all 3,000 draws (20 seeds × 3 K × 5 k × 10 networks), so k = 32 is placeable on every scenario under the separation rule for random greedy draws. This does *not* show the same for optimised designs.
- Random baseline: 10 networks per scenario seed, `SeedSequence([placement_seed, K, k, r])`. The SHA-256 of all 3,000 networks is in the YAML and acts as a regression check.

## 5. Noise

- Observation = truth at the site + noise; the noise array is drawn **once** per (s, K) with shape (256 sites, 1440 h) from `SeedSequence(noise_seed)`, white from child 0 and AR(1) (rho = 0.8, same marginal sigma) from child 1. Every design indexes the same array, so two placements sharing a site see identical noise there. This is the mechanism behind the EXP-003 hypothesis that fixed noise draws isolate placement effects.
- Sigma = 0.05 × median train-period truth concentration over the 256 candidate sites, per scenario. Noise is independent of truth.
- Bias/drift: not in P1 (E14).

## 6. Seed manifest

Replicate s = 0..19: truth 1000+s, noise 2000+s, placement 3000+s, bootstrap 4000; added here **[decision]**: wind 5000 + 100·segment + s, population 6000, model-mismatch 7000+s (reserved). Streams are separated by at least 20, which `Seeds.validate` enforces for the protocol streams. All 20 rows and the 60 scenario draws (source cells, strengths) are written out in `configs/benchmark-v1.yaml`; the generator is numpy PCG64. The stream is not formally guaranteed stable across numpy versions, so the YAML records the numpy version (2.5.4) and the network hash.

## 7. Observability test cases (predictions, not results)

| Case | Setup | Predicted outcome (to be tested in E03) |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | K = 4, k = 2, snapshot | rank ≤ 2 < K, structurally non-identifiable |
| O2 | K = 2, k = 4, 24 h, westerly, all sensors west of all sources | numerical rank 0 at tol 1e-8 |
| O3 | K = 2, k = 2, two sources 1.5 km apart, sensors on the perpendicular bisector | ill-conditioned; condition number reported, no recovery claim |
| O4 | K = 2, k = 32, 24 h | full rank; noiseless recovery within 1e-6 relative |

O2 depends on diffusion being negligible upwind; with K = 50 m² s^-1 a small upwind signal exists, so rank 0 may fail at a looser tolerance. If it fails, report it; do not change the tolerance.

## 8. Failure cases and what was not checked

- Nothing here ran the forward solver; no truth fields were generated. The only computation was the manifest generator (RUN-0007: 60 scenarios, 196 static sites, worst-case 187 admissible, 0 short networks).
- Not checked: that the truncated Gaussian with clipping integrates to 1 on the grid (to be a frozen E02 test); that noise sigma is nonzero for every scenario; that O1–O4 behave as predicted.
- Known solver limits from gate-01 apply: protocol convergence gate (≥ 0.9 on 32/64/128) differs from what was run; dt-halving unmeasured; no wind-reversal test. Upwind numerical diffusion at 4–5 m s^-1 is large on 64×64, so truth from this solver is a defined numerical truth, not an exact PDE solution.
- The generator script lives outside the repository (the task scope allowed only the two output files), so the YAML cannot be regenerated from the repository alone. Hand this to a later task, which should commit it with a frozen test.
