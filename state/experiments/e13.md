# E13: Background, source-basis and sensor-error mismatch (plan 3)

**Design:** `docs/research/model-mismatch-design.md`, committed alone in 8c356f9. Code: `src/pravah/model_mismatch.py`.
- The inverse always uses the nominal model; only the world changes.
- 18 pre-declared mismatches: background offset and drift; source shift, footprint width, a missing source and diurnal
  emission; sensor AR(1), bias, drift, gain and outages; plus a combined case.
- Each estimator also runs with an optional unknown background term (`_bg`).

## 2026-11-14: 32² pilot (exploratory; 6 clusters; k = 8 shown; upwind)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e13-local-*`, `figures/e13-local-uncertainty.png`. Merged in cf28ee9.
- **A known background is fragile.**
  - A +1 µg/m³ offset (4σ) gives skill vs persistence −3.5, strength coverage 0.00 and source bias +128 %.
  - Estimating the background repairs it (skill +0.94, coverage 0.96, 6/6).
  - It only partly repairs a drifting background.
- **A background offset and a common-mode sensor bias look the same at the sensors.**
  - The background term fixes the offset but makes the bias worse (−3.9).
  - With the background term, the common-mode bias is a **silent failure**: fit ratio 1.00, white innovations, coverage
    0.96, yet forecasts are far worse than persistence.
- **Source-basis errors (shift, wide footprint, missing source) are not repaired by any estimator.**
  - No estimator beats persistence for any shift, wide or missing case at k = 8.
  - The residual warnings are weak (fit ratio 1.17–1.34, lag-1 0.17–0.35).
- **Sensor errors:**
  - AR(1) noise leaves the strengths unbiased but drops coverage to 0.45–0.60. Lag-1 warns (0.78).
  - Outages at random, even 50 % missing, are benign.
  - Per-site bias destroys coverage.
  - Under drift, using all past data helps (+0.57 vs map24, 6/6).
- **Diurnal truth:** E10's gain does not reappear for static estimators. The fit ratio of 2.34 is a clear warning.
- **Limits:** n = 6; synthetic stress magnitudes; the full suite was not rerun after the last small fix (the affected
  files pass).
