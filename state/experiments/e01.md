## Current finding

Upwind/backward-Euler FiPy solver meets the convergence rule on 16²/32²/64² for the diffusion and both translating-Gaussian oracles (finest-pair orders 1.47, 0.97, 0.97; floor 0.8). Mass budget matches the discrete-exact recurrence to ~2e-14. No negative overshoot. Numerical diffusion grows strongly with Courant/Péclet (rel. L2 0.025→0.27 for U 1→8 m/s at 32²). Oracle (ii) regime was revised after a first regime failed (human-approved, disclosed in the doc).
