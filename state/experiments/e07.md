# E07: Cross-objective placement ablation

**Question:** Which placement objective is best for which held-out task (forecast, analysis field, source, population-weighted)?
**Design:** `docs/research/cross-objective-design.md`, committed in 3e7d4d2 before any E07 computation. It covers the
three gate-06 conditions:
- each design declares what it knows;
- the POD archive comes from a prior ensemble of strengths, not the truth;
- scoring is on held-out test winds, with white, AR(1) and per-site noise arms;
- the POD mode-count rule is fixed in advance, and a swap or exhaustive check is run where sensor spacing binds.

**Confirmatory test (64² sweep):** `dopt` vs random, primary endpoint = held-out one-step forecast RMSE, ≥ 10 % for k ≥ 8.
`source_near` is the locations-only control.

## 2026-11-12: 32² local pilot (exploratory; upwind, dt 300 s; 18/18 scenarios)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e07-local-*.csv`, figure `e07-local-crossobjective.png`, run record in `reports/runs/runs.jsonl`.
Code: commit e3f7de0 on `experiment/e07-cross-objective`, integrated to main in ad4a642.
- Optimised designs beat random clearly, by a median 0.6–0.9 forecast-RMSE reduction, except at K=4, k=2.
  Uniform layouts are worse than random.
- **The locations-only control `source_near` beats `dopt`** in 44 of 54 (scenario, k) pairs, by 27–48 % at k ≥ 4.
- **The endpoints agree with each other:** the network best on forecast is also best on field, source and
  population-weighted error, with median cross-endpoint regret of 0. The plan's hypothesis of a criterion mismatch
  between endpoints is not supported at pilot scale.
- **Training-wind design criteria mispredict the held-out outcome.** Choosing the network by training-wind D-opt
  gives a median forecast regret of 0.51; choosing by D computed on the test winds gives 0.006.
  Reading (inference): designs tuned to the training winds do not transfer, while sensors at the sources stay
  informative whatever the wind. This is carried to plan-3 E15 as a hypothesis.
- **Robustness:** the AR(1) and per-site noise arms give the same ordering; NNLS is within about 0.05 of MAP.
- **POD-QR (`podqr`)** is undefined in 36 of 162 cases because only 2–7 leading modes are above noise. These cases are
  scored as failures.
- **Swap check where spacing binds:** greedy is exact in 29 of 36 blocks; greedy plus swap is exact in 34 of 36.
- **Limits:** n = 9 per cell; a shortened 4/1/6-day calendar; upwind numerics. All exploratory.

## 2026-11-13: 64² sweep (vanleer dt 30 s, job 204894, code dd2c51c, 12 h on 64 CPUs)
Scope (deviation 6): veering and mixed training winds, K 2 and 4, k 4/8/16, 10 seeds; 40/40 scenarios.
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e07-sweep-*`, `figures/e07-sweep-crossobjective.png`.
Independent audit: `docs/research/gates/gate-07-cross-objective.md` (pass with conditions; numbers reproduced, no leakage).
- **Confirmatory (dopt vs random, held-out forecast RMSE):** median cut 42–84 % per cell.
  - The CI is above 10 % in 8/8 cells (percentile bootstrap); 7/8 with distribution-free CIs (veering K2 k16 straddles,
    because dopt loses to random in 2 seeds); 8/8 with a mean bootstrap.
  - Verdict: **supported in the cells run.** K=8 and k=32 were not run.
- **Exploratory: the gain comes from knowing source locations, not from D-optimisation.** source_near beats dopt in 115/120
  pairs (110 with AR(1) noise, 118 with per-site σ), in all 20 source layouts, by a median of ≈ 0.4.
- **Mechanism (gate-07 field checks, 2 scenarios):**
  - dopt networks lose per-window identifiability (24 h window rank < K) only in held-out steady-southerly blocks, a
    direction absent from training. Those windows carry 37–97 % of dopt's squared forecast error.
  - source_near has no deficient windows.
  - Sweep-wide, dopt has ≥ 1 deficient test window in 30/40 scenarios at k=8, against 7/40 for source_near.
  - Whole-period information criteria miss this.
  - The evidence rests on one unseen wind direction.
- **Endpoint agreement** is close to built in (same static estimate, perfect model), so it is not a general finding.
- **VanLeer superposition error** (up to 0.73 σ at a single cell-hour) does not change any RMSE by more than 1e-4 or
  change the ordering.
- **Possible artefact:** source_near's sites sit inside the 1200 m truncated source footprint. With sites within 1200 m
  excluded, source_near still won, but this was checked in 2 scenarios only.

## 2026-11-13: gate-07 follow-ups (post hoc, exploratory; 40/40 scenarios; no new solves; code 96c5341)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e07-followup-*`, `figures/e07-followup.png`, run record e07-followup-local-96c5341.
- **Exclusion control** (all sites within 1200 m of any source removed; 152–188 candidates remain):
  - source_near vs dopt: median 0.375 [0.31, 0.45], 104/120 pairs, 19/20 layouts. Not an in-footprint artefact.
    The exception is K=4, k=4, which straddles 0 (and reverses under AR(1) noise).
  - **dopt vs random falls from 0.71 to 0.20 [0.15, 0.33]** (87/120). The confirmatory-style table on these rows is
    unresolved (0/8 cells). So much of dopt's margin came from sites near or inside the emission discs.
  - The confirmatory verdict stands for the protocol's candidate set only.
- **Windows by held-out regime** (k=8, practical rank < K):
  - steady southerly: dopt 0.37 (28/40 scenarios) vs source_near 0.01 vs random 0.63;
  - the other two regimes are ≤ 0.017 for dopt.
  - 79 % of dopt's squared forecast error (pooled; median 55 %) falls in deficient windows, against 6 % for source_near.
  - Exact rank is full at k ≥ 8, so this is a signal-to-noise loss.
  - Gate-07's mechanism generalises. It still rests on one unseen direction, and 12/40 scenarios show no southerly loss.
- **Implication for E08/E11/E15:** score placements with in-footprint sites excluded as well, and against unseen wind
  directions.
