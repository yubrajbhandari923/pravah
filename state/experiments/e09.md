# E09: Causal sequential filtering

**Design:** `docs/research/filter-design.md`, committed alone in 30d4c25. Code: `src/pravah/filter.py` (KF predict/update,
causal run with multi-horizon forecasts, linear hour operator for upwind only, CRPS and coverage); tests in
`tests/test_filter.py`. The filter refuses VanLeer because the scheme is non-linear.

## 2026-11-13: 32² local replay (exploratory; E07 pilot world, upwind; 12 scenarios; held-out test winds)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e09-local-*`, `figures/e09-local-traces.png`.
- **Augmented filter (`kf_aug`, state = [field; source strengths]):**
  - With a perfect model it is algebraically the batch MAP over all past data, matching to 4e-13. Gate-09: its gain comes
    from **memory** (all past data: a median 0.99 of the log-RMSE gain), not from the prior or from sequential filtering.
  - It beats E07's 24 h-window MAP on 1 h forecasts by a median 0.58–0.74 (CIs 0.50–0.85), winning 10–12 of 12 scenarios
    per design, and by 0.77–0.91 at 24 h.
- **Field-only filter:** almost useless. It only improves the 1 h forecast over open loop and is 1.6–13× worse than map24.
  Reading (inference): corrections are carried out of the domain within hours, so skill comes from estimating the
  persistent source forcing.
- **The E07 reversal depends on the estimator window.** source_near beats dopt in 10/12 scenarios under map24, but dopt
  beats source_near in 9/12 at k = 8 under kf_aug (unlimited memory). This fits gate-07's per-window rank-loss mechanism.
  It is pilot scale; check it at 64² before rewording E07.
- **Calibration:**
  - kf_aug is calibrated under white noise (coverage 0.50/0.82/0.97, NIS/k = 1.00).
  - Under AR(1) noise it is overconfident (95 % coverage 0.48).
  - kf_aug_ar1 restores calibration (0.945) and lowers RMSE, beating map24 in 12/12 scenarios.
- **64²:** the exact KF takes about 4–5 h per run, and the filter cannot use VanLeer (no linear operator).
  - Recommendation: a growing-window augmented MAP on the K cached unit responses (no process noise).
  - An EnKF with the real VanLeer step only where process noise or model error is needed.

## Gate-09 (pass with conditions; `docs/research/gates/gate-09-filter.md`, commit 3dbdc50)
- The code is correct and causal. All E09 rows and all 1440 white-arm E07 map24 rows were reproduced exactly.
- **Memory, not the prior:** using all past data gives a +0.58 to +0.74 cut in forecast error vs map24; the prior alone
  gives −0.05 to +0.15. At 64², memory gives 0.85–0.94.
- **The reversal holds at 64²:** with all past data, dopt beats source_near in 103/120 pairs and 19/20 layouts (see e07.md).
- **Calibration at 64² with all past data:** 95 % coverage 0.95, NEES/K ≈ 1.0. map24 is mildly overconfident (NEES/K
  median 1.27).
- NIS misses autocorrelated noise; check lag-1 innovation autocorrelation instead.
- P3 (the kf_field prediction) was falsified: its forecast distribution is mis-shaped, not overconfident.
- The moment-matched prior is a "known population prior", not leakage.
- **Baseline for E10/E11 (lead decision):**
  - Primary: the growing-window MAP on cached unit responses (≡ kf_aug) with E07's N(0, a_ref²) prior.
  - Short-memory reference: map24.
  - Placement orderings are reported under both, and memory length (or the source random-walk variance) is a declared
    E10/E11 factor.
