# E08: Sensor sufficiency, field complexity and measurement cost

**Question:** How do sensor count k, number of sources K, noise level, sampling cadence and duration interact? Where are
the breakpoints k*(τ) (smallest k from which held-out forecast nRMSE stays ≤ τ)?

## 2026-11-13: 32² local pilot (exploratory; upwind; 4 seeds per K; 12/12 scenarios)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e08-local-*`, `figures/e08-local-sufficiency.png`. Code eae033b; outputs 72c75e5.
- **Median k*(0.2)** (1× noise, 24 h hourly), for K = 2 / 4 / 8:
  - source_near 2 / 3 / 8;
  - dopt 2 / 4 / 8;
  - random 24 / 12 / 16;
  - coverage 12 / 8 / 8.

  Location-aware designs need about k ≈ K.
- **Span and sensor count matter more than reading density.** At equal total readings, 4k sensors × 6 readings beat
  k sensors × 24 readings in 11/12 scenarios.
- **H2 reversed** in the pilot: source_near is the more noise-sensitive in relative terms, but far better in absolute
  terms. The test is kept unchanged.
- **H3** is inconclusive. **H5** (source_near beats dopt): 10/12 scenarios at k = 8.
- The CIs are (min, max) of 4 scenarios, so all of this is indicative only.
