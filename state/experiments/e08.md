# E08: Sensor sufficiency, field complexity and measurement cost

**Question:** How do sensor count k, number of sources K, noise level, sampling cadence and duration interact? Where are
the breakpoints k*(τ) (smallest k from which held-out forecast nRMSE stays ≤ τ)?
**Design:** `docs/research/sufficiency-design.md`, committed alone in a846753 before any E08 computation. It has
hypotheses H1–H5 from E05 and E07, one Holm family at 64², and lead decisions 6–9 in §13.
- The §10 protocol cells (K = 8; k = 32) stay **secondary**; E07's confirmatory population is unchanged.
- Added an exploratory 1200 m exclusion rerun of stage 2 after the sweep.

## 2026-11-13: 32² local pilot (exploratory; upwind; 4 seeds per K; 12/12 scenarios)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e08-local-*`, `figures/e08-local-sufficiency.png`. Code eae033b; outputs 72c75e5.
- **Median k*(0.2)** (1× noise, 24 h hourly), for K = 2 / 4 / 8:
  - source_near 2 / 3 / 8;
  - dopt 2 / 4 / 8;
  - random 24 / 12 / 16;
  - coverage 12 / 8 / 8.

  Location-aware designs need about k ≈ K.
- **Span and sensor count matter more than reading density.** At equal total readings, 4k sensors × 6 readings beat
  k sensors × 24 readings in 11/12 scenarios.
- **H2 reversed** in the pilot: source_near is the more noise-sensitive in relative terms, but far better in absolute
  terms. The test is kept unchanged.
- **H3** is inconclusive. **H5** (source_near beats dopt): 10/12 scenarios at k = 8.
- The CIs are (min, max) of 4 scenarios, so all of this is indicative only.

## 2026-11-14: 64² sweep done (job 205039, code 37aceb7, 18.8 h, exit 0; 60 scenarios = 20 seeds × K ∈ {2, 4, 8})
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e08-sweep-*`, `figures/e08-sweep-sufficiency.png` (report run locally from the copied
rows CSV). The raw CSVs and 12 GB of fields stay on the cluster in `$PRAVAH_HPC_ROOT/results/e08-sweep-cd7afd9`.
The meta file's `git_rev` is empty because the snapshot is a `git archive`; the rev is in the run record.
- **Preregistered tests (one Holm family, windowed MAP estimator):**
  - **H1 supported.** dopt k*(0.2) is larger at K = 8 than at K = 2 (19 wins, 0 losses, 1 tie).
  - **H2 contradicted.** 0/60 at k = 8. Going from 1× to 5× noise raises source_near's relative error more than
    random's, though source_near stays far better in absolute terms. This matches the pilot.
  - **H3 supported.** dopt's 24 → 168 h span gain is larger in the southerly regime (60/60). Secondary prediction not
    met: source_near's span gain is not small (median log gain 0.40 at k = 8).
  - **H4 supported.** At W = 168, 4k sensors × 6 readings beat k sensors × 24 readings (58/60, random).
  - **H5 supported** on the 40 fresh scenarios: source_near beats dopt at k = 8 (39/40).
  - **Erratum:** the design text says the H5 fresh set has 60 scenarios. It actually has 40 (20 + 10 + 10), which is
    what the code uses.
- **Median k*(0.2)** at the reference setting (1× noise, 24 h hourly):

  | K | source_near | dopt | coverage | random |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2 | 2 | 2 | 8 | 8 |
  | 4 | 4 | 4 | 8 | 16 |
  | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 16 |

  Location-aware designs need k ≈ K. Location-blind designs need 8–16 sensors whatever K is.
- **More sensors beat more readings.** For random networks the saving is large at every budget: total readings beat
  reading density.
- **For location-aware designs the matched-budget result flips at large k.** At k = 8 → 32, 4k × 6 readings loses to
  k × 24 readings: source_near 0/60, dopt 5/60 at W = 168. Once the sources are covered, extra readings beat extra
  sensors.
- **§10 protocol cells (secondary, not added to E07's confirmatory set).** dopt cuts error against random by ≥ 10 % in
  8/9 cells (K = 2, 4, 8 × k = 8, 16, 32; CI lower bound > 0.1). The exception is K = 4, k = 32, which straddles the
  threshold (median 0.24).
- **Caveat (gate-09).** These comparisons hold for windowed estimators only. The all-past-data and 1200 m exclusion
  reruns on the cached fields are being implemented (stage 2 only, after E11's job).
