# E10: Joint source-strength and field sequential inference

**Design:** `docs/research/joint-assimilation-design.md`, committed alone in 131baa2.
- Augmented filter with a source random walk of variance q. This is the declared memory factor: q = 0 ≡ all past data.
- Truth variants: const, diurnal (protocol §4) and logrw (exploratory).
- `nonneg_mode`: the truncated-Gaussian posterior mode.

## 2026-11-13: 32² local pilot (exploratory; 12 scenarios; upwind)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e10-local-*`, `figures/e10-local-traces.png`.
- **H1, constant truth:** q = 0 is best, and error grows monotonically with q. Data-driven q selection picks q = 0 in
  157/216 columns.
- **H2, time-varying truth:** a random-walk filter (q > 0) beats both MAPs for informative designs.
  - diurnal truth: dopt +0.44 and source_near +0.57 vs map_full (12/12 each);
  - logrw truth: dopt +0.58 and source_near +0.59 vs map_full.
  - Validation-selected q recovers most of the gain.
  - The filter lags the diurnal cycle by several hours, so source RMSE under diurnal truth does not improve.
- **H3, calibration:** map_full is badly overconfident under time-varying truth (95 % coverage 0.09–0.25). The filter
  with matched q is calibrated (0.91–0.98).
- **H4, placement × memory:** E09's dependence of the ordering on memory is confirmed under constant truth. Under
  time-varying truth source_near is equal or better at every q. dopt beats random under every estimator.
- **H5, nonnegativity:** negative source means occur in up to 46 % of rows (weak designs). The constraint never
  increased error.
- **Not done:** EnKF, DAPPER, AR(1) noise, background confounding. The constraint is applied at output only.
