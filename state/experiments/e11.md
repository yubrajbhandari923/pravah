# E11: Placement optimised for causal forecasting

**Design:** `docs/research/forecast-design-placement.md`, committed alone in 09f31ad.
- **Objective:** data-free expected multi-horizon forecast variance tr(Q_{t+h} Σ_t(S)).
- **Two memories:** a 24 h window (fc_w24) and a growing window (fc_grow). Each also has a wind-robust version averaged
  over 8 compass directions (_rob).
- **Scoring:** both estimators (map24, map_grow), on held-out winds at h = 1/6/24.

## 2026-11-13: 32² local pilot (exploratory; 12 scenarios; upwind)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e11-local-*`, `figures/e11-local-forecast-design.png`. Code 95c72b6.
- **No single design wins under both estimator memories.**
  - Under map24, **fc_w24_rob beats dopt** (k = 8: +0.37 [0.07, 0.58], 12/0), mostly in the unseen southerly blocks, and
    is non-inferior to source_near.
  - Under map_grow, fc_w24_rob is worse than dopt (−0.13), and fc_grow matches dopt.
- **The non-robust forecast objectives mostly reproduce dopt** (same sites in 50–64 % of cases).
- **H2–H5 hold:**
  - the wind-robust prior helps the 24 h estimator;
  - a design matched to one memory loses under the other;
  - orderings are stable across horizons;
  - every design beats random by 0.72–0.90.
- **Predicted vs realised RMSE:** about 1.0 at the median, but the 90th percentile is 2.7 under map24 (poorly informed
  networks).
- **Caveat:** the robust prior includes the southerly, the direction that motivated it, so this is not generic
  robustness.
