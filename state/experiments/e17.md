## Wind rose (box mean)
- **Cool season:** calm and unsteady. Mean speed is 1.0–1.5 m/s, 27–50 % of hours are below 1 m/s, and no sector holds
  more than ~12 % of hours. The net drift is from the WNW–NW, with a diurnal cycle: SW in the afternoon, N at night.
- **July:** steady easterlies, 67–112° for about 67 % of hours.
- **Spatial spread:** within-box wind sd is 0.4–0.6 m/s, comparable to the mean. The solver takes uniform winds, so
  the box mean is the forcing; spatially varying wind is future work.
- **Mixing depth:** the cool-season median BLH is 47–88 m, far below the model's 500 m mixing depth. Noted only.

## 2026-11-14: 32² pilot (merged 09bcff8; n = 6 clusters per cell; direction only)
Lengths are scaled ×10 (100 km domain); K = 500 m²/s. Cases are date blocks: coolA, coolB, and monsoon (November train,
July test).
- **dopt and source_near beat random in every cell** (6/6).
- **dopt vs source_near:**
  - map24: source_near wins in the monsoon case (dopt 0–1 of 6), matching E07's unseen-regime mechanism; mixed in
    the cool season.
  - map_grow: about a tie. Gate-09's clear dopt win is not reproduced at this n.
- **Persistence is strong in calm winds.** It beats every map24 forecast; only map_grow with dopt or source_near beats it.
- **Wrong model wind** (the ERA5 point nearest Belauri, or a 30° veer):
  - every forecast becomes worse than persistence;
  - the ranking survives, but the advantage over random shrinks sharply;
  - longer memory is more fragile, as in E12.
- **Tests:** 39 passed (test_region, test_benchmarks, test_wind_mismatch). Superposition error ≤ 4e-15.

## 2026-11-14: 64² run done (job 205400, 2 h 01 min, exit 0; 20 clusters per case; exploratory)
Evidence: `tables/e17-sweep-{summary,wrongwind}.csv`, `figures/e17-sweep-results.png`. Rows stay on the cluster and in
the local scratch copy. After the run, the report was changed to also write the sweep summaries and figure; the numbers
are unchanged.
- **Placement still matters with the true (box-mean ERA5) wind.**
  - dopt and source_near beat random in every case and estimator (20/20 at k = 8): by 0.35–0.50 under map24 and
    0.53–0.64 under map_grow.
  - Coverage is barely better than random.
- **dopt vs source_near:**
  - map24: tied in the cool season. In the monsoon (unseen-regime) case source_near wins at k = 8 (16/20, p = 0.012),
    by a small margin (median −0.06). This is E07's direction, much weaker.
  - map_grow: tied everywhere. Gate-09's clear dopt win does **not** appear under calm, rotating ERA5 winds.
- **Persistence is the bar** (1 h RMSE ≈ 0.017–0.019).
  - map24 forecasts only match it (dopt/source_near 0.018–0.024 at k = 8; random 0.034).
  - map_grow beats it about 10× (0.0014–0.0020).
- **The wrong wind dominates everything.**
  - With the ERA5 point nearest Belauri (16.5° and ×1.13 off the box mean) or a 30° veer, every forecast is 2–2.5×
    worse than persistence (0.032–0.052).
  - All four strategies become nearly equal, and the memory advantage disappears: map_grow is degraded 15–27×.
  - This is E12's result reproduced under realistic winds. Even modest wind representativeness error at the scale
    of one site removes the value of both placement and memory.

## Gate-12-17 audit (2026-11-14; `docs/research/gates/gate-12-17.md`, merged c602e1c)
