# E12: Wind mismatch (plan 3)

**Design:** `docs/research/wind-mismatch-design.md`, committed alone in 2171df0.
- Truth uses the true wind; the inverse and forecast use a perturbed wind:
  - direction bias +10° and +30°;
  - speed × 0.8 and × 0.6;
  - lag of 2 h and 6 h;
  - AR(1) direction noise with sd 15° and 30°.
- The oracle branch uses the true wind.
- Code: `src/pravah/uncertainty.py`. Tests show truth is bit-identical and the inverse never sees the true wind outside
  the oracle.

## 2026-11-13: 32² pilot (exploratory; 6 clusters; upwind; mixed training wind; k = 2/4/8)
Evidence: `reports/quarto/tables/e12-local-*`, `figures/e12-local-bias.png`. Commit c915bf0.
- **H1, fast degradation:**
  - Direction ≥ 30°, lag ≥ 2 h and AR 30° make the 1 h forecast worse than persistence (0/6 clusters).
  - Direction +10° is about level with persistence.
  - Speed underestimation keeps forecast skill: × 0.8 and × 0.6 beat persistence, 6/6.
- **H2, source–transport compensation:**
  - The sensor fit barely moves (ratio 1.02–1.3) while source RMSE rises 5–16×.
  - Speed bias pushes strengths low (−15 % / −32 %).
  - 95 % coverage of the strengths collapses to 0.00–0.54.
  - Warning sign: lag-1 innovation autocorrelation rises to 0.13–0.89, but stays weak for speed × 0.8.
  - 298 "deceptive fit" cells.
- **H3:** the large gain from using all past data (+0.6 to 0.77 with the true wind) mostly vanishes under any wind error.
- **H4 not supported in absolute terms:** dopt and source_near are within a few per cent under the wrong wind.
  Robustness rankings based on ratios flip with memory, but that is a denominator artefact.
- **Limits:** n = 6 clusters, upwind numerics, a short calendar, one noise arm. The persistence baseline is post hoc.
