Status: done at smoke scale. Independent review docs/research/gates/gate-06-methods.md: passes as a methods/reproduction step; three conditions before E07 reuses it.

## Current finding

Smoke scale (32², 8 scenarios: steady/veering × seeds 0,1 × K = 2,4; k = 2/4/8; a_ref = 1 g/s; preregistered σ ≈ 0.25; G over 96 training hours).
- Greedy vs exhaustive on ≤25 candidates (spacing never binds there): own-objective optimum in 69/87 cases; D-opt 24/24.
  Where spacing binds (gate-06, synthetic 5×5 lattice) greedy D-opt is exact in only 35/80 (max rel. gap 19.8 %); the real lattice binds.
- Practical rank at preregistered σ, K = 4, k = 4: source-informed designs (source_near, dopt, aopt) 4/4, diversity 3.5,
  uniform/random/coverage 2.2; K = 4, k = 2: dopt 3.5 vs baselines 0.5–1.8. In-sample, and mostly the value of knowing source
  locations (locations-only source_near ties dopt in 21/24). On held-out hours (one spot case) A-opt and diversity drop 4 → 3.
- Objectives choose genuinely different networks (k = 4: D 17.9 dopt vs 12.3 diversity; coherence 0.85 vs 0.38). No winner claim.
- QR pivots match scipy and PySensors 0.4.3 (dev dependency).
- POD/QR is undefined in 46/48 cases with a noise-aware mode count: the smoke training calendar (4 days of piecewise-steady wind,
  constant sources) gives only 0–2 POD modes above noise. The noise-aware rule was set after a diagnostic (documented as a revision);
  `podqr_num` is an exploratory noise-unaware variant. Field-reconstruction score is uninformative here.

## Open questions

All source-informed objectives use true source locations; POD uses a noise-free training archive. Greedy guarantees do not hold
with binding spacing. Independent noise across hours assumed (AR(1) would overstate information).
