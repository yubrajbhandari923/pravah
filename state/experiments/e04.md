# E04 Footprints, Fisher information, identifiability — research journal

Status: run at smoke scale, one replicate; partly cleared.

## Current finding

4 known sources, 4-sensor designs, 2 winds, 32², 24 h: at the preregistered σ the practical rank (singular values with s·a_ref/σ ≥ 1) is 0 of 4 for every design and wind, although floating-point rank is 3–4. At an exploratory, non-preregistered rescaled noise it is 2–4. Moving the source vector along the weakest singular mode changes the data by <1σ in 10 of 12 cases. Fisher/CRB machinery is correct in the linear-Gaussian interior case and conservative under NNLS constraints.
