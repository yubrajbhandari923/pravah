# E15: Robust (scenario-distribution) placement (plan 3)

**Design:** `docs/research/robust-placement-design.md` (see the branch commit 3a28fe9), committed alone before any
computation.
- Code: `src/pravah/experiments/robust_design.py` and `scripts/e15_robust_placement.py`.
- **Per-scenario loss:** expected squared field-forecast error, normalised by the no-sensor loss.
- **Criteria:** mean, CVaR (α = 0.8, declared) and worst case.
- **Two ensembles, neither containing a test direction:**
  - noSN6: 44 scenarios;
  - train3: 20 scenarios, drawn from the training regimes.
- **Memories:** w24 and full.

## 2026-11-14: 32² pilot (merged 1d6203a; code e217a02; 6 clusters; exploratory, uncorrected)
Evidence: `tables/e15-local-*`, `figures/e15-pareto-local.png`, `figures/e15-sites.png`.
- **Tail (CVaR, worst case and max regret over 18 held-out wind-error × regime conditions):**
  - The only clear gain is against dopt at k = 2 under map24: rob_cvar_w24_noSN6 +0.09, rob_worst +0.10, 6/6,
    p = 0.031 uncorrected.
  - At k = 4 and 8 the gain is +0.02 to +0.04 (unresolved).
  - Under map_full it is about ±0.02.
  - There is no meaningful tail gain over source_near.
- **Nominal:** under map24 the w24 robust designs behave like source_near (+0.25 to +0.37 vs dopt), so robustness costs
  nothing nominal there.
- **Memory tradeoff again (E11, gate-09):**
  - w24 robust designs lose badly under map_full at k = 2 (cvar −0.46, worst −1.1, 0/6).
  - full robust designs score like dopt.
- **H3 not supported:** the criteria (mean, CVaR, worst) and the two ensembles are indistinguishable.
- **Why tail gains are small:** wind errors degrade every design about equally (E12's H4), which compresses differences.
- **Pilot finding (lead):** robust ≈ source_near under map24; no gain under map_full. Nothing universally wins.
